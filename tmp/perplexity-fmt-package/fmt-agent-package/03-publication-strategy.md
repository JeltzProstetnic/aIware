# Publication Strategy

## Core insight

The 64-page integrative paper has been desk-rejected four times. **Do not
submit it again as a single piece.** The paper is internally three
publishable units that land independently with much higher acceptance
probability:

| Unit | Content | Target venue tier | Rationale |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Unit 1: Criticality** | The 2015 Wolfram-derived criticality prerequisite + 2024–2026 empirical confirmation (Algom & Shriki, Hengen & Shew, Bhatt, Li). 15–25 pages. | *Entropy* (MDPI) Special Issue | Highest ROI. Unit 1 *is* what the criticality-consciousness research program has been building toward. Editors there want this. |
| **Unit 2: Predictions** | The four novel testable predictions (psychedelics → anosognosia, sensory control of ego dissolution, etc.) framed as a registered-report or predictions paper. 10–15 pages. | *Neuroscience of Consciousness* (registered-report track) | Bypasses desk-reject because the format is explicitly designed for novel-prediction work. |
| **Unit 3: Integrative FMT** | The full Four-Model Theory as the unifying framework. 40–50 pages. | *Frontiers in Psychology* / *Journal of Consciousness Studies* / *Philosophy and the Mind Sciences* | Lands easily once Units 1–2 are in print, because the author is no longer "independent researcher with big theory" but "criticality-prediction author with unifying framework." |

## Why this sequencing

Each unit, in isolation, looks like a normal, narrowly-scoped contribution
that editors are not afraid of. Together, after publication, they form
the integrative case Matthias originally tried to make in one piece. The
sequence converts the rhetorical liability ("ambitious solo theory from
independent researcher") into the rhetorical asset ("track record of
peer-reviewed criticality work, now unified").

## Tier-1 target: *Entropy* (MDPI)

See `04-entropy-submission-kit.md` for the full submission package. Key
points:

- Open access, APC ~2400 CHF, IF 2.0, Q2 Physics-Multidisciplinary.
- **Reputational baggage:** some academics dismiss MDPI as pay-to-publish.
  Net assessment: published-anywhere beats desk-rejected by an enormous
  margin. The reputational cost is real but bounded.
- **Editorial dynamics favor acceptance** when submitting into an open
  Special Issue (Guest Editors are incentivized to fill the issue;
  general-journal editors are incentivized to reject).
- Reviewer pool overlaps directly with FMT's citation graph: Tagliazucchi,
  Chialvo, Beggs, Carhart-Harris-adjacent reviewers, criticality program.
  They will recognize the work rather than pattern-match it to crank.

## Tier-2 alternatives (for Unit 1 if *Entropy* fails)

| Venue | Pros | Cons |
|---|---|---|
| *Frontiers in Psychology — Consciousness Research* | Open access, friendly to independents, accepts large theoretical pieces | APC similar to *Entropy*, similar reputational positioning |
| *Journal of Consciousness Studies* | Natural home for self-modeling tradition; Metzinger/Dennett-adjacent audience | Less prestige, smaller readership; editorial cycle slow |
| *Philosophy and the Mind Sciences* | Open access, peer-reviewed, takes integrative theory; growing reputation | Smaller audience than *Entropy* |
| *Neural Computation* | Substrate-criticality pieces land here | Strict mathematical formalism expectations |
| *Cognitive Neurodynamics* | Criticality + consciousness friendly | Smaller readership |

## Tier-3: do-not-submit list (failed or hostile)

  via Kanai-DM channel if engagement opens (Unit 2 registered-report
  track only).
- *Consciousness and Cognition* — adjacent to the four desk rejects.
  Treat as exhausted unless evidence to the contrary.
- *Nature Neuroscience*, *Neuron*, *PNAS* — unrealistic for an independent
  researcher's first peer-reviewed consciousness paper. Revisit only after
  Units 1 and 2 are in print.

## Preprint posture

**Post Unit 1 to PhilSci-Archive AND PsyArXiv simultaneously with journal
submission.** This:
- Provides a stable, citable URL for Twitter/X exchanges.
- Establishes priority on the criticality-prediction track record.
- Is standard practice and does not interfere with peer review at
  *Entropy* or any tier-2 venue.

The full 64-page integrative paper can stay as preprint-only for now.
Post it to PhilSci-Archive once Unit 1 is under review, with a note
linking forward to Unit 1 as the primary submission.

## Timeline (suggested, not prescriptive)

- **Week 1–2:** Spin out Unit 1 from the integrative paper. New abstract,
  new title, recompose bibliography for narrow scope. Preprint.
- **Week 3:** Submit Unit 1 to a current open *Entropy* Special Issue.
- **Week 4–6:** Spin out Unit 2 as a registered report. Hold for Unit 1
  reviewer feedback or submit in parallel depending on bandwidth.
- **Concurrent:** Twitter/X engagement (see `02-twitter-playbook.md`)
  and book promotion track (see `06-book-promotion.md`).
