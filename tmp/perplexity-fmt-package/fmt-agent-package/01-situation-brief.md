# Situation Brief

## Current state of FMT dissemination (as of 2026-05-01)

### What exists
- **Paper:** Four-Model Theory of Consciousness v4, 64 pages, single author,
  ORCID 0009-0005-9697-1665, refined through adversarial-challenge process
  in 2026. German precursor: *Die Emergenz des Bewusstseins* (Gruber 2015).
- **Book:** *The Simulation You Call I*, self-published on Amazon KDP.
  English-language manuscript derived from the same theoretical core.
- **Track record asset:** Five 2015-derived claims independently confirmed
  by 2024–2026 empirical research (criticality convergence, sleep-dependent
  criticality restoration, sleep onset as bifurcation, split-brain
  holographic degradation, anesthetic-criticality convergence).
- **Four novel untested predictions** that no competing theory generates,
  most notably: psychedelics should alleviate anosognosia; ego dissolution
  content is controllable via sensory input.

### What has failed (do not retry)
- **Four desk rejects** at *Neuroscience of Consciousness* and similar
  consciousness-specialist journals.
- **Direct cold-email outreach** to establishment figures has been
  exhausted with no result. **Metzinger specifically is out** — Matthias's
  read is that he's self-absorbed and won't engage. Do not propose Metzinger
  outreach as a tactic.
- Submitting the full 64-page integrative paper as the first move at any
  venue. Triple-failure mode: independent researcher signal, length signal,
  "dissolves Hard Problem" framing signal.

### Why the desk rejects happened (working hypothesis)
1. **Field risk-aversion post-2025.** The IIT-pseudoscience letter (April
   2025) and equivocal COGITATE results (2025) made consciousness-journal
   editors hyper-cautious about big-theory submissions. Anything that
   pattern-matches "dissolves the Hard Problem" gets desk-rejected on
   signal alone, regardless of content.
2. **Author + length + scope triage.** Editors triage on metadata before
   reading. Independent researcher + 64 pages + integrative theory =
   triaged out before the abstract is read.
3. **Framing mismatch.** "Addresses all eight requirements" and "dissolves
   the Hard Problem" trigger crank-detection heuristics in editors who
   are currently allergic to ambitious framing.

The content is not the problem. The packaging and venue choice are.

### What is working / available as leverage
- **Twitter/X engagement.** Bach (@Plinz) and Kanai (@kanair) are actively
  threading on simulation-and-consciousness arguments compatible with
  FMT's two-level ontology. Kanai is an editor at *Neuroscience of
  Consciousness* — direct reach via Twitter is a legitimate channel.
- **The criticality prediction track record** is unusually strong and
  independently verifiable. Algom & Shriki 2026 (ConCrit), Hengen & Shew
  2025, Bhatt et al. 2024, Li et al. 2025, and Pinto et al. 2017 all
  confirm 2015-published claims. This is the rhetorical anchor.
- **Claude Code agent system** for operating social and drafting workflows.
