# Framing Rules (apply to ALL FMT communication)

These rules apply to: paper abstracts, tweet drafts, book promo copy,
emails to editors, DMs to researchers, podcast pitches, and any other
outward-facing FMT material the agents produce.

## Lead-with-track-record rule

The single most powerful rhetorical asset is **five 2015-derived claims
independently confirmed by 2024–2026 empirical work**. This converts the
"independent researcher" signal from a negative (crank flag) to a positive
(prescient outsider). Lead with it whenever the audience is academic or
quasi-academic.

**Strong opening patterns:**
- "In 2015 I derived [X]; in 2024–2026 [Y, Z, W] independently confirmed it."
- "A theoretical prediction made in 2015, confirmed by Algom & Shriki 2026."
- "The criticality–consciousness convergence has a theoretical
  derivation that predates its empirical synthesis by a decade."

**Weak opening patterns (avoid):**
- "I have developed a theory that..."
- "My new framework dissolves..."
- "The Four-Model Theory addresses all eight..."
- "I solved consciousness when I was 25..."

## Allergy-trigger words and phrases

These pattern-match to crank submissions for journal editors and to
self-promotion for Twitter audiences. **Eliminate or strongly reduce.**

| Avoid | Prefer |
|---|---|
| "dissolves the Hard Problem" | "reframes the Hard Problem as a level confusion" |
| "addresses all eight requirements" | "contributes to [specific requirement]" |
| "unified theory" | "integrative framework" or just name the contribution |
| "I have shown that..." | "the derivation suggests..." or "the framework predicts..." |
| "groundbreaking" | (delete) |
| "revolutionary" | (delete) |
| "true nature of consciousness" | (delete) |
| "deciphered consciousness" | (never use externally) |
| "self-published book" | "monograph" or just title it |

## Length and density rules

- **Tweets:** ≤2 in a thread for a substantive intervention. Single
  tweet preferred when possible. No abstract-as-thread.
- **Abstracts:** ≤250 words; structured per `04-entropy-submission-kit.md`.
- **Cover letters:** ≤350 words.
- **Cold emails:** ≤200 words. Specific ask in last sentence.
- **DMs:** ≤120 words. One link maximum.

## "We" vs "I"

Single-author paper, so "I" is permitted but "the framework," "the
derivation," "the model" are stronger. Reduce first-person where the
content allows.

## Citation hygiene

Always cite primary sources by name+year in body text, not "[1]" style,
when communicating outside the paper itself (tweets, emails, blog posts).
Five anchors to memorize:

- Algom & Shriki 2026 — ConCrit, 140-dataset criticality consolidation
- Hengen & Shew 2025 — anesthetic-criticality convergence
- Bhatt et al. 2024 — sleep-dependent criticality restoration
- Li et al. 2025 — sleep onset as bifurcation
- Pinto et al. 2017 — split-brain holographic degradation

These are the five-confirmed-claims roster. They should appear together
or in subsets whenever the track record is being invoked.

## Voice and register

Matthias's voice: technical, direct, dry, concrete, allergic to fluff,
dark humor permitted in the right register. Agents should produce copy
that:
- Uses concrete nouns and active verbs.
- Names specific papers, predictions, mechanisms, not abstractions.
- Treats the reader as competent (peer-level, not student-level).
- Permits a single sharp sentence per piece for emphasis. Not more.
- Never apologizes for being independent or for having a long manuscript.

**Voice examples:**

✅ Good (technical, direct):
> "Wolfram's Class-4 derivation predicted the criticality requirement
> in 2015. Algom & Shriki's 140-dataset synthesis confirmed it in 2026.
> The two derivations are independent."

❌ Bad (breathless, salesy):
> "Imagine a unified theory that ties together psychedelics, dreams,
> split-brain phenomena and the very nature of consciousness itself."

✅ Good (sharp, single emphasis sentence):
> "Self-referential closure is precisely the condition under which
> the gap between process and feeling does not exist."

❌ Bad (academic mush):
> "It is hoped that this contribution may, in some small way, advance
> the broader discussion within the consciousness studies community."

## What NOT to mention externally

These are real but should not appear in any outward-facing copy:
- IQ, Asperger's, or any cognitive self-assessment.
- "Deciphered consciousness at 25."
- Personal biography (commando, mountaineer, Wing Chun, pilot) unless
  podcast-context-specific. Never in academic copy.
- The 2015 German book's reception story.
- Frustration with prior desk rejects.
- Any identifying information about Ivoclar or day-job context.
