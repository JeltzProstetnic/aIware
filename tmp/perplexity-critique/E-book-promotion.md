## Executive Verdict

Perplexity's core recommendations (podcasts, Substack, Twitter) are sound and 2026-evidence-based. Its guidance on what doesn't work (Amazon ads, Goodreads, cold blurbs) is accurate. **But** the strategy:
- Confuses effort with ROI potential
- Lacks realistic sales projections (the €27.52 Lulu first-month royalty is the truth-anchor)
- Omits the critical gating factor: **podcast and Substack acceptance rates depend heavily on peer-review status**
- Ignores the German edition entirely

**Realistic 6-month sales under full execution:** 100-200 copies (€150-300 gross) IF a peer-reviewed paper lands. Without one, 50-75 copies.

## Market reality checks (2026)

**Podcast acceptance rates:**
- Top-tier (Mindscape, ToE, Closer to Truth): **5-15%** for unknowns with preprints
- Mid-tier (Mind Chat, MLST): **20-40%**
- Realistic outcome: 2-3 acceptances out of 8-10 pitches

**Substack economics:**
- Free → paid conversion: 1-3% for high-quality niche
- 50 paid subs ≈ €50-150/month
- Realistic 6-month: 300-500 subs, 5-15 paid, €50-200/month
- Book funnel: 15-30 buy of 300 readers

**Amazon/Goodreads discovery:** Self-published academic-adjacent books rank near-zero without (a) 10K+ Twitter followers, (b) 50+ verified-purchase reviews, or (c) series/franchise momentum.

**Reddit:** r/consciousness and r/philosophy have strict no-self-promo rules (perma-ban risk). r/slatestarcodex allows essays IF author engages genuinely. **Viable only if essay is already on Substack/Medium and author is active.**

## The peer-review gate (Perplexity ignores this)

In 2026, podcast hosts (especially top-tier) expect:
- Preprint or accepted peer-reviewed paper, OR
- Institutional affiliation, OR
- Significant prior audience (100K+ Twitter)

**The currently in-flight Cortex RR (McFarnell), BBS commentary (Seth), and JCS submission are the gates** for higher-tier podcast acceptance. Perplexity treats book promo as independent of paper status — false in 2026.

**Recommendation:** Defer top-tier podcast outreach until BBS commentary submission (Jun 12) is in the system; mid-tier podcasts (MLST, Mind Chat) can be pitched now.

## Realistic success metric (not "sales")

- 3-4 podcast appearances over 6 months → theory validation in subject-matter audiences
