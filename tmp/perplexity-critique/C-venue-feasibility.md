## Entropy MDPI: detailed assessment

**Right:**
- Tucker/Luu/Friston 2025 (Entropy 27(8):829) is direct proof of concept for a theoretical criticality-consciousness paper in this venue.
- Reviewer pool overlap with FMT citation graph is real.
- SI track has weaker affiliation filter than regular submissions.

**Underweighted by Perplexity:**
- Finland JUFO Level-0 downgrade (Dec 2024) is *material*, not minor. JUFO classified 193 MDPI journals (Entropy included) as APC-driven editorial practice "grey area."
- Chinese Academy of Sciences removed all MDPI from its 2025 list.
- For a researcher with no prior peer-reviewed publication, a *first* citation in a Level-0 journal does not function as the credibility-building asset the strategy requires. Senior consciousness researchers — Melloni, Seth, Chalmers — track this landscape. CHF 2,600 buys peer-review certification the target audience discounts.
- A Zenodo preprint already provides the citable record. The marginal value of Entropy SI publication over Zenodo is small relative to its cost and reputation drag.

**Verified open SIs:** "Mathematics of Structured Experience: Exploring Dynamics, Topology, and Complexity in the Brain" — deadline within 2026, scope plausibly matches Unit 1. Needs direct verification of deadline and scope statement match before planning. The IIT II SI closed February 2025.

## Salami-slicing: is it sound?

**Concept: valid.** Splitting FMT into smaller units reframes "ambitious solo theory" → "targeted contribution to existing program." The criticality angle is the strongest reframe. Already AIW-47.

**Execution model: not calibrated.** Perplexity proposes 65-90 pages of new writing (Unit 1: 15-25, Unit 2: 10-15, Unit 3: 40-50) on top of:
- AIW-51 v5 deep revision (1-2 weeks focused work, prerequisite)
- AIW-49 BBS commentary (Jun 12 deadline)
- AIW-59 McFarnell reply (highest-leverage active item)
- AIW-04 Bochum prep (May 30 reg)
- Ivoclar full-time + family + summer

The opportunity-cost budget is missing.

**Sequencing assumption is unverified.** Perplexity claims Unit 3 "lands easily" once Units 1-2 are in print. No empirical basis. C&C's 104-minute desk reject was about operationalization, not publication count. A reviewer holding that standard will continue to hold it regardless of Entropy Unit 1.

**Historical precedent:** IIT (Tononi 2004) launched with full UW-Madison Center for Sleep & Consciousness backing; IIT 3.0 with multiple institutional co-authors. GWT (Dehaene 1998) launched in PNAS with INSERM/Collège de France + neuroimaging from day one. Neither built credibility via solo papers in lower-tier venues escalating to flagship. The actual path for new theories from outside the institutional mainstream is via **commentary culture (BBS, conference posters, invited talks)** and **co-authored empirical work** — exactly AIW-49 and AIW-48.

**Unit 2 → NoC RR is a known-blocked door.** Perplexity flags NoC as do-not-submit in the general track, then recommends its RR track. Andrillon desk-rejected ×2. Submitting again — even via RR — without confirming a different editor handles RR submissions is reputational risk.
