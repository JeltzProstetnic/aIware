## The critical problem: prediction specificity

The FMT paper §3.6 (line 209) states:
> "This classification was applied to the question of consciousness in Gruber (2015), where it was argued that consciousness requires Class 4 dynamics... This requirement was derived *theoretically*, from the computational properties needed for ongoing self-modeling, not from empirical neuroscience."

**What was actually derived in 2015:** a *general* requirement that consciousness needs Class-4 (criticality) dynamics.

**What Perplexity claims as "five confirmed predictions":** specific phenomena (anesthetic convergence, sleep restoration, bifurcation dynamics, split-brain holography).

**Assessment:** The 2015 work made a **broad** prediction (criticality is necessary). The 2024-2026 papers confirmed aspects empirically. Perplexity then *retrospectively organized* existing literature into five "predictions" presenting them as if 2015 made these exact predictions.

**This is post-hoc rationalization framed as prediction-confirmation.** A reviewer who reads the 2015 source will catch the gap.
