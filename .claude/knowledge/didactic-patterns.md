### Pattern 8 — the corrected arc (S232, authoritative draft `docs/aiw92-drafts/pattern8-revised.md`)
Both causal roles are **real but neither is free**:
1. **Two grips, neither free** — the felt "I chose to retreat inward at will" is the *same delayed-observer illusion* as "I chose to move my arm" (must follow/refresh the Libet material).
2. **The real second role = necessity, not freedom** — consciousness is the *necessary link* without which the inward chain reaction can't run; **no independent causal power, but not epiphenomenal**.
3. **The superpower (counterweight)** — the same capacity in graded/voluntary measure is *the most powerful tool evolution produced*: cognition, creativity, **Gedankenpaläste** (memory palaces), imagination, planning, what-if, mental time travel.
4. **The danger** — locked verbatim line (EN): *"and if you do it too much, who knows if you will come back and how many of you."* (DE locked, no "als": *"…ob du zurückkommst, und wie viele."*).
5. **Free-will resolution + practical program** — you didn't choose to find this, but the substrate is *shapeable*: block time for consciousness work (meditation, planned thinking, creative work, music, sport, reframing dead time) + mental hygiene (kill the negative monologue; anger = self-punishment with a hunting-era emotion; martial arts the easier road, meditation the harder).
- **ESM-on-EWM control (open, S232):** the degree of control over inner-world *contents* is specifically the **ESM's grip on the EWM** (self-model reshaping the world-model) — its own **superpower**, **level-gated** (recursion depth → ties to #9), and **partially trainable** (cf. phosphenes→faces cortical navigation). Magnitude unknown → needs a connectome + compute (AIW-91 / AC programme).
