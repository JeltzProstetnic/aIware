## 2. Consciousness-terminology discipline (French fuses the words — do NOT let it)

- **conscience** carries BOTH "consciousness" AND "moral conscience". Disambiguate by adjective/context; for the
  phenomenal sense just "la conscience".
- **"awareness" is a trap.** French has no clean word (attention ≠ awareness). In the book "awareness" is
  *content-directed access*, not the felt floor. Prefer restructuring: "avoir conscience de", "se rendre compte de",
  or a qualified "conscience de X". When EN contrasts awareness vs consciousness, preserve the contrast with an
  adjective ("conscience d'accès" vs "conscience phénoménale"), not two bare nouns.
- **The felt floor = sentience**, not "awareness".
- **self-model is the escape hatch:** when EN leans on architecture ("self-model"), keep it architectural in FR
  ("auto-modèle") — it sidesteps the conscience/conscience-morale fusion.
