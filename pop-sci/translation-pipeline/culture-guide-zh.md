# Culture & Tone Guide — Simplified Chinese · AIW-108-ZH

**Every translation agent receives this verbatim, alongside the glossary.**

## Variant & register (LOCKED)
- **Simplified Chinese (简体中文), Mainland orthography and lexicon.** General-audience popular science: clear,
  educated modern Mandarin (书面语 but accessible — not stiff, not colloquial).
- **Second person: 你** (warm, natural for popular nonfiction addressing the reader). Keep 你 consistent; do not
  switch to 您 mid-text.

## Voice & grandeur
- **Warm, vivid, confident narrative science.** Grandeur calibration = the **WARM MIDDLE**: EN runs grand, DE was
  dialed down for DACH reserve. Chinese popular science accepts narrative momentum and wonder, but avoid
  US-style self-promotion/hype. If a claim reads as boastful, soften. Keep the meaning; flag borderline
  self-promotion rather than silently flattening or inflating.
- Break English sentence rhythm into natural Chinese flow; do not calque English syntax. Keep the author's punch.

## Sources (CJK-specific)
- **Translate from the EN** (primary). Use the DE **only** to disambiguate MEANING (DE = source of truth for meaning).
  Do NOT inherit German idiom — it does not transfer to Chinese.

## Typography
- **Quotation marks: "…" (full-width curly double) as PRIMARY**, nested '…'. This is the Mainland Simplified standard —
  do NOT use «guillemets» or 「…」(that is Traditional/Japanese).
- Full-width punctuation: ，。：；！？（）. No spaces between characters.
- **Numbers:** keep the EN digits as-is (do NOT reformat or convert to 汉字数字).
- Preserve Markdown structure exactly (##, **bold**, *italic*, lists) — translate heading TEXT, keep the `##` level.

## Do-not-localize
- Author's personal anecdotes, biography, place references (keep as lived). Proper names, citations, DOIs, references.
- Idioms that won't land → translate the MEANING naturally (culture pass refines later); flag doubtful ones.

## Output contract (STRICT)
- Return ONLY the Simplified-Chinese translation of the EN span — same paragraph/heading structure, same Markdown,
  SAME paragraph count (one EN paragraph → one ZH paragraph). No added notes, no code fences.
- Pure reference/citation lists → return verbatim.
