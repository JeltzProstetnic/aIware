## 2. Consciousness-terminology discipline
- **意识 = consciousness.** Awareness (content-directed access, the book's sense) → **觉知**, NOT 意识. Preserve the
  awareness-vs-consciousness contrast (觉知 vs 意识). The felt floor = **感知力**.
- **self-model (自我模型) is the architectural escape hatch** — keep it architectural when EN leans on it.
