## 2. Consciousness-terminology discipline
- **consciência** = consciousness AND moral conscience (Latin *conscientia*). Disambiguate by adjective; for the
  phenomenal sense just "a consciência".
- **"awareness" is a trap.** In the book it means *content-directed access*, not the felt floor. Prefer
  "ter consciência de", "dar-se conta de", or qualified "consciência de X". When EN contrasts awareness vs
  consciousness, keep the contrast with an adjective ("consciência de acesso" vs "consciência fenomenal").
- **The felt floor = senciência**, not "awareness".
- **self-model is the escape hatch** — keep it architectural ("automodelo") when EN leans on architecture.
