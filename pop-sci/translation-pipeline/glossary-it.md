## 2. Consciousness-terminology discipline (Italian is EASIER here — it HAS an awareness word)
- **coscienza** = consciousness AND moral conscience; disambiguate by adjective; phenomenal sense = "la coscienza".
- **Italian HAS a clean awareness word: "consapevolezza"** (awareness / being-aware-of). In the book "awareness"
  means *content-directed access* → render as **consapevolezza** (or "consapevolezza di X"), NOT "coscienza".
  This preserves the EN awareness-vs-consciousness contrast cleanly (consapevolezza vs coscienza).
- **The felt floor = senzienza**, not consapevolezza and not coscienza-bare.
- **self-model is the escape hatch** — keep it architectural ("automodello") when EN leans on architecture.
