## 2. Consciousness-terminology discipline (Spanish collapses the words — do NOT let it)

- **conciencia** carries BOTH "consciousness" AND "moral conscience" (Latin *conscientia*). Disambiguate by
  adjective/context; for the phenomenal sense just "la conciencia". Do not invent "consciencia".
- **"awareness" is a trap.** Spanish has no clean word; and in the book "awareness" is *content-directed access*,
  not the felt floor. Prefer restructuring: "darse cuenta de", "percatarse de", or a qualified "conciencia de X".
  Never equate awareness with conciencia bare. When EN contrasts awareness vs consciousness, preserve the contrast
  with an adjective ("conciencia de acceso" vs "conciencia fenoménica"), not two bare nouns.
- **The felt floor = sintiencia**, not "awareness". (fish/reptile: feels & fears, no past/future, no symbols.)
- **self-model is the escape hatch:** when the EN leans on architecture ("self-model") rather than the lexical
  war, keep it architectural in ES too ("automodelo") — it sidesteps the conciencia/conscience fusion.
