- Reversibility commitment (§8.4). The declared commitment is now that the realised substrate dynamics is reversible: along the trajectory the substrate actually follows, no two distinct states evolve to the same state, and total information is conserved. An injective rule meets it on every state; a finite substrate without a beginning meets it even under a many-to-one rule, since only states on its attractor cycle occur and each has exactly one predecessor there. The paper previously required the rule itself to be injective.
