*Prediction 5 (Section 8.6).* The testability paragraph now bounds what similarity judgments can show. A dissimilarity matrix built from judgments is a readout, and taking its structure for the structure of the qualia space commits the readout fallacy (Gruber, 2015, p. 45; Gruber, 2026a, Section 2.7). On this theory the judgments recover the relational organization of explicit content that enters a judgment.
