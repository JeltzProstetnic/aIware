**v2 (2026-10-19)**

Supersedes v1 (2026-05-20). The data, the analysis and all numerical results are unchanged.

*Interpretation of the small-scale excess (abstract, Sections 5.6, 5.7, 6).* The 9.6σ excess in Band 6 (ℓ = 1500–2500) is now attributed tentatively to non-Gaussian instrumental noise and unresolved astrophysical sources, with confirmation requiring end-to-end noise simulations. Weak gravitational lensing is added as a further candidate contributor: the Gaussian simulations contain none of lensing's mode coupling, so lensing can contribute to the Band 6 excess, and a comparison of lensed and unlensed simulations is named as the test. The earlier text dismissed lensing on the grounds that the detected multifractality sat at ℓ < 100; there is no large-scale detection, since Bands 0–5 match the Gaussian null.

*Movahed et al. (2011).* The author list and the reference are corrected to Movahed, Ghasemi, Rahvar & Tabar (2011), *Physical Review E* 84(2), 021103. The quotation of their conclusion now reads "mainly due to the long-range correlations", and the paper says the power spectrum largely, not entirely, explains the multifractality, here and in Conclusion 1.

*Broadbridge et al. (2022).* The reference now gives the published version, *ANZIAM Journal* 64(2), 90–118 (2022), in place of the 2021 arXiv preprint; the year is updated in the text and in the Section 5.2 heading.

*Other corrections.* An unsourced sentence claiming that SOC systems typically show Δh > 0.2 was removed from Section 3.3. The suppression of non-Gaussian signatures by inflation is stated as exponential in the number of e-folds (N ≈ 60), without a specific factor. The self-citation of the cosmology paper now carries its actual title, "Emergent spacetime from self-referential computation: A hierarchical cellular automaton framework", and its concept DOI (10.5281/zenodo.18698605). The Band 6 null values in the tables are given to one more significant figure (0.00233 ± 0.00075; observed 0.00951). The DOI printed under the author block is now this work's concept DOI (10.5281/zenodo.20306784).
