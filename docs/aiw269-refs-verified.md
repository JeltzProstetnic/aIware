**Claim checks that change a draft.** Details are in the rows and in the summary at the end.
1. **A8, Bajwa et al. 2025.** The abstract says participants were woken from **deep** sedation, not "lighter sedation". It also found **no difference** in PCIst or LZc between awakenings with and without experience, which bears directly on A8's proposed prediction.
2. **A8, Sikka et al. 2026.** The maintenance-or-emergence point is in the full text (Discussion), not in the abstract.
3. **A4, Wu.** The registration is dated **2026-09-20**, not 09-23.
4. **A9, Corcoran et al.** It is a *three*-theory review: IIT, Neurorepresentationalism and Active Inference. It also carries a group author, and it went online 2026-05-14.
5. **F1, Roig et al.** The necessary-and-sufficient theorem is **Sugiura et al.'s**. Roig et al. extend it to discrete time.
6. **G1, Gurnee et al.** The paper reports ignition-like sharpening, but its authors write that it is "unclear whether this mirrors the sharp, competitive 'ignition'" seen in the brain.
7. **Bates et al.** "Reality monitoring" there means spoken-versus-imagined *source memory*, not live perception against imagery.
