**v5 (2026-10-19)**

Supersedes v4 (2026-10-07). Companion to the Four-Model Theory v21.

*Corrections from a cross-check of all FMT publications.* A cross-check of the FMT corpus against the master paper and against itself led to these corrections:
- Explicit models. The abstract and §2 now say that explicit models are phenomenal in the self-model's closed loop, where they previously called every explicit model phenomenal.
- Sufficiency. §2 said that free modelling and the four-model architecture are jointly sufficient. It now says they are separately necessary and not jointly sufficient, and states the theory's sufficient condition: the four model kinds under closure, free compute, embodiment and at least one pain-pleasure axis. The canonical four are now "the minimum set of model kinds the architecture forces", no longer a "minimum sufficient set".
- Criticality as signature. §6 no longer treats criticality as an ingredient that buys an advantage; the sentence now says that closure does nothing on its own.
- Notation. The spectral radius of the echo-state networks is now written ρ_sr throughout §3.2, §4.4 and §4.6, so it is no longer confused with the branching ratio σ. The self-knowledge readout of §4.6 is named R_ISM and described as a correlation proxy.
- Chance levels. §4.3 now defines the chance level of the transfer probe (one move with five possible actions, one of which steps onto the hazard, so 0.8) and states that the arm without the self/other projection scores at chance. §4.5 gives the decoding figures (0.61–0.76 against chance 0.50 across distractor delays of 20–45 steps) in place of a mislabelled "decodability" range.
- Ordinals. §8 now refers to the fifth result, not the sixth, as the one pricing the removal of closure, and to the seventh, not the eighth, as the optimality result, matching §5 and the master paper.
- Citations to the master paper. The master is cited as Gruber (2026) with its Zenodo concept DOI, 10.5281/zenodo.18669891, where it was listed as a manuscript. The section references in §1 and §8 now point to §3.4.3, §7.1 (Table 5, note ‡) and §8.10.

No result or method changed, and no reference was added or removed. The paper is 15 pages.
