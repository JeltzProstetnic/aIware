When editing the German book manuscript (`pop-sci/book-manuscript-de.md`):

- **No US-style grandeur or egocentrism.** German/Swiss/Austrian culture shuns self-celebration. Never compare the author to Mendel/Boltzmann. Never call decisions "remarkably strategic." No hero's journey framing. No "tears on a bridge" dramatics.
- **But also not dead academic Sachlichkeit.** The book should be readable *despite* DACH academic culture, not in submission to it. Don't strip all personality — strip the ego. The book is pop-sci, not a Habilitationsschrift.
- **Third person for "Über den Autor."** CV-based, factual. Professional credentials, not an autobiography.
- **Theory over person.** Frame everything around what the theory explains, not what the author experienced or felt. "Die Teile ordneten sich" not "ich sah die gesamte Architektur."
- **Present tense for field descriptions.** The consciousness literature IS scattered, not WAS scattered when the author encountered it.
