# Citation and quotation audit — "Pointing Is Not Defining" (drafts/papers-moc7/definitions-not-settled.md)

Read-only audit; no file edited, no git run. Method: Crossref API for all 31 DOIs + bibliographic queries for the non-DOI entries; full-text comparison of every verbatim quotation against the original (BBS print scan for Block, BRR layout PDF for Searle, NLM scan for Crick & Koch, PMC XML for Tononi 2004 and Francken, AAAS reprint for Casali, 1980 Harvard scan for Kripke, JSTOR scan for Nagel, PLOS/Springer/phimisci OA PDFs, author PDFs for Chalmers/Schwitzgebel/Frankish/Dennett); MoC7 quotes checked against the local booklet (`docs/moc7/moc7-booklet-web-full.pdf`, pdftotext); in-text/reference-list consistency and alphabetical order checked by script. Five research subagents did the fetching; I independently re-checked the three findings that would be most damaging if wrong (Webb/Graziano order, Block "desires", Kleiner & Ludwig/Nagel).

**Plain verdict: nothing is fabricated and every verbatim quotation is real and attributed to the right source, but there are five attribution errors that the cited authors would catch on sight (Webb & Graziano reversed; Bayne et al. 2024 made to say a definition is required; Kleiner & Ludwig's unity aspect credited to Nagel; Tsuchiya & Saigo said to take the phenomenal concept as given when they try to define it; Casali's calibration said to "rest on report"), one misquotation of Block (hyphen) and one of Nagel (dash/italics), plus a dozen formatting fixes.**

Legend: OK = verified correct; FIX = change needed; SOFTEN = defensible but a specialist would push back; UNVERIFIED = source not reachable, wording not checked.
