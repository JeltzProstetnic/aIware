# Opus referee — paper1 draft 3 ("Limits Without an Outside", AIW-289), S344 2026-10-20

Verdict: careful argument, citations real; three quoted slogans in the opening misattribute; the "unconditional" limits are unconditional only relative to the paper's definitions, so the abstract's "input every claimant presupposes" overreaches. 8,087 body words.

## Argument defects
- **A1 (critical, l.15)** Quoted slogans misattribute: Nagel 1986 defends the view from nowhere as an approachable ideal; Priest 1995 holds limits are crossed AND not crossed; Putnam 1981 p.50 says no God's Eye view "that we can know or usefully imagine". Fix: paraphrase, no quote marks.
- **A2 (major, l.27/41/105)** L1 rests on stipulations (content complete at time held; anything that distinguishes is a perspective). The refutation binds only claimants accepting the §2 frame/content relation. Narrow l.9 and l.141 ("every claimant presupposes") to that, so l.127 and l.141 agree.
- **A3 (major, l.33/53)** L2 holds by what "certify" means (conceded l.53). Say so in §6 and abstract.
- **A4 (major, l.31/129)** "Proper part" assumed; a frame gives part-of-W, not proper part. Add D = W case (solipsist/absolute idealist) to §8 or argue it.
- **A5 (major)** Mackie terms mixed: l.31 "pragmatic", l.107/129 "operational", l.121 "logical rather than performative". Use "operational" throughout; l.121 → "logically inconsistent for a claimant that admits its position (Section 6)". (Earlier F6/N4, half fixed.)
- **A6 (moderate, l.63)** Resource premise smuggled; Culik & Yu is undecidability (Rice-type), not irreducibility. Fix: "so whether the limit applies has no general decision procedure"; state resource premise.
- **A7 (moderate, l.83 vs l.91)** "complete security under classical logic" vs revisable falsity verdicts → "security relative to classical logic and the membership notion every later theory keeps".
- **A8 (moderate, l.133)** Unsupported priority claim vs Popper; Bartley 1962 / Miller 1994 apply CR reflexively. "to our knowledge" + where Bartley stops.
- **A9 (minor, l.107)** Burnyeat claim needs page or softer wording.
- **A10 (minor, l.43)** Recursion theorem statement: "for any computable transformation f of programs there is e with φ_e = φ_f(e); a corollary is a quine".
- **A11 (minor, l.21)** Gruber 2015 p.21 equates Bezugssystem/Modell "zumindest für unsere Zwecke" → "for present purposes".
