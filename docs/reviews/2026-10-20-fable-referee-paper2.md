Fix: settled = a shared procedure, whether or not its verdicts are agreed (fits C5 second disjunct and Europa analogy l.150) — non-settledness becomes empirical.

- **A7 (minor, l.140)** Francken: three explicit questions + one indirect, not "four questions".
