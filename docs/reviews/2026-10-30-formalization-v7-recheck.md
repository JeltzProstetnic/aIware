# FMT formalization v7 — independent Fable re-check (S367, 2026-10-30)

Read-only Fable pass over the v7 revision (commit cadeba91 vs cadeba91~1), after the Fable review workflow (`2026-10-30-formalization-fable-review.json`) had been applied. Line numbers refer to cadeba91.

**Verdict (re-checker):** the revision moves in the right direction (coupled-update closure, nesting, two closure grades, separated criticality axes, companion hedged, no 2026-10-30 crucible result cited by name), but introduced six formal inconsistencies, one breach of an MG-held-open ruling and one false attribution to Toker et al. Fix 1–8 before it goes to MG; 9–19 are polish. After that it is sound enough for MG's review.

1. **§2.1 l.66 / §2.3 l.96 — critical.** "EWM-pole (s = 1, ν = 1): explicit modeling of the non-self, EWM∖ESM" and "θ_EWM bounds the explicit non-self mass, the closure partner EWM∖ESM". l.57 defines s = 1 as everything the system models, the self included; l.60 defines EWM(t) := {m : ν > ν_crit}; "EWM" now means three things and §4.1 l.280 uses the whole-stratum reading. Fix l.66: "EWM-pole (s = 1, ν = 1): explicit modeling of everything the system models, fully explicit; the closure partner EWM∖ESM is the explicit stratum outside N_ESM, a set of models, not a pole." Fix l.96: "θ_EWM bounds the explicit mass outside N_ESM, ∫_{ν>ν_crit} ρ − ∫_{N_ESM} ρ > θ_EWM, the closure partner EWM∖ESM."
2. **§2.3 l.96 — critical.** "N_ESM is defined by the role a model plays in the closure … mass there without closure is the predicted non-conscious case" is self-contradictory and contradicts §4.5 l.344. Fix: "N_ESM is defined by target and integration, a model of the system itself or at least the viewpoint to which the senses are referred (Gruber, 2026, Section 3.4.3), not by its s-coordinate alone; whether that mass is in closure is the separate condition χ_act > 0 of §3.5."
3. **§2.1 l.60 — major (MG-held-open).** "whether a pattern outlives its input is a question about its kind, not about whether it is a model" settles what D-S366-G T5 holds open until CRU-238 (Principle 2 "survive their own inputs"; D-S366-C provisional). Fix: "The parent theory's second principle has the explicit patterns survive their own inputs (Gruber, 2026, Section 3.3); whether a pattern locked to current input is a model of a simpler kind is open, and the formalization carries kind as a label until a measure exists."
4. **Table 1 l.187–188 — major.** "Self-scope P collapses | P(0 → 0) → 0" removes the ESM's only implicit source under G = κ A_s φ(ν), contradicting §5.3 l.442 and §5.1 l.416–418 (α → 0 on i_self). Fix rows: "Self-scope P unchanged; self-referential sensory input unweighted | T_{i_self→e|i_ext} → 0; T_{i_ext→e|i_self} ↑ (§5.2)"; highest doses: "… T_{i_ext→e|i_self} ↑ with T from sensory input ≈ 0: i_ext internally generated".
5. **§4.2 l.300 — major (false attribution).** "In Toker et al.'s usage the chaotic side counts as the ordered phase relative to the edge" — Toker label periodic/stable vs chaotic/unstable (D-S360-3; master Table 4 note). Fix: delete, or "Toker et al. label the two sides periodic/stable and chaotic/unstable; 'ordered' here keeps its reservoir-computing sense of stable, periodic dynamics."
