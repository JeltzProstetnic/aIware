# AI Handoff Brief: FMT, Rival Theories, and Research-Program Assessment

Prepared 20 October 2026. This is a self-contained handoff of a research conversation, not an independent replication, proof audit, or authoritative ranking of consciousness theories.

## User context and evaluation task

The user is Matthias Gruber, author of Four-Model Theory (FMT). He asked where Johannes Kleiner’s research program and his own program fall on a scale from “sinnlos” to “Weltformel,” then asked which three FMT claims would most change that assessment and how FMT’s potential compares with its main three rivals.

Evaluate intellectual substance, not institutional affiliation, prestige, or team size. The user explicitly asked to disregard the current one-person nature of his program and said that this is about to change.

The comparison used IIT, Global Neuronal Workspace, and predictive-processing/active-inference consciousness theories. These are useful comparison groups, not an exhaustive or objectively ranked “top three.”

## Version and evidence boundary

- **FMT snapshot:** The reviewed master paper was v23, published 20 October 2026; use the pinned [v23 record](https://zenodo.org/records/23492859), not an older cached rendering of the [concept DOI](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.18669891).

- **Review depth:** Central FMT sections, its predictions and limitations, and selected rival/Kleiner papers were examined. Some surrounding works were assessed from abstracts and detailed repository descriptions rather than audited in full.
- **Unperformed work:** No simulations were independently rerun, no novel experiments were conducted, and no exhaustive mathematical consistency or priority review was completed. Verify subsequent versions before making new claims about the current program.

## Prior assessment to scrutinize, not inherit as fact

The previous assistant’s rough judgments were Kleiner around **7/10**, the FMT-centered program around **6–7/10**, and the cosmological extension around **3–4/10**. These were subjective assessments of substance and promise, not probabilities of correctness or percentages of progress toward a completed Weltformel.

The substantive verdict was that Kleiner has more completed formal machinery, while FMT offers an explicit candidate architecture with an emerging computational test program. FMT could become an important theory of consciousness, but its potential is not demonstrably greater than that of IIT or strong active-inference approaches.

Keep four questions separate:

- **Explanatory ambition:** How much would the theory explain if correct?
- **Formal maturity:** Which definitions, derivations, and implementations are actually complete?
- **Discriminating evidence:** Which observations favor it over specified alternatives?
- **Durable usefulness:** What remains valuable if its central consciousness identification fails?

## Interpret FMT accurately

- **Architecture, not four modules:** FMT distinguishes implicit/explicit modeling across self/world scopes, yielding four model kinds rather than four anatomically separate modules; the kinds are a minimum, not a claim that the brain contains exactly four models ([FMT v23](https://zenodo.org/records/23492859)).
- **Closure:** The core proposal concerns self/world modeling entering a closed, self-referential organization, not generic feedback, a position estimate, narrative self-talk, or DMN activity alone ([FMT v23, §§3.1–3.5](https://zenodo.org/records/23492859)).
- **Free modeling:** Open-ended computation autonomously recruited for the system’s own modeling is treated as necessary but insufficient for consciousness; near-criticality is a possible biological signature, neither a necessary nor sufficient condition by itself ([FMT v23, Table 1b and §3.7](https://zenodo.org/records/23492859)).
- **Sufficiency is conjunctive:** The proposed sufficient conditions include the four model kinds under closure, free computation, embodiment, and at least one pain–pleasure axis, rather than closure or complexity considered alone ([FMT v23, §3.7.3](https://zenodo.org/records/23492859)).
- **Phenomenal bridge:** FMT explicitly acknowledges that identifying the relevant closure with lived experience is argued for rather than derived; do not criticize it as if this gap were unnoticed or claim that the paper has deductively eliminated it ([FMT v23, §3.4.3](https://zenodo.org/records/23492859)).
- **Reported simulations:** These test component mechanisms and resource-sensitive architectural claims, not consciousness; the program retains negative results and retreats from stronger functional closure-necessity claims ([in-silico companion](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23483525)).
- **Formalization status:** The mathematical companion is a proposed formalization and research specification requiring expert verification, not a completed collection of established theorems ([formalization companion](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23483510)).

The prior assessment credited architectural specificity, prospective predictions, and retained negative results. Its central reservation was that engineering utility, self-referential organization, and phenomenal experience are not interchangeable evidential achievements.

## Three claims with the highest assessment impact

### Closure is necessary for experience, not merely its report

FMT predicts that disrupting the self-modeling loop itself should degrade experience, while metacognitive readout disruption alone need not do so; it acknowledges the lack of an established direct measurement protocol for closure ([FMT v23, Table 1b](https://zenodo.org/records/23492859)). The assessment therefore identified this as the highest-stakes claim, but not a ready-made decisive experiment.

- **Upward evidence:** Selective, reproducible effects of independently verified closure disruption on experience, beyond general impairment and predictions of a specified rival.
- **Downward evidence:** Credible preservation of experience after the operationally defined closure has genuinely been disrupted.
- **Required safeguards:** Identify closure without defining it by the desired consciousness verdict; control arousal, sensory processing, memory, and response demands; triangulate experience independently.
- **Do not overclaim:** Loss of confidence or autobiographical narration is insufficient. Neither no-report markers nor synthetic closure ablations provide infallible consciousness ground truth.

### Free modeling is independently identifiable

Table 1b proposes comparing novel-input behavior with a fixed lookup fitted to past behavior ([FMT v23](https://zenodo.org/records/23492859)). The assessment regarded this as insufficient as the principal discriminator: generalization beyond memorized examples does not establish unrestricted computational universality, autonomous self-modeling, or necessity for consciousness.

- **Upward evidence:** A formal definition yielding a nontrivial scaling, transfer, or causal prediction that survives strong matched-resource baselines.
- **Downward evidence:** Inability to distinguish free modeling from ordinary adaptive inference, or post hoc reclassification of every counterexample.
- **Recommended comparisons:** Trained predictive models, active-inference agents, recurrent controllers, and appropriate feedforward/unrolled alternatives, not only reactive or memorizing baselines.
- **Scope limit:** An architectural efficiency result supports that efficiency claim. It does not by itself validate a consciousness criterion, and a null result in one task need not falsify the entire criterion.

### Self-model redirection predicts altered identity content

FMT predicts that dominant sensory input shapes what a participant reports “being” or “becoming” during ego dissolution; it explicitly acknowledges a related predictive-processing explanation through relaxed self-priors ([FMT v23, §8.3, Prediction 2](https://zenodo.org/records/23492859)). This was identified as a concrete empirical opportunity, not an already demonstrated unique prediction.

- **Upward evidence:** A preregistered, held-out pattern of identity attribution predicted better by FMT than by an explicitly specified rival.
- **Downward evidence:** No meaningful input–identity relationship under independently satisfied prerequisites; equal performance primarily weakens distinctiveness rather than the mechanism itself.
- **Controls:** Attention, salience, expectancy, suggestibility, degree of ego dissolution, and the distinction between identity attribution, imagery, metaphor, and compliance.
- **Crucial unresolved question:** Modality-specific likelihoods and precisions could allow a predictive-processing model to predict modality-specific identity changes. Establish an actual disagreement before collecting data or claiming a unique discriminator.
- **Ethics:** Any human intervention requires an appropriately approved research setting. No experimental protocol or drug regimen has been designed here.

## Potential against the main rivals

### Integrated Information Theory

IIT 4.0 identifies experience with the intrinsic cause–effect structure of a maximal physical complex; phenomenal quality is associated with the full structure, not just a scalar quantity, and exhaustive calculation for realistic systems is computationally infeasible ([IIT 4.0](https://journals.plos.org/ploscompbiol/article?id=10.1371/journal.pcbi.1011465)). Its small-system calculations illustrate the formalism rather than independently demonstrate consciousness ([IIT 4.0](https://journals.plos.org/ploscompbiol/article?id=10.1371/journal.pcbi.1011465)).

- **FMT opportunity:** A tractable architectural account of an embodied perspective, potentially easier to implement and manipulate.
- **Comparative judgment:** IIT is more formally explicit; FMT could become more operationally useful.
- **Unresolved burden:** Specify systems on which the criteria disagree, then identify accessible evidence bearing on that disagreement. Tractability does not establish truth.

### Global Neuronal Workspace

GNW proponents associate conscious content with workspace activity and make commitments concerning ignition, distributed availability, and decodability; they do not describe the program as merely a theory of verbal report ([GNW response to COGITATE](https://academic.oup.com/nc/article/2025/1/niaf037/8280147)). Their response also points to developed neuronal models and disputes the interpretation of some adversarial-test predictions ([GNW response](https://academic.oup.com/nc/article/2025/1/niaf037/8280147)).

- **FMT opportunity:** More extensive architectural treatment of selfhood, altered identity, and experience/report dissociations.
- **Comparative judgment:** GNW is the more mature experimental benchmark; FMT has a plausible route to additional explanatory breadth.
- **Unresolved burden:** Demonstrate a closure-versus-workspace dissociation. Finding self-modeling within a workspace could support synthesis rather than replacement.

### Predictive processing and active inference

The minimal active-inference theory connects conscious content to inferred states and discusses access at the continuous-sensory/discrete-policy interface ([minimal theory](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2410.06633)). AI-C also has a protocol testing whether active inference is necessary for changes in conscious content; that protocol is not a report of completed experimental outcomes ([AI-C protocol](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0328836)).

IWMT already proposes coherent, embodied self/world modeling and integrates active inference with IIT and GNW ([IWMT](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/computational-neuroscience/articles/10.3389/fncom.2022.642397/full)). It should therefore be treated as a close conceptual comparator, not ignored in favor of generic prediction-error minimization.

- **FMT opportunity:** Additional restrictive architecture that identifies which forms of modeling matter.
- **Comparative judgment:** This is the strongest direct competition and the hardest novelty test; FMT’s ceiling is high but not clearly higher.
- **Unresolved burden:** Show a predictive, causal, or explanatory-economy advantage beyond redescription or a particular implementation of an embodied generative model.

The 2025 COGITATE study found challenges for specified IIT and GNWT predictions in 256 participants, while testing selected biological implementations rather than conclusively adjudicating either entire theory ([COGITATE study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12137136/)). This is not automatic evidence for FMT: fewer demanding tests also mean fewer opportunities to fail.

## Kleiner and the surrounding Gruber program

- **Kleiner’s reusable contribution:** His program includes mathematical consciousness science and formal measurement work, rather than a single rival architectural theory ([research overview](https://jkleiner.de/), [Co-Activation Analysis](https://philarchive.org/archive/KLEOTL)). The assessment considered such work potentially useful even if FMT succeeds.
- **Chip no-go theorem:** Its conclusion depends on technical “dynamical relevance,” concerning changes relative to reference dynamics, not simply ordinary causal participation in computation ([paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11671748/), [author’s clarification](https://johanneskleiner.substack.com/p/on-our-no-go-theorem-for-ai-consciousness)). An FMT-style constitutive account may reject the relevant premise; that is neither a demonstrated refutation of the theorem nor a completed proof that FMT falls outside its scope.
- **Kleiner’s physics branch:** Collaborative causal-fermion-systems work includes an effective collapse derivation under specified assumptions and limits ([CFS paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2405.19254)). The assessment judged this more mathematically developed than SB-HC4A, without treating it as an empirically established Weltformel.
- **RIM:** The motivation-as-allocation-policy proposal provides a concrete cognitive-modeling direction, but retrospective accommodation must remain distinct from prospective confirmation ([RIM v7](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23483515)).
- **SB-HC4A:** Its computational cosmology contains acknowledged open bridges and speculative unifications; its grade should not be inherited from FMT or vice versa ([cosmology v9](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23483519)).
- **CMB analysis:** The reported lack of large-scale excess multifractality and cautious instrumental/astrophysical interpretation of the small-scale excess do not establish the cosmological architecture ([CMB analysis](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23483637)).
- **Philosophical extensions:** No Outside and The Worm on the Disk develop arguments about embedded description and implementation, not independent empirical confirmations of FMT ([No Outside](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23473517), [The Worm on the Disk](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23483528)). Do not allege a simple “running versus stored” contradiction without examining the latter’s conditional argument and its distinction between structural realization and temporal unfolding.

## Recommended continuation

The next proposed task, not yet performed, is a focused adversarial analysis of Prediction 2. Determine whether FMT’s redirection mechanism and a strong predictive-processing or IWMT account actually imply different observable outcomes.

- **Extract commitments:** Separate what each theory entails from auxiliary assumptions added to construct a model.
- **Specify models:** Use the same inputs and observables, with transparent parameter freedom and complexity costs.
- **Find divergence:** Derive a condition with different predictions, or explicitly conclude that this experiment cannot distinguish the accounts.
- **Design only afterward:** Specify outcomes, confounds, preregistered failure criteria, and held-out evaluation if a genuine divergence exists.
- **Report neutrally:** A valuable synthesis is an acceptable result. Do not force a winner, invent effect sizes, interpret rival silence as a prediction of no effect, or treat the prior assistant’s ratings as evidence.

The central standard is consistent across programs: architectural utility, preferred explanations of conscious phenomena, and an established criterion for experience across substrates are different achievements. The highest-value next result would be independently reproduced evidence that favors a distinctive FMT commitment over a strong, explicitly specified alternative.
