# Brief: propagate the FMT v17 criticality ruling to the other papers (S332, 2026-10-07)

## The ruling (MG S329, binding, published in FMT v17 `10.5281/zenodo.23216247`)
- The requirement for consciousness is **free modelling**: open-ended (Class 4) computational capability **recruited by the system for its own modelling**, together with the four-model architecture. Free modelling is **necessary and not sufficient**.
- **Criticality is a contingent signature** that free modelling usually leaves in cortex. It is **neither necessary nor sufficient**. A subcritical conscious system is accommodated. Direction: free modelling → criticality pattern, never criticality → consciousness.
- Criticality data **cannot disconfirm** FMT in either direction. The disconfirmer targets free modelling: *"robust consciousness where a fixed lookup reproduces the system's behaviour on novel input"* (FMT v17 Table 1b).
- Universal computation does not need the edge of chaos (Siegelmann & Sontag 1995; Maass 1996). In rate reservoirs the computational optimum sits in the driven-stable, ordered regime (Schuecker, Goedeke & Helias 2018; Haruna & Nakajima 2019; crucible CRU-203 replication, companion v4 §4.4).
- Whether free modelling at depth needs a near-critical operating point in any substrate is **deliberately open** — do not claim it either way.

## What to change — by ROLE, not by phrase
Any passage that makes criticality / the edge of chaos / a near-critical regime a **condition** of consciousness, of explicit modelling, of binding, of lucid or dream onset, or of self-simulation. Indirect forms count:
- "a substrate operating at criticality" as what produces X
- "the regime consciousness requires"
- "loss of consciousness = a push below criticality" stated as the cause, rather than the loss of free modelling showing as a subcritical push
- "X becomes possible when the substrate reaches sufficient criticality"
- "Class 4 = edge of chaos = universality" equated as the requirement

Banned forms: "no criticality → no consciousness", "criticality is necessary/required/a prerequisite", "operates at criticality" as a condition, and any reverse or biconditional form (criticality → consciousness; only / iff / exclusively / tracks / consciousness-locked).

KEEP: criticality as an observed signature, the empirical criticality literature as convergent evidence *for the signature*, and measurement methods. Descriptions of what other theories (e.g. ConCrit) claim stay as attributions.
