# A cheap, drug-free, discriminating test of FMT — candidates and verdict (S358)

Sources read in full: the FMT master (`paper/full/four-model-theory-full.md`, cited below by section and line), the 2nd-edition book (`~/simbook/emergenz/de/`, cited by chapter file, line and 2015 page `S<n>`; theory chapters read directly, neuroscience chapters 09, 10, 12–15 extracted claim-by-claim by two reader agents), `prediction-framing.md`, `fmt-prediction-map.json`, `fmt-misconception-registry.md`, `fmt-2015-definitions.md`. Rival commitments were checked against the published statements named in the source list, not inferred. Bar applied (coordinator relay of MG, this session): an a-priori FMT prediction counts when IIT/GNW/PP are silent or could only absorb the result after the fact; anything every theory predicts in advance is rejected.

## 1. Verdict

The cheapest genuinely new and pre-registrable FMT test is a two-questionnaire study on ~200 colleagues: the frequency with which a person's own substrate processing leaks into experience (phosphenes, visual snow, hypnagogic imagery, filling-in artefacts) should predict the "hidden depth" form of the hard-problem intuition — a prediction the master's meta-problem account makes in so many words (§3.8), that IIT, GNW and PP do not make, and that AST and illusionism predict should fail.

Two further candidates follow, ranked below it. A general point first, because it explains why the field is thin: FMT absorbs GNW's access machinery as channel occupancy (§3.6.1, §7.2), adopts PP's inference dynamics as the implicit layer (§7.2), and shares IIT's structural commitments about qualia space (§8.6). Its own mechanisms — closure, permeability modes, redirection, forking, the temporal echo — are reachable only through drugs, lesions, imaging or in-silico ablation, and the master says so itself ("No such manipulation exists in brains", §7.2, line 877). What remains cheap is (a) the meta-problem account, whose second mechanism is FMT-only, (b) the recursion-depth axis, which the SMoC chart records as field-blank, and (c) a drug-free route to redirection. The three candidates are exactly those.

## 2. Ranked candidates

### Candidate 1 (rank 1). Trait permeability leaks predict the hidden-depth intuition

**FMT derivation.** §3.6 (line 419): in normal waking the implicit–explicit boundary is "a graded filter, not a wall"; "substrate-level processing artifacts routinely leak through: blind-spot filling …, phosphenes …, and visual snow". §3.8 (lines 547–549) gives the meta-problem two mechanisms: structural opacity of the substrate to the ESM, which every person has, and "near-opacity punctuated by occasional leaks", which "produces precisely the phenomenology that the Meta-Problem describes: the persistent, nagging intuition that consciousness is somehow deeper than any explanation can reach, that something vast operates just beyond the edge of introspective access". §3.7.2 (line 523) names the cortical component of closed-eye phosphenes as "the lowest layer of implicit-to-explicit permeability". Prediction 6 already carries a leak arm (§8.7, lines 1025–1031): a contingent but uninterpretable display "leaves mystery unchanged or raises it". The book supplies the trait side: the closed-eye observation (`20-…`, L137–143, S283–284) and the closed-eye progression dots → fractal → faces → scenes (`15-…`, L321–323, S246–247) are presented as things anyone can observe, with individual differences in patience and concentration.

Leak frequency varies across people — visual snow symptoms 3.7 % and visual snow syndrome 2.2 % in a UK sample (Kondziella et al., 2020), lifetime hypnagogic hallucinations 37 % (Ohayon et al., 1996), closed-eye phosphene noticing and filling-in awareness vary without any syndrome. If the second mechanism of §3.8 does any work, the intuition it feeds must vary with it. That is the prediction; it is not in the master, the map, or the proposed-predictions file.

**Pre-registrable hypotheses.**
H1: partial correlation between leak frequency and the hidden-depth intuition, controlling the covariates listed below, is positive, with r ≥ .15 as the smallest effect of interest.
H2 (differential): leak frequency predicts the hidden-depth items more strongly than the explanatory-gap items ("even a complete physical account would leave out what it feels like"), because the gap intuition is carried by the universal opacity mechanism, the depth intuition by the leaks. Williams/Steiger test of dependent correlations.
H3 (secondary, book S116, `10-…` L177–181): death anxiety correlates positively with dualism. This is not an FMT discriminator — terror-management theory predicts it — but it tests a 2015 claim at no extra cost and supplies a competing predictor for H1.

**Rival predictions.** IIT (Albantakis et al., 2023) has no account of why the hard problem seems hard; nothing in Φ or cause–effect structure varies with phosphene frequency in a way that bears on belief — silent. GNW (Mashour et al., 2020) has no account of the intuition at all — silent. PP's meta-problem account (Clark, Friston & Wilkinson, 2019: mid-level estimates inferred with high precision present as raw givens) has no term for leaks — silent a priori; the natural retrofit is that unexplained low-level prediction errors inflate an "inexplicability" prior, which is permeability under another name. AST (Graziano & Webb, 2015) locates the intuition in the attention schema's omission of mechanism and predicts no relation to perceptual leaks; illusionism (Frankish, 2016) likewise. The live non-FMT rival is sociocognitive: anomalous experiences foster paranormal and dualist belief (Riekki, Lindeman & Lipsanen, 2013, for ontological confusions → dualism), and dualists then endorse mystery. FMT predicts a direct path from processing-artefact leaks to the hidden-depth intuition that survives controlling dualism, paranormal belief and generic anomalous experiences; the sociocognitive account predicts full mediation by belief.

**Procedure and materials.** One anonymous online questionnaire, ~25 min, framed as a "perception and beliefs about the mind" survey, scales in randomized order and never labelled.
1. Processing-Leak Inventory (new, ~14 items, past-year frequency 1–5): closed-eye flicker and coloured dots; visual snow in daylight; afterimages that intrude; noticing the blind spot or filling-in (preceded by a 30-second on-screen blind-spot demonstration so the item is anchored); hypnagogic images; hypnagogic sounds or voices; ringing or music in silence; words or faces assembling "late" or visibly; thoughts arriving before they are thoughts; Troxler fading. Exclude or flag migraine aura and diagnosed visual snow syndrome.
2. Mystery intuitions, two subscales: hidden depth (6 new items, e.g. "when I attend to my own mind I sense far more going on than I could ever reach") and explanatory gap (items from Díaz, 2021, and the "impossible for science to explain" items of Gottlieb & Lombrozo, 2018). Both subscales need a reliability check and a two-factor CFA; this is the only instrument-building step.
3. Covariates: O-LIFE Unusual Experiences (short), Tellegen absorption (short), a brief trait dissociation measure, Stanovich (1989) Dualism Scale (short), a paranormal-belief short form, religiosity, death anxiety (Templer DAS, 5–10 items), philosophy-of-mind exposure (3 items), meditation experience, a one-item sleep quality rating, Big-Five openness (10 items), age, field.
4. Funnel debrief: "What do you think this survey was testing?" — exclude anyone who names the leak–mystery link.

**Scoring and analysis.** Sum scores; ordinal omega reliability; CFA for the two mystery factors. Primary: regression of hidden depth on leaks with all covariates, robust SEs; report the partial r. H2 by Williams test. Exploratory mediation by dualism and paranormal belief, reported descriptively.

**Power (n ≈ 200, α = .05).** r = .20 → .81 two-tailed, .89 one-tailed; r = .25 → .95; r = .15 → .56 two-tailed, .68 one-tailed. Pre-register one-tailed for H1 (the derivation is directional). Partial correlation with ~10 covariates changes these numbers negligibly. Honest reading: the design decides r ≥ .20 and is inconclusive between .10 and .20.

**Falsifier for FMT.** Partial r ≤ 0 with a confidence interval excluding .15, or full mediation by dualism and paranormal belief. Either result says the leak mechanism of §3.8 contributes nothing measurable to the intuition; the opacity mechanism and self-referential closure are untouched, and §3.8 would have to be rewritten with one mechanism.

**Weaknesses and escape routes.** Cross-sectional; reverse causation is open (people who hold the intuition may attend to leaks — and §3.7.2 says attention recruits more of the visual system into the phosphenes). Common-method variance across self-report scales. A sample of scientists may show a restricted range on dualism and a polarized range on the hard problem; report distributions and a range-corrected estimate. Construct validity of the inventory (visual snow vs floaters vs migraine) needs the exclusion items. The causal complement already exists in the master and is equally cheap: run Prediction 6's leak arm (a 20-minute dark-room closed-eye session, pre/post hidden-depth ratings, active control a lit relaxation session) on a subset of ~60. Rival retrofits: PP absorbs through unexplained prediction errors; AST would have to say leaks make the schema's gaps salient; GNW and IIT would have to import a meta-problem account they do not currently have. None of them predicted it.

**Already run?** No study linking processing-artefact leaks to hard-problem intuitions was found. Adjacent: Díaz (2021) finds problem intuitions not widespread and driven by factors "unrelated to the nature of consciousness" — which is consistent with FMT (the leak mechanism is one such factor); Gottlieb & Lombrozo (2018) find science judged least able to explain phenomena tied to introspective access, exceptionalism and conscious will; Riekki et al. (2013) tie dualism to ontological confusions.

### Candidate 2 (rank 2). Imagery control loads with metacognitive efficiency, not with imagery vividness

**FMT derivation.** §3.5 (lines 399, 411): above the gate, consciousness is graded on two axes, richness of content and depth of recursion, and "richness of content varies along the other axis, independently of depth". §3.6.1 (line 448): "a system can be wide and shallow or narrow and deep". §4.2.3, scope caveat (line 615): the self-model's steering of the explicit world model (ESM-on-EWM) "is plausibly a higher-order capacity, presupposing sufficient recursive depth (Sections 3.4.6, 3.5) and partially trainable; … essentially untested." §9 OQ4 (line 1107): metacognitive efficiency (M-ratio) "indexes the explicit self-model's read-out of its own perceptual states". Book: D26 and D32 (`08-…` L77–78, L106, S62–63) define the doubly and triply extended rungs as observing the self-model and modelling the interaction with it; the continuum lies "in der Reichhaltigkeit des Selbstmodells … und in der Bandbreite der Beobachtung des Selbstmodells" (L121, S64) — richness and observation bandwidth, two quantities; lucid-dream control is treated as a trainable grip on content (`08-…` L171, S68; `09-…` L639, S98).

Put together: voluntary control of imagery content (transform, recolour, hold against competition, suppress) and metacognitive read-out of one's own perceptual states are both operations the ESM performs on explicit content — the depth axis. Imagery vividness is how richly the EWM renders generated content — the richness axis. FMT therefore predicts that, across people, imagery control correlates with perceptual metacognitive efficiency measured in an unrelated task, and does so more strongly than vividness does. It also predicts control correlates with trait self-observation (decentering) more than vividness does.

**Pre-registrable hypotheses.** H1: r(control, M-ratio) > 0. H2: r(control, M-ratio) − r(vividness, M-ratio) ≥ .20 (Williams/Steiger). H3: r(control, decentering) > r(vividness, decentering). Exploratory, from "wide and shallow or narrow and deep": r(working-memory span, M-ratio) ≈ 0 — exploratory because FMT's architectural independence of rank and depth does not entail statistical independence of their individual differences.

**Rival predictions.** GNW treats imagery, its manipulation and self-monitoring as workspace functions; the natural GNW expectation is a positive manifold with no differential — a differential pattern would be retrofitted as monitoring and manipulation sharing frontoparietal control circuitry while vividness depends on sensory cortex (plausible neuroscience, but not a GNW prediction). PP treats imagery as weak perception along one strength axis (Pearson, Rademaker & Tong, 2011; Dijkstra & Fleming, 2023) and has no control construct; the retrofit is that control and confidence both set higher-level precision. IIT is silent. HOSS/HOT make confidence a higher-order inference and say nothing about imagery control. Nobody predicted the differential in advance.

**Procedure and materials.** Online, ~35 min, phone or laptop.
1. Vividness: VVIQ (16 items).
2. Control: Gordon's Test of Visual Imagery Control (12 items) plus a 10-item transformation battery with keypress latency and self-rated success ("image a red apple on a table; make it green; rotate it; split it; now remove it and keep the table empty for 10 s").
3. Metacognitive efficiency: 2AFC Gabor-orientation or dot-numerosity task, ~250 trials, staircased to ~71 % correct, 4-point confidence after each response; meta-d′/d′ via HMeta-d or metadPy. Trial count is the floor for a usable M-ratio (Fleming & Lau, 2014).
4. Depth trait: Experiences Questionnaire decentering (11 items).
5. Exploratory: a two-block operation-span task for working memory.
6. Attention checks; funnel debrief.

**Scoring.** Control = mean of z(TVIC) and z(battery success); vividness = VVIQ; M-ratio log-transformed; Steiger's test for H2 and H3 with the shared variable M-ratio or decentering.

**Power (simulated, n = 200, dependent correlations).** If r(control, M) = .30, r(vividness, M) = .05 and r(control, vividness) = .40, power for H2 is .92; at .30/.10/.40 it is .75; at .25/.10/.40 it is .50. The design decides a differential of ≥ .20 and is inconclusive below it; the TVIC's reliability (α ≈ .69, several factors; Pérez-Fabello & Campos, 2004) caps the observable correlation, so apply Spearman–Brown disattenuation as a sensitivity analysis only.

**Falsifier for FMT.** Control correlates with M-ratio no more than vividness does (difference ≤ 0, CI excluding .20), or a single imagery factor absorbs both and carries all the covariance with M-ratio. Then FMT's placement of ESM-on-EWM control on the depth axis is wrong as stated, and §4.2.3's caveat would have to be retracted rather than left open.

**Weaknesses and escape routes.** Control is self-reported while M-ratio is behavioural, which biases H2 against FMT (method variance helps vividness–control, not control–M-ratio) — acceptable because it is conservative. VVIQ and TVIC correlate at r ≈ .3–.5 in the literature, so a strong single factor is a real possibility. What is already known and must be cited as consistent-with, not as support: metacognition about imagery is good and improves with training while imagery strength does not (Pearson, Rademaker & Tong, 2011; Rademaker & Pearson, 2012), and metacognitive efficiency shares variance across domains (Rouault et al., 2018, DOI not verified in this session). The new content is specifically that control, not vividness, travels with cross-domain metacognitive efficiency.

**Already run?** No study pairing an imagery-control measure with cross-domain M-ratio was found.

### Candidate 3 (rank 3). Drug-free redirection: salience beats bodily contingency under gazing-induced attenuation of self-input

This one is honest about its status: it is not a new prediction but a new, drug-free route to the master's redirection claim, generalized to any sufficient attenuation of self-referential input that does not generate competing internal imagery. It has the sharpest rival contrast of the three — PP predicts the opposite — and the largest execution risks.

**FMT derivation.** §6.0 (line 730): the ESM "will latch onto whatever input dominates when its normal self-referential input is disrupted". §6.1 (lines 761–765) and §8.3 (lines 979–987): identity content tracks the most salient content even without contingency with heartbeat, breathing or movement; test (ii) crosses salience with heartbeat synchrony. MG S349: psilocybin may not allow outward migration because internally generated content competes for salience — so a drug-free state must keep eyes open on an external salient object. Interpersonal and mirror gazing do that: ten minutes of eye-to-eye gazing in dim light produces strange-face apparitions, derealization, depersonalization and in some participants identity-level experiences, more strongly in dyads than before a mirror (Caputo, 2010, 2015, 2019). Book S252 (`17-…` L29): what belongs to the "Ich" is learned, not pre-programmed, with dreams of being an object as the example.

**Design.** Within-subject 2 × 2, screen-based partner in a dim room, 4 sessions of 10 min (or 2 sessions with two simultaneous streams). Stream A = live webcam image of the participant's own face (contingent with their movement); Stream B = pre-recorded stranger's face (non-contingent). Factor 1: which stream is salient (larger, brighter, moving vs small, dim, still). Factor 2: which is contingent (live own face vs stranger; optionally the faint stream pulsed in time with the participant's heartbeat via phone PPG, following Suzuki et al., 2013). The informative cells pair a salient non-contingent stream with a faint contingent one. Dependent variables: free report plus Caputo's items, scored by blinded raters into perceptual distortion vs identity migration ("I felt I was that face / that face was me"). Manipulation check before each session: a 2-minute enfacement procedure (synchronous stroking of own face and the stranger's on screen) to confirm contingency still drives self-attribution in this participant. Screen for phenomenological control (Lush et al., 2020) and exclude dissociative or psychotic history.

**Hypotheses.** H1: identity migration occurs more often toward the salient stream than toward the contingent one when they differ (FMT), against more often toward the contingent stream (precision-weighted self-attribution: Apps & Tsakiris, 2014). H2: identity migration toward a non-contingent stranger occurs at all above the no-salience baseline — any positive rate is a PP anomaly.

**Power.** McNemar on discordant pairs at n = 200: 25 % vs 10 % → .97; 20 % vs 10 % → .75; 15 % vs 8 % → .55. Caputo's identity-level effects run at roughly 20–30 % of participants, so the design is adequate only if the manipulation moves at least ten points.

**Falsifier for FMT.** Migration follows the contingent stream with the manipulation check passed; or no identity migration at all despite measurable dissociation — in which case redirection needs stronger self-input disruption than drug-free states afford and FMT retreats to the salvinorin version, which is a real loss of generality.

**Weaknesses and escape routes.** Mild state; strong demand characteristics (illusion reports track phenomenological control, Lush et al., 2020 — screen and covary); interpersonal awkwardness among colleagues (the screen version avoids dyads); the PP escape named in §8.3 (dissociation abolishes contingency inference, attention alone sets precision) is excluded only in sessions where the manipulation check passes. Not new as a prediction. Ethics heavier than the other two (see §4).

## 3. Candidates rejected and why

- Prediction 6 without its BCI sub-arm (fake generation display lowers mystery): entirely browser-runnable with colleagues, cheapest FMT test that exists — but already in the master (§8.7) and the map (F11). Run it; do not count it as new.
- Illusory-agency consolidation benefit (§4.2.2, line 587): already in the master, and already consistent with perceived-choice memory effects where choice had no real contingency.
- Pain unpleasantness–intensity gap in martial artists with mental training (§8.9, no-drug arm): exists in the master; needs pain induction.
- Individual-level qualia-structure alignment (§8.6): exists; the cheap half is shared with IIT, the decoding half needs brains.
- Richness  independent of  depth as a bare null across people: FMT's architectural independence does not entail statistical independence; a null is not a win and a correlation is not a loss.
- Working-memory span  independent of  M-ratio ("wide and shallow"): same problem; folded into Candidate 2 as exploratory.
- Subjective duration as processing volume per second: FMT's frame count is underdetermined for prospective vs retrospective judgments, and novelty-driven dilation is predicted in advance by storage-size and PP accumulation models alike.
- Self/world boundary via direct vs indirect feedback (the glass, `17-…` L142, S261): a contingency account PP shares (rubber hand); nothing cheap separates them.
- Reality monitoring by intensity, degraded by fatigue (`15-…` L268, S242): consistent with Dijkstra & Fleming (2023) and predicted by PRM.
- Closed-eye progression dots → geometric → figurative (`15-…` L321–323; §3.7.2): PP's relaxed priors and the sensory-deprivation literature predict the same order.
- "Lag", spectator feeling and autonomous inner voices under exhaustion (`17-…` L13, S250; `18-…` L35–39, S271–272): coarse version known (sleep loss → dissociation, Giesbrecht et al., 2007); the fine structure is 2015-native but no rival is committed either way, and the state is not inducible ethically.
- Presence-without-report in flow or absorption; Sperling "all letters seen" (§3.4.6): the overflow deadlock; needs no-report neural markers.
- Trainability of imagery control vs vividness: Rademaker & Pearson (2012) already show metacognition trainable and strength not; used as consistent-with in Candidate 2.
- Self-model richness predicts theory of mind (§6.4): simulation theory predicts it; a metacognition–mentalizing literature exists.
- Death anxiety → dualism (`10-…` L177–181, S116): terror-management theory predicts it; kept as a covariate in Candidate 1.
- Falsity-consolidation asymmetry (truth principle, `fmt-2015-definitions.md`): a good standalone belief-updating experiment, but epistemology, not a consciousness discriminator.
- The "Schwarz-Weiß-Bias" (non-linearly separable categories, `12-…` L286–288, S141–142): an explicit 2015 invitation to psychologists, cheap and runnable, not about consciousness.
- Trainable, cross-modal conscious frame rate (`17-…` L51–61; `09-…` L674–676): a supramodal temporal limit is also what GNW's central bottleneck and the temporal-g literature expect.
- Choice blindness, audience-dependent reasons, confabulated inference (`15-…` L339–345): Nisbett & Wilson and Johansson territory; everyone predicts it.
- Qualia richness scales with associative richness (`17-…` L172, S264): IIT predicts the same from relations in the cause–effect structure.
- Depth-gated mystery (triply extended operation, §3.5): no independent depth measure exists; circular.
- Ganzfeld redirection with heartbeat pulsing: merged into Candidate 3 (eyes-open gazing avoids the competing internal imagery MG flagged in S349).

## 4. Ethics and sample notes

- Power asymmetry. MG is a manager recruiting at his own employer. Participation must be anonymous, opt-in through a neutral channel, with no record of who took part, no employer benefit or penalty, and the study framed as personal research unless the company formally sponsors it. Do not run it on company infrastructure or store personal data in company systems (GDPR; works-council consultation is advisable before recruiting).
- Review. Candidates 1 and 2 are anonymous questionnaires and a non-invasive cognitive task; in most jurisdictions they are exempt or light-touch, but pre-registration platforms and journals will ask for a statement, and a university collaborator's committee is the clean route. Candidate 3 induces a dissociative state and needs committee review, exclusion of psychiatric and dissociative history, a trained person present, and a structured debrief.
- Sensitive items. Dissociation, death anxiety and hallucination-frequency items are mildly sensitive; provide a contact line and a skip option; make the visual-snow and migraine items clinically neutral and non-diagnostic.
- Demand characteristics. Use a neutral title, randomize scale order, never show scale names, separate the predictor and outcome blocks by a filler block, and run a funnel debrief; exclude participants who name the hypothesis. In Candidate 3 screen for phenomenological control and covary it.
- Sophistication and range. Scientists compress the range on dualism and may polarize on the hard problem; report distributions and a range-corrected estimate, and expect the hidden-depth subscale to carry more variance than the explanatory-gap subscale.
- Volunteer selection. 200 of 300 volunteers will be the more curious and open; this inflates absorption and openness means but does not bias within-sample correlations much. One employer limits generalizability, not validity.
- Pre-registration. OSF, with the hypotheses, smallest effects of interest and exclusion rules stated above; a one-tailed test is defensible for H1 of Candidate 1 because the derivation fixes the sign.
