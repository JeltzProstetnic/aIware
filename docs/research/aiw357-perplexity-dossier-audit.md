## Verdict

**No thesis was contradicted by evidence. Each one was weakened by one of three moves.**
1. **Inflate then refute.** The dossier restates a thesis more strongly than MG wrote it, refutes the
   stronger version, then offers MG's original wording as its "defensible reformulation". This happened
   in theses 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8 and 9.
2. **Novelty graded as weakness.** "Thin", "speculative" or "unsupported" wherever no study exists.
   MG: *"if all my wisdom was already known, the book would not be needed."*
3. **Citing MG's books as origin without engaging their arguments.** *No Outside*, the seed exchange
   (counterexamples and answers), calibration by ridicule, and the 2015 amygdala prediction were all
   supplied and none were used.

Part of this is **our own doing**:
- The brief asked for a single evidence scale (solid/contested/thin/speculative) and "support or
  refute with external literature".
- It headlined thesis 3 as "model everything".
- It opened thesis 8 with the implementability sentence, which merged theses 3 and 8.
- Our excerpt glosses introduced "a default of every brain" and "emergence non-causal".

Not ours: the fifth label "unsupported", grading inflated restatements, the invented "Libet" item, and
the claimed registry categories.

## Revised brief for any next research pass

1. **Two-axis verdict per thesis.** Literature: confirmed / contradicted / silent / not empirical.
   Argument: valid / premise contested / invalid. "Silent" is a finding, not a grade.
2. **Weaken only on contradiction.** Where the literature is silent, say what argument or test would
   carry the thesis. Never downgrade it.
3. **Per thesis: a canonical sentence plus a "what I do not claim" line.** The logic report has drafts
   for theses 2 and 5. Fix theses 3 and 8 so they are not merged.
4. **Engage my arguments, not only my conclusions.** Supply full *No Outside* chapters 1, 2, 4, 6 and 8
   for the logic theses.
5. **Test the stated claim, never a stronger restatement.**
6. Fix the package glosses (monocausality, emergence) and carry the 2026 truth definition. *No Outside*
   §6.2 revises the 2015 consensus theory of truth.
