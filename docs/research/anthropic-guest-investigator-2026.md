## Verdict

named anywhere. Our only possible entries are "causal human biology" or "clinical science" with a
disease-mechanism angle. The real blockers are eligibility and approval: the applicant needs a qualifying
institution and a named person there who approves participation. Neither MG's employer (Ivoclar, a for-profit)
nor an institute whose ZVR registration is still pending clearly qualifies.

## Eligibility (verbatim)
- Form: "You must be a researcher at an academic institution, government lab, or nonprofit research
  organization." Institution type is one of University / Government lab / Nonprofit research organization.
- Terms 2.2: researcher "in biology, chemistry, drug discovery, medicine, or clinical science who is employed
  by or affiliated with a university or other academic institution, government lab, or a nonprofit research
  organization. Researchers applying on behalf of a for-profit company are not eligible." The applicant also
  warrants employer consent and no conflict with "any obligation you owe to that employer".
- There is no co-PI concept. The single "designated lead" applies, and other people are listed as team members.
- Country exclusions do not affect Austria.

## Scope (form definitions)
- Causal human biology: "Using human genetics and patient data to work out which genes, cells, and pathways cause disease."
- Structural and computational molecular biology, medicinal chemistry, drug discovery.
- Clinical science: "Designing, running, and analyzing studies in patients, from trial design to biomarkers and endpoint selection."
- Selection criterion (b): "fit with the research mission of Anthropic's research group". Every application gets a biosecurity review.

## The application (Google Form, "under 30 minutes")
No CV, budget or letters. Fields:
- About you, plus a link to a lab page or publication list.
- Proposal, under six word limits:
  - the question (100 words)
  - why it matters (100 words)
  - why it needs Anthropic's models and harnesses, and what was tried without them (150 words)
  - the public data to be used and any independent validation (100 words)
  - the result after 8–12 weeks (100 words)
  - the most likely reason it fails (50 words)
- Team members with their hours per week.
- **Institutional approval**: the name and email of the person who approved. Anthropic contacts that person.
- Confirmations: the application is NOT confidential, and it may contain no unpublished results that the
  applicant does not want made public and no "unique approaches or methods".

## What we would get and give
- Anthropic's team runs its internal models and harnesses on the problem. **The applicant gets no model or
  compute access** (Terms 1.3), and no money.
- Only public data may be used, and the applicant sends no data.
- Outputs are released under CC BY 4.0 for papers and data and Apache 2.0 for code, with Anthropic researchers
  as co-authors. A preprint is due about 2–3 weeks after the project ends. Anthropic reviews any publication
  for 10 business days and may hold it on safety grounds.
- The applicant runs any IRB process and gives a monthly one-paragraph update.
