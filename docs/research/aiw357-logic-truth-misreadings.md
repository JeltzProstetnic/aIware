## Part 1 — Numbered findings

### 1. Thesis 5 refuted as universal dialetheism, a view MG rejects in three supplied places

- **Dossier.** D:207 *"I reject the universal literal claim. In a fixed classical interpretation, P∧¬P is
  not made true by changing the meaning of P, the observer, or the time; and the external philosophical
  alternative supports selected true contradictions, not unrestricted universal contradiction."*
  D:223 verdict *"unsupported as the universal literal claim."* D:689 risk #2 *"Every statement is literally
  both true and false … even dialetheism does not say that every proposition is a true contradiction."*
  D:533 *"universal dialetheism does not follow from perspective dependence."*
- **MG's source.** B:29 states the literal claim as frame-relativity, not dialetheism: *"sometimes literally,
  because truth is reference-system dependent … Distinguish the literal claim (frame-relativity) from the
  heuristic claim."* S:35, MG's verbatim answer to the self-application counterexample: *"NO fact is true
  together within it's opposite."* NOx:128 / NOf:614 *"A contradiction inside a single derivation is fatal:
  from a statement and its denial anything follows."* NOx:114 / NOf:460, on Priest: *"A perspective that
  accepted every contradiction it met would have given up the one instrument that could have told it
  anything about its own errors."*
- **The dossier concedes MG's actual literal claim in its own table** and then grades a different claim.
  D:200 *"Frame dependence … Different indexed predicates can both be true; no same-frame contradiction
  follows"* is MG's position. The verdict is written against the "Genuine dialetheia" row (D:204), which MG
  never occupied.
- Perplexity cites Priest (SEP) as *"the external philosophical alternative"* as if MG had not met it. NOx:114
  is MG positioning himself against Priest; it was in the package and is never mentioned.
- **Verdict: STRAWMAN. Severity: high.** It sets risk-table rank 2 and the thesis-5 verdict.
- **Brief-induced share.** The brief's headline *"Every statement can be true and false simultaneously"*
  (B:29) and its request to research *"dialetheism and paraconsistent logic (Priest)"* invited the test. But
  the same sentence told Perplexity what the literal claim was, and S:35 plus NOx:114 were supplied and
  unused, so the strawman is not excused by the brief.

### 2. The "fixed classical interpretation" yardstick answers a different question, and No Outside's argument is never engaged

- **Dossier.** D:207 (quoted above) makes "a fixed classical interpretation" the arbiter. D:223 adds
  *"contested as perspectival epistemology"* with no contesting argument anywhere in the section (Nietzsche
  gets a caution at D:219, Zhuangzi a nod at D:217). The only references to No Outside in the whole dossier
  are D:101 (patch error) and D:194 (*"the perspective argument in No Outside"* as origin). Sections 4.2, 4.3,
  6.2, 6.5, 8.5 and 8.6 were supplied (NOx:58–132) and none is discussed.
- **MG's source.** Within one frame No Outside *is* classical, so the yardstick itself is not contrary to the
  book: NOf:260 *"an inference is valid or invalid whatever a brain is disposed to make of it"*; NOf:614
  (finding 1). The tension is in the use. MG's literal claim is a claim *about* reference systems: NOx:124 /
  NOf:610 *"Absolute truth, in the sense used throughout this book, is truth from no perspective"*; NOx:98
  *"Truth in the strong sense, a match certified from no perspective, would need a standpoint outside all
  reference systems, and an embedded observer has none."* Testing a cross-frame claim inside a single fixed
  frame does not touch it, and treating the fixed frame as *the* arbiter presupposes the frame-free truth
  value that §8.5 denies.
- The specific position Perplexity should have met is NOx:96–108 (§6.2): no absolute truth, but absolute
  *falsity* relative to admitted input, and two senses of "absolute" kept apart. That is a narrower and more
  defensible thesis than "perspectivism", and it is the one the guide should carry.
- **Verdict: MISREADING (category error plus non-engagement). Severity: medium.** The "contested as
  perspectival epistemology" label is a label without an argument.

### 3. "Opposite" tested as logical negation, against the dossier's own distinction

- **Dossier.** D:207 tests MG's "opposite" as ¬P. D:209 itself says *"Reversing a slogan, changing a quantifier,
  and finding a counterexample are three different exercises"* and D:585 proposes entry 35 *"The opposite is
  not the negation."*
- **MG's source.** S:22 *"for every fact it's opposite must also be true in some way"*, from Taoism and martial
  arts; the Black Book's Yin–Yang material (B:38 *"contradictions have to be accepted"*). The Taoist opposite is
  a contrary (polar), not a contradictory. MG's answer at S:35 (*"NO fact is true together within it's
  opposite"*) is a classical statement about contradictories; the heuristic is about contraries.
- **Verdict: MISREADING (internal inconsistency). Severity: low.** The dossier supplies the right tool and
  then does not use it on its own refutation. Entry 35 is worth keeping; the P∧¬P refutation is not.

### 4. The seed exchange is cited as origin and then not used; MG's scope ruling and concessions are presented as the critic's repair

- **Dossier.** D:194 names *"the personal discipline documented in the unpublished seed exchange"* as origin.
  D:213 *"not a validation of the author's lifelong, all-propositions protocol."* D:207 *"The heuristic remains
  strong precisely when it is allowed to end with 'I could not find a defensible opposite under these
  conditions.'"* The thesis-5 book (D:583–590, entries 33–40) uses none of the seed material.
- **MG's source.** S:57 *"Scope ruling (MG): the discipline is a heuristic for learning to think, not (yet) a
  hard claim for No Outside."* S:55 *"subjective experience is really a hard case i have to admit."* S:35,
  answers 1–3: the principle and the law of non-contradiction are each other's opposite (self-application
  instantiates the principle at the meta level); an infinitesimal instant contains no experience; *"we could
  also be wrong about peano arithmetic no?"* (the opposite becomes true in another system, and the system's
  consistency is not certifiable from inside, which is No Outside's own thesis).
- "All-propositions protocol" is an accurate description of the heuristic as stated. What is misattributed is
  its status: MG delimited it as a heuristic and conceded a hard case; the dossier's "allowed to fail"
  recommendation is MG's own position returned to him as a correction.
- **Verdict: MISATTRIBUTION (status) plus omission. Severity: medium.** The three counterexamples and MG's
  answers are the strongest entry material for Book 5 and the proposed contents ignore them.

### 5. Patch error turned into a frame-switching / two-systems claim and told to supply what it already contains

- **Dossier.** D:101 *"No Outside supplies the 'patch error' challenge to saving a problematic inference by
  changing its frame."* D:117 *"the 'patch error' is productive as a diagnostic question: did I resolve the
  contradiction within the original commitments, or quietly replace them? It is not by itself a theorem
  refuting a mathematical foundation; the manuscript should provide a precise worked example, stating both
  systems and the rule allegedly broken, and invite a logician to assess that argument."* D:563, TOC entry 13
  *"The patch that changes the problem — Work one precise 'patch error' example with both frames visible."*
- **MG's source.** Patch error is about adding an *object* to a *formalism*, not about frames or inferences.
  NOx:30 / NOf:64 *"A formalism meets a case it cannot handle, and instead of revising the concepts
  underneath, someone adds an object that makes the case come out right. The textbook example is the
  epicycle."* NOx:32 / NOf:66 *"A patch consumes the residue."* NOf:561 explicitly separates the two tools the
  dossier conflates: *"A patch … is an object added to a formalism … A cut adds no object. It removes a
  connection."*
- **MG says the opposite of "refuting a mathematical foundation"**, in the excerpt supplied: NOx:46 / NOf:86
  *"Patched formalisms are usually consistent. What they lose is the ability to tell us what they describe"*;
  NOf:76 *"It is tempting to say that patches corrupt the mathematics. Mostly they leave it sound"*; NOf:78
  *"The damage happens in the mapping from formalism to world."* No theorem was claimed; the claim is
  about the reading, and the dossier refutes a theorem the text disclaims.
- **Worked examples were supplied.** NOx:30–46 contains epicycles, renormalization (Dirac, Feynman's
  "dippy process"), Lakatos's monster-barring, Duhem–Quine, Redhead's surplus structure, and Neptune versus
  Vulcan (*"The two moves were formally identical … One was a discovery and the other a patch"*). The full
  book adds Einstein's cosmological constant (NOf:37), the number ladder (NOf:106–134, negative → rational →
  real → complex, each an import with its own model), complex numbers (NOf:76) and Banach–Tarski (NOf:132).
  The demand for *"both systems and the rule allegedly broken"* shows Perplexity took patch error for a claim
  about two logical systems and a violated rule. It is not.
- The dossier's diagnostic question half-fits the Duhem–Quine auxiliary move but misses MG's actual
  operational criterion, also supplied: NOx:50 / NOf:92 *"admit a new mathematical object only if it predicts
  something new or is labelled as bookkeeping"* (Neptune predicted; Vulcan absorbed).
- **Verdict: MISREADING. Severity: high.** TOC entry 13 would have MG write a chapter about something other
  than his idea. **Brief-induced share:** the brief's compression *"must not be allowed to lead you away from
  logic — file 07 has my patch-error argument"* (B:26) is cryptic and can suggest a maths-versus-logic
  conflict; but the excerpt was attached and is unambiguous.

### 6. Reductionism attributed to an emergentist; "builds on" read as "deducible from"

- **Dossier.** D:103 *"Logicism, the attempt to reduce mathematics to logic, is a substantive and contested
  foundational programme, not the uncontested fact that mathematics simply follows from logic."* D:107
  overclaim-to-avoid *"All mathematical truth is deducible from pure logic."* D:113 *"Evidence for the crucial
  qualification: Anderson …"* D:115 *"I reject the hierarchy as either a complete epistemology or a
  demonstrated universal learning sequence; it confuses ontological dependence with deduction and
  instructional necessity."* D:121 *"I want a dependency map, not a ladder of intellectual castes."* D:123
  verdict *"contested as reductionism."*
- **MG's source.** B:26 says *"builds on"* five times (dependency), lists Anderson and emergence as things to
  research, and adds *"note my own book argues emergence."* D:101 itself records *"Die Emergenz des
  Bewusstseins supplies the accompanying commitment to emergence."* MG's view of the logic–mathematics
  relation in the supplied text is anti-logicist: NOx:82 (§4.4) *"The codification of logic was an induction"*;
  NOf:293 *"Several formal logics exist, they differ in what they refuse"*; E:197 (2015, truth no. 3)
  *"in der Algebra kann es durchaus sein, dass eins und eins null ergibt … je nachdem von welchem Zahlensystem
  und von welcher Algebra wir sprechen."* No Outside Chapter 2 (NOf:102 *"Every extension of the number system
  beyond this point imports something that counting does not supply"*) is an explicitly non-deductive account
  of how mathematics grows.
- The dossier's own *"Defensible reading"* column (D:107–111) is MG's reading. The deduction reading is
  Perplexity's, charged to MG as a confusion. "Ladder of intellectual castes" has no source in the brief.
- **Fair residue.** B:26 *"Whoever does not understand this layering and the basic building blocks of each
  layer has a world model standing on a weak foundation and becomes an inefficient thinker"* is a learning
  prescription, and *"thin as a universal prescription for efficient learning"* is an honest label for an
  untested claim — untested, not contradicted. Sala et al. 2019 (D:113) does not contradict it: it is about
  working-memory, chess and music training programmes, which the dossier concedes at D:115 (*"the transfer
  evidence is about identified training programmes, not all interdisciplinary education"*). "Transfer of
  learning" was on the brief's research list (B:26), so its presence is brief-induced; its use against thesis 2
  is not.
- **Verdict: MISATTRIBUTION. Severity: medium.** "Contested as reductionism" grades a position the brief
  disclaims.

### 7. "An unfamiliar mathematical result must violate common sense" listed as MG's overclaim

- **Dossier.** D:107, overclaim-to-avoid for Logic → mathematics: *"… or 'an unfamiliar mathematical result
  must violate common sense.'"* This glosses *"must not be allowed to lead you away from logic"* (B:26) as
  "distrust counterintuitive mathematics".
- **MG's source.** The direction is the reverse. NOx:16 (preface) *"A model written in equations can expose
  logic that intuition would never find on its own, and that is the reason the formalism is worth having."*
  NOf:37: Einstein *"patched them to agree with his intuition"* and the equations were right. MG's warning is
  against patching the formalism to rescue it or one's intuition, not against trusting it when it surprises.
- **Verdict: MISATTRIBUTION. Severity: low.** One table cell, but it shows "away from logic" was read as
  "away from common sense".

### 8. Wason cautions aimed at claims the 2015 text does not make

- **Dossier.** D:211 *"not a population estimate that almost nobody possesses logic."* D:532 *"Do not claim that
  every error is simple ignorance of modus tollens or that the small laboratory sample estimates humanity's
  rationality."*
- **MG's source.** E:121–125: *"Die meisten Menschen wählen die 'E'-Karte"*, *"Nur wenige Menschen denken aber
  daran, auch die '7'-Karte zu untersuchen"*, then modus tollens as the basis of falsification. That is a
  correct report of Wason's result (and of Johnson-Laird and Wason 1970, 5 of 128). Nothing about "almost
  nobody possesses logic" or "every error is ignorance of modus tollens".
- **Verdict: FAIR as a generic caution, MISATTRIBUTION if read as describing the source. Severity: low.**
  The exercise the dossier builds from it (D:211, entry 37) is good.

### 9. "Acht Wahrheiten": the one-line row is fair, but it prescribes distinctions the 2015 text already performs and warns against an inference it never draws

- **Dossier.** D:533 *"Retain the demand to expose frames, meanings, and burden of proof. Distinguish an asserted
  impossibility, absence of evidence, indexed truth, and genuine contradiction; universal dialetheism does not
  follow from perspective dependence."* Listed under D:521 *"… some are the author's proposed explanations, and
  some are examples of bad inference; the distinction should remain visible."*
- **MG's source.** E:181 *"Ich will damit nicht sagen, dass diese Sätze unwahr sind, aber ich werde … aufzeigen,
  dass Wahrheit relativ ist, und nicht absolut."* The eight dismantlings are exactly the dossier's categories:
  no. 1 semantic convention (E:189 *"Es handelt sich also um eine semantische Vereinbarung, und dies würde ich
  gerne von dem Begriff Wahrheit abtrennen"* — the dossier's "Lexical change" row, D:202, restates MG's own
  analysis); no. 2 indexed to who can see it (E:193); nos. 3–5 inductive fallibility (E:210 *"Man weiß nie, ob es
  auch beim Drölfzig Millionsten Versuch noch immer klappt"*); no. 6 open physics (E:214); no. 7 the sceptical
  hypothesis (E:216); no. 8 burden of proof on "there is no X" (E:222). Never "true and false simultaneously";
  no dialetheism, universal or otherwise.
- **Verdict: FAIR as written, with a low-severity MISREADING edge** (correction of a step not taken; the
  source is the worked example of the distinctions the dossier asks for). The dossier's book 5 could cite the
  eight truths as eight *kinds* of frame change — that is what they are.
- **What the dossier missed, and a logician reviewer would not.** The 2015 chapter ends in a consensus theory
  of truth: E:238 *"Wahres Wissen ist … ein Wissen, das von der Mehrheit der Menschen … auf dieselbe Art
  interpretiert wird"*; E:240 *"Konvention von Wahrheit"*; E:248 the Austrian-liar paradox gets *"ungefähr
  50%"*. No Outside 2026 revises this explicitly: NOx:104 / NOf:422 *"falsity relative to admitted input is not
  a consensus property. The flat earth was not true while most people held it and false once they stopped."*
  The brief cites both works as one lineage (B:29); the dossier treats them as one. The guide has to carry
  the 2026 position (no absolute truth; absolute falsity relative to admitted input; "truth by convention" as
  the 2015 step that was superseded). This is an omission, not a misreading, and it is the most useful thing
  the logician review (D:717) will say.

## Part 3 — The methodology pattern and a revised brief

### What happened

The dossier grades theses on a single evidential-strength scale (D:11: *speculative* = *"a proposed mechanism,
universal claim, or literary interpretation outruns what has been tested"*). On that scale an original claim
scores "thin" or "speculative" by definition, because no external literature has tested it yet. MG's objection
("if all my wisdom was already known, the book would not be needed") is correct about the scale.

In this scope the pattern shows four times: thesis 2 *"thin as a universal prescription"* (untested, no
contrary evidence offered); thesis 5 *"thin direct evidence for the full training discipline"* (nobody has
tested MG's discipline, which is the point of writing it up); thesis 5 *"unsupported as the universal literal
claim"* (the strawman of finding 1); thesis 2 *"contested as reductionism"* (a position not held).

### How much is brief-induced

Mostly the scale, not the strawmen.

- Brief-induced: B:7 *"Support or refute each thesis with external literature"*; B:23 *"Separate 'evidence' from
  'speculation' and mark where the science is thin"*; B:49 the four-label verdict *"on evidential strength"*;
  B:63 *"Never present my theses as established."* All nine theses were put on one empirical scale, including
  theses 2 and 5, which are philosophical and logical claims where the currency is argument, counterexample and
  precedent, not effect sizes. Perplexity applied the scale it was given.
- Partly brief-induced: B:7 *"my books … are not independent evidence"* can be read as "do not engage their
  arguments". That is the likeliest reason No Outside §6.2, §6.5, §8.6 and the seed answers were cited as origin
  and never discussed. "Not evidence" and "not argument" needed separating.
- Not brief-induced: adding the label *"unsupported"*; grading restated claims instead of stated ones (ledger
  rows a–d); collapsing "supporting evidence" and "strongest counter-evidence" (which the brief asked for
  separately, B:23) into one label that does not say whether the literature is silent or contrary.

Fair to Perplexity: for theses 1 and 4 (empirical) the scale works and the dossier's handling is careful. The
damage is concentrated where the scale does not fit.

### Proposal for the revised brief (replace B:7, B:23 and B:49)

1. **Two-axis verdict instead of one label.** For each thesis report (i) *literature status*: confirmed /
   contradicted / silent / not an empirical claim; and (ii) *argument status*: valid with premises accepted /
   valid with premises contested (name the premise) / invalid (name the step) / not assessed. "Silent" is not
   a defect; write it as *"novel — proposed test: …"*. Use no label the brief does not define.
2. **One canonical sentence per thesis, plus a "what I do not claim" line, written by MG.** Draft for the two in
   scope:
   - *Thesis 2.* Claim: each science depends on the primitives of the one below it (logic → mathematics →
     physics → chemistry → biology); a thinker who lacks those primitives has a weak world model. Not claimed:
     reduction or deducibility — my own book argues emergence. The patch-error argument (No Outside §1.3–1.5):
     a formalism rescued by adding an object usually stays consistent but loses its reading; the test is whether
     the added object predicts something new (Neptune) or only absorbs the discrepancy (Vulcan).
   - *Thesis 5.* Literal claim: a statement's truth value is relative to a reference system; there is no truth
     from no perspective, but there is falsity relative to admitted input (No Outside §6.2, §8.5). Not claimed:
     P and not-P within one system (No Outside §8.6; seed answer 1). Heuristic claim: for every statement, search
     for the sense — frame, scale, time, meaning, quantifier — in which its contrary holds; the search may fail
     (seed scope ruling). "Opposite" means contrary, not negation.
3. **Test the claim as qualified.** *"Test the thesis as I state it in the canonical sentence. If you think my
   headline wording overstates it, say so in a separate editorial note; do not grade the headline."*
4. **Engage my arguments, not only my conclusions.** *"My books are not evidence for my theses, but they contain
   arguments. Where my supplied text already answers an objection you raise (e.g. No Outside §6.5 on Priest,
   seed answer 1 on self-application), quote that answer and assess it. Where you disagree with a thesis,
   quote the step in my argument you reject."*
5. **Weaken only on contradiction.** *"Recommend weakening or dropping a thesis only where you cite evidence or a
   valid argument that contradicts it. 'No literature found' is a reason to propose a test, not to weaken."*
6. **Right currency per thesis.** *"Theses 2 and 5 are philosophical and logical. Assess them by argument,
   counterexample and precedent; cite empirical studies only where a premise is empirical."*
7. **Supply the full chapters for theses 2 and 5.** The "light use" excerpt of No Outside was enough to avoid the
   misreadings in findings 1, 2 and 5 and was still misread; the logician review (D:717) needs Chapters 1, 2, 4,
   6 and 8 in full, plus the seed file, and should receive item 2 above verbatim.
