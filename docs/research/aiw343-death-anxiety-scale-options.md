# AIW-343: free death-anxiety measures to replace the Templer DAS

Researched 2026-10-28. Constraint: no cost, non-commercial academic online survey, English and possibly German.
"Verified" means I opened the source document myself (curl/PDF text or Wayback snapshot), not just a search snippet.

## Verdict

Use the **DAP-R Fear of Death subscale** (7 items). It is the only candidate that has both of these: an author statement that the scale is free for research use, and a validated German translation published under CC BY 4.0 with Wong as co-author.
The **DABBS Affect subscale** (4 items, or the full 18 items) comes second. Its English text is openly licensed, but the ND clause rules out a German translation unless the author gives permission.

## Candidates

### 1. Death Attitude Profile-Revised (DAP-R), Fear of Death subscale (Wong, Reker & Gesser 1994)

- **Items:** 7 Fear of Death items (1, 2, 7, 18, 20, 21, 32) out of 32 in the full DAP-R. The subscale can be given on its own, and the full instrument is not needed.
- **Format:** 7-point agree/disagree scale (SA…SD). The anchor polarity is randomised per item in the original. Subscale score = mean of its items.
- **Reliability/validity:** Original Fear of Death α = .86. For the whole DAP-R, 4-week test-retest ranged from .61 to .95 across subscales (Wong et al. chapter, verified). German DAP-GR Fear of Death α = .82 (N = 216 medical students). Fear of Death correlates .38 with Death Avoidance and −.39 with Neutral Acceptance. The German CFA "slightly diverged from the original", and the German norms come from a small student sample.
- **Languages:** Validated in many languages. **German: DAP-GR**, Jansen et al. 2019, *BMC Psychology* 7:61, doi:10.1186/s40359-019-0336-6. Table 3 prints all 32 items in English and German. Fear of Death items, verified from the article:
  1. Death is no doubt a grim experience. / Der Tod ist zweifellos eine grauenvolle Erfahrung.
  2. The prospect of my own death arouses anxiety in me. / Die Aussicht auf meinen eigenen Tod verursacht mir Angst.
  7. I am disturbed by the finality of death. / Die Endgültigkeit des Todes verstört mich.
  18. I have an intense fear of death. / Ich habe große Angst vor dem Tod.
  20. The subject of life after death troubles me greatly. / Das Thema Leben nach dem Tod beunruhigt mich sehr.
  21. The fact that death will mean the end of everything as I know it frightens me. / Die Tatsache, dass der Tod das Ende von allem, wie ich es kenne, bedeuten wird macht mir Angst.
  32. The uncertainty of not knowing what happens after death worries me. / Die Ungewissheit, über das was nach dem Tod passiert, beunruhigt mich.
- **Licence: EXPLICITLY FREE (verified).**
  - Wong's assessments page says, verbatim: *"They are free for psychologists to use for research and clinical purposes."* The page lists the DAP-R among those measures. The live page has an expired TLS certificate, so I read it from the Wayback snapshot: https://web.archive.org/web/2025/https://www.drpaulwong.com/meaning-therapy-assessments-and-interventions/
  - Wong's research page (live, verified) refers to *"his past open science practice"* and says the measures will be *"freely available for all researchers and practitioners"*: http://www.drpaulwong.com/research/
  - Wong hosts the instrument PDF himself (marked © Wong & Reker 1994): http://www.drpaulwong.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/03/Death-Attitude-Profile-Revised-DAP-R-Wong-Reker-1994-Scale.pdf
  - The German version and the English item text are reprinted in the DAP-GR article, which is **CC BY 4.0** (verified from the article and Crossref). Wong is a co-author.
  - Small caveat: the free-use sentence says "psychologists". For an academic research use the intent is clearly covered, but the wording is literal. The 1994 chapter itself appeared in a Taylor & Francis handbook (Neimeyer, *Death Anxiety Handbook*). The author's free-use statement and the CC BY reprint are what make the items usable.

### 2. Death Anxiety Beliefs and Behaviours Scale (DABBS) (Menzies, Sharpe & Dar-Nimrod 2022)

- **Items:** 18 items: Affect 4, Beliefs 7, avoidance Behaviours 7. The **Affect subscale** ("I feel anxious about death", "The fact that I will die someday is terrifying", "I am scared of dying", "Death frightens me") is a very short core death-anxiety measure.
- **Format:** 5-point scales with different anchors per section: agreement (Affect), frequency of the thought (Beliefs), frequency of avoidance (Behaviours).
- **Reliability/validity (verified from the article):** Total α = .90, 3-week test-retest .86 (N = 400/364). Affect α = .94. It converges with Templer DAS, Death Concern Scale and the Conte DAQ. It separates clinically significant death anxiety from non-clinical levels. Samples were Australian and UK, community and treatment-seeking, recruited online via Prolific, which is close to the AIW-343 setting.
- **Languages:** Turkish, Persian, Korean and Romanian validations exist. **I found no German version.**
- **Licence: OPEN, CC BY-NC-ND 4.0 (verified).** The article is open access under http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-nd/4.0/ (licence link embedded in the PDF and confirmed via Crossref), doi:10.1111/bjc.12387. Table 3 prints all items. Menzies' own site hosts the questionnaire: https://guppy-copper-j5ed.squarespace.com/s/DABBS-measure.pdf. The OSF project is https://osf.io/ay36v/ (node licence: none set).
  - Verbatim use in a non-commercial survey, with citation, is allowed, and so is using one subscale ("in whole or in part").
  - **A German translation counts as "Adapted Material" under CC 4.0, and ND forbids sharing it.** German use therefore needs the author's permission.

### 3. Collett-Lester Fear of Death Scale, revised (CL-FODS-R) (Lester & Abdel-Khalek 2003)

- **Items/format:** 28 items in four 7-item subscales (own death, own dying, death of others, dying of others), 5-point scale (1 = not, 5 = very).
- **Reliability:** Subscale α .81–.84 in the French validation (Cuniah et al. 2021, *Front Psychol* 12:736171, CC BY, verified). The factor structure is known to be unstable: five items did not load in the French data.
- **German:** I found no German validation.
- **Licence: LIKELY FREE, NOT FULLY VERIFIED.** MedEdPORTAL's "Critical Synthesis Package: Collett-Lester Fear of Death Scale" (Rosenberg 2013, doi:10.15766/mep_2374-8265.9572) contains *"a copy of the CL-FODS instrument and the scoring instructions developed by David Lester"*. The package is published under *"Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-Share Alike"*. I verified this from a Wayback snapshot: http://web.archive.org/web/20180601223754/https://www.mededportal.org/publication/9572/. The live site returns 403 to curl. Two open points: the copyright line names Rosenberg rather than Lester, and the package is the original version, not necessarily the 2003 revision. No explicit statement from Lester was found.

## Ranked recommendation

1. **DAP-R Fear of Death subscale (7 items).** It is the only option with an explicit free-for-research statement from the author and a validated, CC BY-licensed German version, so the English and German arms use matched items.
   **Action:** none. Cite Wong, Reker & Gesser (1994) and Jansen et al. (2019, doi:10.1186/s40359-019-0336-6, CC BY 4.0), and take the item text from DAP-GR Table 3. Optionally, a courtesy email to the DAP-GR corresponding author confirming subscale-only use; this is not required.
2. **DABBS, Affect subscale (4 items) or full 18 items.** It has the strongest modern psychometrics and was validated in Prolific online samples, but the ND licence covers only the verbatim English text.
   **Action:** for English only, none (CC BY-NC-ND 4.0, doi:10.1111/bjc.12387, cite and do not modify). For German, email Rachel Menzies (Menzies Anxiety Centre / University of Sydney) for permission to translate. There is no validated German version, so a translation would also need its own back-translation and validation.

Two other points:
- Item 20 of the DAP-R ("life after death troubles me") brings in afterlife content. If AIW-343 measures beliefs about an afterlife or about consciousness continuing, check that this item does not overlap with the study's own outcome items.
- If both languages are run, DAP-R is the only option here that does not leave the German arm on an unvalidated ad-hoc translation.
