# Phenomenology and the Cognitive Sciences — submission facts (verified S348, 2026-10-21, official Springer pages via headless Chromium)

Sources: link.springer.com/journal/11097/submission-guidelines, /how-to-publish-with-us, /ethics-and-disclosures, /aims-and-scope; Springer journal policies (preprint sharing); editorialmanager.com/phen.

- **System:** Editorial Manager, https://www.editorialmanager.com/phen — account required. ⚠ On 2026-10-21 the EM start page showed the banner *"Important Message: Site under development. Do not use for live manuscript submission."* Check it is gone (or ask the editorial office) before submitting.
- **Review:** double-blind ("double-anonymous"). Author anonymises the manuscript and all files; **separate title page** with title, names, affiliations, corresponding author's contact; acknowledgements, disclosures and funding also go on the title page.
- **Self-citation:** "avoid citing their own work in a way that could reveal their identity." Unlike Synthese there is no instruction to cite in the third person by name, and none to use "[Author]"; third-person citation that does not reveal authorship meets the rule.
- **Abstract** 150–250 words, no undefined abbreviations or unspecified references. **Keywords 4–6.**
- **Length:** average submission ~9,000 words **including references**; over 10,000 words discouraged.
- **Headings:** no more than three levels. Footnotes, not endnotes.
- **Citation style:** name–year in parentheses with a comma, e.g. (Thompson, 1990); "&" between two authors; APA 7 reference list, alphabetical, journal and book titles italic, full DOI links.
- **Declarations:** a "Statements and Declarations" / "Declarations" section on the separate title page: Funding, Competing interests, Ethics approval, Consent, Data/Materials/Code availability, Authors' contributions. Submissions without the relevant declarations are returned as incomplete. Funding is also entered in EM at submission. Research data policy type 1 (deposit encouraged, not required).
- **LLM use:** LLMs cannot be authors; use must be documented in the Methods section or a suitable alternative part of the manuscript. AI-assisted copy editing (readability, grammar, style) need not be declared.
- **Files:** editable source required at every submission — Word (.docx/.doc) or LaTeX (Springer Nature template recommended for maths). Missing source files mean the article is not considered for review. Plain font, automatic page numbering.
- **Cover letter:** required only to disclose re-use of material or unpublished material (e.g. in-press manuscripts). Reviewers may be suggested or excluded (suggested reviewers need institutional email or verifiable ID).
- **Fees:** hybrid. Subscription route free. OA APC £2,390 / US$3,290 / €2,690 (+VAT), choice made after acceptance.
- **Preprint:** Springer Nature encourages preprints; posting is not prior publication. Disclose preprint DOI and licence at submission.
- **UNVERIFIED:** exact article-type labels in EM (the EM page needs a login); whether EM asks for the preprint DOI in a dedicated field.
