## 5. Commentary outline

**Thesis: keep the idea, change the wording.** Proposed form:
*Recurrency becomes consciousness-relevant when the loop re-conditions a model of the world that
contains a model of the system itself, and that self-model's output changes the system's next state.*

Why: (1) "closes" will be read as their *causal closure*, whose depth is a scalar, the very measure we
argue cannot discriminate; (2) the thesis must name *what circulates*; (3) the last clause makes it
testable by their own §3.4.4 perturbational test and matches the FMT master's Putnam reply (feedback
without counterfactual difference is no implementation); (4) "consciousness-relevant" claims neither
sufficiency nor derivation.

**Move 1. Their open criterion is already about modelling.** Their test for a deep loop (quotes 3–4)
asks what the loop does: it sustains perceptual hypotheses, holds representations decoupled from the
stimulus, runs offline simulation. A long-memory feedback reservoir can be deep in their glossary's sense
while modelling nothing. *(This is our argument, and it needs a citation or a worked example. None has been
verified yet.)* Ask the plain question: is the depth measure doing the work, or is the content
of the loop doing it? Then offer the thesis as one candidate answer to the question they pose in quote 2.

**Move 2. Repair the unfolding-argument reply (§3.3.2).** Read as a claim about function space, quote 8 contradicts premise 2 (quote 5),
which they accept, and its citation concerns trainability. Acknowledge both charitable readings: budget-relative,
and closed-loop with an unbounded horizon. Repair the sentence using their own two arguments (quotes 6–7), and add
the independent 2026 replies (compact domains, Basri et al.; plasticity, O'Reilly-Shah et al.).
This point can be checked by any reader, whatever they think of FMT. Keep it to one paragraph.

**Move 3. Self-modelling as a dimension, not a fifth level.** Box 3 grounds the "I" in body loops. Those
loops supply inputs that a self-model uses, but nothing in Box 3 or in the four levels requires the system
to represent itself. Self-directed modelling appears in Table 1 as two entries among nine. Propose
self-modelling as a dimension that cuts across the four levels: the loop at any level may or may not carry a model
of the system. That framing is consistent with the heartbeat-evoked-response work they cite and avoids a box-and-arrow
reading (misconception #1).

**Box 4 against MG's own SNN.** By Box 4's logic, MG's GPU-run spiking network only simulates
recurrence. Do not mention that network. Ask about their framework instead: Box 4 judges by hardware, while
§3.4.4 and Table 2's "Artificial systems" row judge by perturbational and counterfactual tests, which a
simulated network can pass or fail. Which criterion governs? Our thesis sits at the level of the
implemented state, which fits their claimed multiple realisability. Cite the in-silico companion, if at all,
for loop-ablation *methods*, never as evidence about consciousness. aIware has confirmed none of crucible's
results, and crucible has withdrawn four it had filed here (`AIW-232`).

**Missing criticality** (Box 4's "dynamical regime" is never specified, quote 11). ⚠ The assessment's line
"FMT's criticality signature fits that placeholder" predates S329 and must not be used. At most one clause:
the regime FMT names is open-ended modelling, and near-critical dynamics are a signature some brains show
while doing it (Wilting & Priesemann 2019). On a three-page budget, dropping the point is defensible.

**⛔ Claims the commentary must not make**
- that closing the loop derives phenomenality or bridges the explanatory gap (they made the matching concession, quote 13);
- that recurrence computes what feedforward cannot, in function space;
- priority on causal closure (they cite NTIC and integrated-information decomposition), or "FMT already said this" about the efficiency argument;
- that their biological detail is a mistake;
- that they missed self-modelling (Table 1; "offline simulation", quote 4);
- that criticality is required, or that the regime *is* criticality, or any criticality↔consciousness implication;
- "four models"/four modules: say two kinds of model along two dimensions;
- that MG's SNN or the crucible robot is conscious, or that crucible results confirm FMT;
- Wolfram, Metzinger or Class-4 cellular-automaton vocabulary (neuro venue, name-drop advisory);
- "samples", "gated by" (banned framing), or any sentence narrating the commentary's own honesty or moves.

Cite FMT by Zenodo concept DOI only: no PsyArXiv, no RIM.
