# Synthese — submission facts (verified S347, 2026-10-20, official Springer pages via headless Chromium)

Sources: link.springer.com/journal/11229/submission-guidelines, /how-to-publish-with-us, /ethics-and-disclosures; editorialmanager.com/synt.

- **System:** Editorial Manager, https://www.editorialmanager.com/synt — account required.
- **Review:** double-anonymized. Author anonymizes manuscript; **separate title page** with names, affiliations, contact, acknowledgements, disclosures, funding.
- **Self-citation:** in the **third person by name**, e.g. "It was earlier discussed in Morganton [2013]" — NOT "[Author]".
- **Abstract** 150–250 words, no undefined abbreviations or unspecified references. **Keywords 4–6.**
- **Length:** no limit; typically 15–30 printed pages.
- **Declarations:** competing interests mandatory (else returned as incomplete); funding at submission; data policy type 1 (encouraged).
- **LLM use:** document in the Methods section (we use a "Declaration of AI use" section); AI-assisted copy editing need not be declared.
- **Files:** LaTeX source + compiled PDF, or Word. Not format-free (name-year citations, APA-7-influenced references).
- **Cover letter:** only for re-use of material / unpublished material.
- **Fees:** hybrid; subscription route free; OA APC €2,790 — choice offered after acceptance.
- **UNVERIFIED:** preprint policy (no Synthese-specific statement found; Springer generally permits preprints), exact article-type label in EM.
