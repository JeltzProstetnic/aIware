# v15

Supersedes v14 (2026-08-06). Two kinds of change: a substantial theory expansion in §4.2,
and a systematic accuracy pass over the paper's citations that found — and repaired — a class
of defect the previous versions carried.

## The accuracy pass, and why it matters

Every citation in the paper was checked against its primary source, asking not "does this work
exist" but "does it say what it is cited for". The existence question was already gated: the works
exist, the bibliographic details are right, the keys resolve. **Nothing had ever checked
characterization.** Sixteen findings resulted, eleven confirmed against primaries, and every one
failed in the same direction — toward more support than the source provides. All are repaired here.

The three most serious:

- A **quotation attributed verbatim to Hohwy & Seth (2020)** did not appear in that paper. The
  substance of the sentence was defensible; the quotation was not. It now carries their genuine
  wording.
- **Pinto et al. (2017)** was presented as *finding* that each hemisphere retains a functionally
  complete conscious agent. Their stated conclusion is the opposite — that callosotomy "does not
  create two independent conscious perceivers within one brain". The theory's reinterpretation of
  their data stands, but it is now presented as the theory's, explicitly against those authors'
  conclusion, rather than as their result.
- Two quantitative attributions to **Schartner et al. (2017)** — a "15-20%" complexity increase and
  "dose-response curves" — are absent from that study, which used a single psychoactive dose per drug
  and reports no such curve. This was the paper's own *quantitative grounding* paragraph for the
  permeability parameter, and it now states what the study reports and what remains to be done.

Also repaired: both species in the criticality row of the FMT-operations table were wrong (the cited
works are rat preparations, not turtle and cat), which left the row's own "not mammal-specific" claim
unsupported — now sourced to Shew et al. (2015), turtle visual cortex, and Hahn et al. (2010),
anesthetized cat; a group-level alignment result described as cross-individual, in three places,
including Prediction 5's claim of prior demonstration; a DMT study cited as psilocybin evidence; a
correlational imagery result reported as causal, on the paper's anti-epiphenomenalism claim; a
single-patient case study carrying a universal "requires"; a citation resolving to no reference
entry; statistics attributed to papers that do not contain them; a motor-learning dissociation that
contrasts two *implicit* systems cited for the implicit/explicit axis; and focal-seizure evidence
deployed inside claims about generalized seizures, now scoped, with the extrapolation stated as the
theory's own.

Four references were added and three, no longer cited, removed. Every replacement was verified
against a primary record before insertion.

## §8.9 — four repairs to the banked results, and a scope clause

- An **over-claim**: "the advantage grows by roughly an order of magnitude for each additional
  subsystem" was false on its own three numbers — the steps are elevenfold and then under threefold.
- An **under-claim** in the same paragraph: a scope clause read "one content condition" onto a result
  measured in four, and implied a number of cost-curve crossings the data does not support.
- An **arithmetic inconsistency** that turned out to be two units in one parenthesis: the endpoints
  are R², while the paired statistic beside them is a reduction in normalized error. Both figures are
  correct; the sentence now says which is which.
- The seventh banked result described its loop arm as a **bottleneck**. Every such arm was in fact
  simulated as the materialised dense product of its factors. The storage claim is unaffected — the
  factored bill is the right quantity for what an operator costs to store — but the dynamical
  description was wrong and now reads as what was actually run.

The eighth banked result gains the scope clause it was missing. Its spiking campaign ran throughout
without a synaptic filter, which the underlying work named as its most consequential untested
assumption. A sweep over three of the four supports has since run. At the substrate's decided synaptic
time constant the result reproduces unchanged, rival cost margins included. At the longest constant
tested the achievable ceiling falls below the fixed criterion in two of those three supports, where no
loop arm reaches it at any budget, so the comparison is not adjudicable there; in the third every loop
arm still reaches, at unchanged cost. The boundary between the two lies in an interval the sweep did not
resolve. One difference is stated rather than glossed: the paper's near-ceiling bar is *relative*, at
90% of the achievable ceiling, while the criterion held fixed across the sweep is *absolute*.

## §6.0 — source distinguishability, as a requirement and not a mechanism

Forking the self-model raises a question the previous derivation did not answer: a forked run and a
live one are computed by the same models in the same format, so nothing in the theory made them
discernible from within. §6.0 now states the constraint that follows — composing the explicit models
must not destroy the source distinguishability the pathways already carry — and states it as a
requirement rather than a mechanism.

That restraint is the substance of the change, not a hedge around it. A discrete source marker
affixed to internally generated states is the position the source-monitoring literature defined
itself against in 1993, and the nearest modern candidate has been tested and rejected. The paper
therefore claims no such marker. It concedes in its own voice that the empirical literature supplies
no mechanism, and states the finding that sharpens the problem rather than solving it: perception
carries a bottom-up drive that imagery lacks, and reality judgements do not appear to consult it. The
theory's contribution is an ordering — under perturbation, source attribution should degrade before
phenomenal experience does — with dreaming the everyday instance and lucid dreaming the recovery
case. Five references were added, all verified.

## Vocabulary: "closure", not "return"

Where the paper used "return" for what the theory calls closure, it now says closure, and where a
sentence needed the mechanism rather than the property it says what feeds back into what. Four
occurrences are deliberately unchanged: *return-free*, *returning component*, *non-return* and
*return-freedom* are graph-theoretic terms of art, and the sixth banked result is priced on the
distinction between them — opening the global loop costs 14.0% and 12.0% and *relocates* closure,
while return-freedom, meaning no closed walk anywhere, costs 74.0% and 71.5%. Naming both "closure"
would leave the 5.3× and 6.0× multipliers describing nothing.

## §6.3 — a case study is now called one

Dresler et al. (2012) was cited as having *shown* increased prefrontal and frontolateral activation
in lucid REM sleep. That paper's own title calls it a case study, and it is now cited as one, with
what a case study cannot establish stated alongside what it reported. The companion hedge on the
frontal gamma result was made in §8.5 in the previous pass; this completes it.

## §3.4.4 — subjective temporal order, stated for the first time

The paper already argued that the felt "now" and temporal *flow* are constructions of the explicit
layer. It stopped short of temporal **order**, and that claim is now made: the felt sequence of
events is constituted at the explicit layer, not read off the order in which signals reach the
substrate. The postdiction literature is the ground — colour phi, the cutaneous rabbit, the flash-lag
effect, and Libet et al.'s (1979) backward referral of *sensory* timing, which is a different result
from the volitional-timing work the paper already cited and is introduced as such. Dennett and
Kinsbourne's Stalinesque/Orwellian indeterminacy supplies the moral: the question presupposes a
substrate-level finish line that does not exist.

The second half is the part that is not merely a gap being filled. The construction supplies *which*
order its contents carry, but not the direction that order runs. The explicit models are implemented
in the coarse-grained regime in which the thermodynamic arrow is defined, so the cognitive arrow is
**inherited** from the thermodynamic one rather than built alongside it — a containment claim, not a
correspondence claim. There are not two arrows requiring alignment; there is one, and the modeller is
inside the layer that has it.

## §7 — theory of mind is not a rung on the ladder

The *erweitert* ladder counts self-recursion, with no other agent anywhere in the chain, and §7 was
grading theory of mind on it — the table row's "including theory of mind" read as ToM being a
*component* of the doubly-extended rung. The theory never held that. Modeling another agent is a
**world-model** operation: the explicit world model is a general-purpose modeller and the explicit
self-model supplies a rich template to instantiate into it, so the theory posits no dedicated
mechanism for theory of mind because two mechanisms it already posits deliver it. Both sites now say
so, and the species gradient is stated as the correlation it is. The theory is unchanged; the wording
that invited the misreading is gone.

## §4.2 — the theory expansion

The free-will treatment gains a mechanism for the *phenomenology* it previously left unexplained:
a decision presents not as uncaused but as originating in the self, with nothing behind the self,
and felt freedom is what an origin feels like from inside. The account is scoped so it composes with
the paper's existing position that whether the system *is* free is a question for physics — it holds
whichever way the physics comes out, and is therefore not evidence for either answer.

§4.2.3 is re-cut around two routes distinguished by the timescale of their effect rather than by
direction, since both run outward to the substrate first. The readout function and the causal power
are separated: reading a quale is a dashboard, not causal power; causal power begins where the
readout triggers associated simulation.

## Criticality is an effect, and the paper now says so consistently

The paper had two readings of what criticality is to the theory. The abstract and §3.7's heading called
it the **signature** of the underlying requirement; the Introduction called it a **prerequisite** — the
requirement itself. Those are different claims, and the difference matters: if criticality is the
requirement, an argument about criticality measurement is an argument about the theory; if it is the
effect the requirement leaves in tissue, the requirement remains computational and survives that
dispute.

The requirement is open-ended Class 4 computation. Criticality is the neural effect. Twelve sites where
the two readings disagreed now say the same thing, including the Introduction's showcase paragraph, the
operational-definitions table, and two claims that had drifted into the converse direction — one
asserting that restoring the criticality signatures should restore consciousness, and one equating the
Class 4 extent with "how much brain is conscious".

## Four barrier claims, removed

§4.2.4 states in the paper's own voice that the theory claims no operation unconscious processing is
barred from, and that a barrier claim is false where a price claim is true. Four sentences elsewhere
made barrier claims anyway: that a system without closure cannot blend frames (false of the paper's own
weather-simulation example, which carries state from frame to frame by construction); that removing the
simulation stops the substrate evaluating consequences or adapting; that closure cannot be removed from
a recurrent architecture, contradicted by the same result's own price for removing it; and that only a
system modelling its own modelling can formulate the question "What is consciousness?", which the
paper's own §3.4.3 concedes any language model can emit.

## Attribution and consistency

Frankish's weak and strong illusionism were transposed throughout a named engagement: the position
described — quasi-phenomenal states misrepresenting themselves, phenomenality itself illusory — is the
strong form. The label is corrected at eight sites; the argument is unchanged.

The operational-definitions table asserted sustained DMN and frontoparietal co-activation at rest, which
the cited source does not report and which contradicts the anticorrelation finding the paper cites
approvingly elsewhere. A claim that criticality could not arise from a sub-Class-4 substrate as a matter
of mathematical impossibility is withdrawn — the paper supplies its own counterexample 27 lines later.
And a claim that computational-level properties cannot be recovered from any substrate description is
restated as the claim actually intended, that they are not expressible in substrate-level vocabulary,
which no longer contradicts the paper's own position that a complete substrate description entails them.

## The unfolding argument, answered rather than deflected

§7 described the unfolding argument as aimed at theories identifying consciousness with a recurrent
network's input-output function. That is the reverse of its target: the argument catches theories on
which two systems agreeing in all input-output behaviour can nonetheless differ in consciousness, which
includes IIT and includes this theory. The old reply — that consciousness is identified with the running
architecture rather than with a mapping — was therefore the same move the argument was built to block,
and the section closed by claiming the argument dissolved for this theory while continuing to apply to
others.

The architectural difference is real and is kept. What is added is the concession that it does not by
itself answer anything, because the argument's force is epistemic: if two systems agree on every
measurement, no experiment separates them. The answer offered is that they agree only where resources are
unbounded. Unfolding trades a reused loop for replicated stages, and §8.9 prices the replication
directly. Under a resource bound the two systems do not agree on behaviour, and the difference is
measurable. The section now says explicitly that this converts an unfalsifiable distinction into an
empirical one rather than dissolving the argument, and that on an unbounded substrate the theory has no
experiment to offer. A citation elsewhere in §2, which credited the same paper with an objection about
panpsychist commitments, is restated as the falsifiability objection it is.

## A second correction pass over the whole manuscript

Beyond the defects listed above, a systematic pass applied 167 further corrections. Most are small; the
classes worth naming are these.

**Criticality is an effect, and the consequence reaches the substrate-independence claims.** Correcting
the twelve sites where criticality was called the requirement left a larger set untouched: the
substrate-independence thesis, the multiple-realizability footnote, the artificial-consciousness
implication, the comparison with language models, and the falsifiability condition all still asked for
operation "at criticality". Criticality is what the requirement leaves in *neural tissue*; a silicon
system has no such signature to show, so as written those claims could not be satisfied by the very
substrates they quantify over. They now ask for open-ended Class 4 computation.

**Predictions no longer claim more distinctiveness than they have.** Prediction 1 asserted that no
consciousness theory predicts a psychedelic–anosognosia connection. Predictive processing does: REBUS
holds that psychedelics relax overweighted priors, and anosognosia has a standing treatment as an
entrenched prior, so the coarse prediction follows in one step. The section now concedes that and states
what is left over as this theory's own — dose-dependence below the ego-dissolution threshold, the
correlation with complexity over the lesioned hemisphere, and the disconnection-versus-destruction
boundary.

**A derivation that did not survive its own arithmetic.** The reinterpretation of Libet's results
derived a uniform lag from the ratio between the simulation's frame rate and the substrate's. A 20 Hz
frame is 50 ms and the offset being explained is 350–500 ms, so the ratio cannot be its cause, and
half-rate sampling would not equalise latencies across pathways with different conduction delays in any
case. The reinterpretation needs only that the lag be uniform, whatever its size, and now says so. The
20 Hz figure is also reconciled with the discrete-sampling literature's 7–13 Hz as the upper, trained
end of a range rather than a competing estimate.

**Attributions corrected.** IIT was described as identifying consciousness with Φ at four sites while
the paper states its actual position correctly at a fifth: an experience is a maximally irreducible
cause-effect structure, and Φ measures the irreducibility. Gamma-synchrony findings were cited as
pointing away from a dedicated binding mechanism by authors who proposed exactly that, and the
disagreement is now stated in the open. The classic cat and monkey commissurotomies were called partial;
they were complete sections of callosum and chiasm. Mirror self-recognition was attributed to corvids
and parrots; the cited work reports magpies, with failed replications.

**Claims restated as the theory's own reading** wherever they had been asserted as established: the
split-brain two-simulations interpretation, the caching account of familiar percepts, the mechanism of
cognitive therapy, discrete-frame processing, and salvia phenomenology, which rests on user and case
reports rather than controlled studies.

## A third pass, and the stratum it reached

A further review pass over the whole manuscript, run section by section and with a separate audit of the
LaTeX source against the markdown, produced forty-five more repairs. Two of them were rendering in the
published v14 PDF.

**The criticality correction was still incomplete, in a way earlier passes could not see.** The twelve
sites named above used the word *prerequisite*. Four more still did and were missed. Six others placed
criticality inside the theory's own definition or criterion without using that word at all, so every
search written against the earlier vocabulary passed straight over them. A further family spoke of a
"criticality commitment" where what the theory commits to is open-ended Class 4 computation; those now
say so. The correction is applied by pattern rather than by site, and the substrate-independence claims
no longer ask a silicon system to display a signature that belongs to neural tissue.

**The explicit models are not stored in the substrate, and §3.7.2 said they were.** One sentence had
consciousness arising from "the models stored in the substrate — the IWM, ISM, EWM, and ESM", against
four other passages stating that the explicit models have no permanent physical substrate of their own.
The real/virtual distinction rests on that asymmetry, so the sentence contradicted the theory's central
ontological claim. It now separates the two: the automaton supplies the medium, the implicit models are
stored in the substrate, the explicit models are generated from them.

**Two citations were rendering incorrectly in the published PDF.** In the LaTeX source, from which the
PDF is built, an author list was spelled out *and* the citation command called on top of it, so the
document showed "Aru, Suzuki, and Aru et al. (2020)" and "Laukkonen, Friston, and Laukkonen et al.
(2025)". The same audit found two further places where the LaTeX had drifted from the markdown: Table 1b
had dropped a "one-directional" qualifier, and one sentence still said the virtual system *must operate*
at the edge of chaos — the requirement framing that v14 removed from the text but not from the source the
PDF is built from.

**Citations replaced rather than repaired, where the work cited was the wrong work.** Tagliazucchi et al.
(2016) supported a criticality claim at three sites and measures functional connectivity; Atasoy et al.
(2017) replaces it. A ketamine claim rested on a study with no ketamine condition and now rests on
Maschke et al. (2024). Shepherd (2011) and Shepherd (2013) are different papers by different authors of
that name, and the citation was to the wrong one; the accompanying "deep layers 4–6" is corrected, layer
4 being local-only. Reinders (2008), a single-authored review, is replaced by the 2006
symptom-provocation study the claim actually needs. Carhart-Harris et al. (2014) is dropped from a
visual-progression claim it does not contain, and the retinocortical mapping is corrected from V2/V3 to
V1 per Bressloff et al. Kleiner gains its second author; Long et al.'s author order is corrected. Six
reference entries were added. Siegel and Jarvik (1975) is dropped rather than carried unverified.

**Further corrections, by class.** A third site equating consciousness with Φ, together with an IIT
threshold value that does not appear in the literature. A category error described as *seeking*
something. Three authorial "we" in a single-author paper, beside a phrase that undercut
substrate-neutrality two clauses earlier. A reference to "Predictions 1–4" where there are five. Hengen
and Shew credited once more with the extension that is ConCrit's. The REBUS concession, added earlier to
Prediction 1, propagated to the Conclusion and §10.2, which were still stating the older claim. One arm
of a double dissociation that had no citation behind it, removed, and the claim rescoped. Self-model
localization language at two sites, which is the framing the paper disavows elsewhere by name. An appeal
to evolutionary forcing, restated as autonomous recruitment and glossed so that it excludes a laptop by
what the system does rather than by where it came from. A Wolfram-class assignment for seizure dynamics
that the literature does not settle.

**§7's predictive-processing entry gains its discriminating point.** The entry argued that predictive
processing is scope-limited, which is defensible and does not separate the two accounts. It now states
the separation: a prediction run through a world-model is a weather simulation, fully real computation
that no one undergoes; a prediction run through a self-model is one that someone undergoes, and closure
is what carries it across frames rather than recomputing it from nothing each frame.

Spelling is normalized to American English throughout, at forty-four points. Reference titles keep the
spelling under which they were published.
