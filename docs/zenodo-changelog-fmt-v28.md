**v28 (2026-10-23)**

Supersedes v27 (2026-10-22).

*Free-modeling falsifier (Table 1b, Section 3.7).* The baseline is now an unrolled feedforward model of matched capacity (same parameters, exposure and memory), trained on the system's own history and compared with the system on novel input of increasing compositional depth. The falsifier reads: robust consciousness where that model keeps pace with the system as compositional depth grows. The fixed lookup used before tested generalisation, not free modeling.

*Kleiner and Ludwig's no-go theorem (Section 7.3).* New paragraph replying to Kleiner and Ludwig (2024b), "The case for neurons: a no-go theorem for consciousness on a chip". The theory rejects the premise that consciousness is dynamically relevant: consciousness is constituted by the self-simulation the system runs and adds no dynamics to the system's own computational description, so the theorem leaves artificial consciousness open for it.

*The structural turn (Section 7.3).* New paragraph: qualia structures reconstructed from report data meet the measurement problem known from *g* (method-imposed structure, between-subject pooling, Borsboom et al., 2003; several generating structures for the same data, van der Maas et al., 2006). The theory keeps a narrower structuralism bound to the explicit content that enters a report.

No prediction changed.
