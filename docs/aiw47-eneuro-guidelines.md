# eNeuro — Opinion Article Submission Guidelines (fetched 2026-06-12, Session 223)

Source: https://www.eneuro.org/content/information-authors (live fetch). Supplements the
high-level facts in `aiw47-venue-analysis.md` (4,000w, double-blind, $1,945 APC, portal eneuro.msubmit.net).

## Hard mechanics
- **Word limit: 4,000 words** ("limited to 4,000 words"). Page does NOT state whether references count.
  → Target ≤4,000 words of body text *excluding* references/legends to be safe.
- **No Abstract** for Opinion ("Commentary, opinion, or review manuscripts do not include Abstracts").
- **Significance Statement**: required for research articles (≤120 words), NOT explicitly required for
  Opinion. Optional — include a short one only if it aids the editor; it does not de-anonymize.
- **No stated figure/table cap** for Opinion. Figures must be **color-blind accessible** (use textures /
  different line types, not color alone, in bar plots).
- **References**: author-date in text (e.g. "Green, 1978"); alphabetical by first-author surname. No count cap stated.
- **Title page submitted separately** from the manuscript text (carries author info + ORCID).

## Double-blind (double-anonymized) — non-negotiable
- "Eliminate author names and contact information throughout the paper."
- "Use the third person to refer to personal work."
- Remove identity-revealing "self-citations and citations to unpublished work."
- Materials and Methods "do not refer to personal work."
- → FMT/RIM/SB-HC4A cited neutrally as "Gruber 2026" (published preprint, third person). Frame the paper
  as TESTING an existing published theory, never as advancing "our"/"my" theory.
- ⚠️ Residual de-anonymization risk: a reviewer who looks up the Gruber 2026 preprint sees the author.
  Standard for double-blind preprint self-citation and accepted, but flagged for user awareness.

## AI-use disclosure
- "Both language-editing services and AI used in the preparation of a manuscript must be disclosed in the
  Acknowledgments at the time of submission." → include an AI-use sentence in Acknowledgments.
