**v9 (2026-10-19)**

Supersedes v8 (2026-10-08).

*Corrections from a cross-check of all FMT publications.* A cross-check of the FMT corpus against the master paper and against itself led to these corrections:
- Criticality as signature (§3.2, §10.1). Class 4 now self-maintains its regime, which in self-organizing systems typically shows as criticality; Axiom 3(b) reads "self-maintaining, in physical substrates typically through self-organized criticality". The paper previously named criticality itself as what Class 4 maintains.
- Notation of the two closure conditions (§1.2, §6.3, §7.1, §7.2). The fixed point of self-representation in the Four-Model Theory formalization is now written Ψ(m*) = m*, as that paper writes it, and the cosmological operator keeps the name Φ in Φ(U) = U. §6.3 cites Section 6.4 of the formalization and calls the notation parallel, not shared.
- No replay within the recurrence time (§5.4). In the coarse-grained interior description the decompression does not retrace the previous cycle within the recurrence time, which is astronomically long. A reversible substrate with finitely many states (§8.4) does revisit its states, and the interior with them, but only after that time. The paper previously stated that a return to a prior state cannot happen.
- Implicit/explicit parallel (§8.3). Explicit models are described as generated from the substrate as a lower-bandwidth running process, not as a copy of its content, and permeability as how readily substrate knowledge is drawn into that generation, where the paper spoke of information transferring from implicit to explicit.
- Opacity of the substrate (§7.2). The limit on self-representation is now named the structural opacity of the substrate to the explicit self-model, with the citation corrected to Section 3.8 of the master paper.
- CMB result (§9.4). The multifractal analysis of the Planck 2018 map found no excess across ℓ = 2–1500, including ℓ < 100; the paper now also reports the 9.6σ excess at ℓ = 1500–2500, which that analysis tentatively attributes to instrumental noise and unresolved sources.
- Citations. The formalization of the SB-HC4A model is cited as Gruber (2026d, §6) and added to the reference list (10.5281/zenodo.22133504). The test of self-referential computation as a local instance (§9.4) now points to the master paper's §3.7 and Table 1b for the free-modeling disconfirmer, §8.11 for the in-silico program and §8.2–8.8 for the architectural predictions. A duplicated clause in Weak Point 1 (§9.1) was removed.

No prediction or falsification criterion changed. One reference was added: Gruber (2026d).
