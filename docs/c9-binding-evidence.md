# C9 "binding dissolved": evidence base and candidate predictions (S339, 2026-10-19)

Research subagent (Opus), read-only, S339. Persisted before use. Citation marks: [CR] Crossref-verified, [OA]
OpenAlex only, "registry only" = metadata checked, content not read.

**Verdict.** Parietal disruption moves illusory conjunctions in **both** directions in the literature, so it cannot
test C9. On existing evidence C9 has **no FMT-unique prediction**; everything FMT says about binding is consistent with
known results (postdiction). Two FMT-committing candidates exist, both needing new experiments: **C9-1** (misbinding
and field unity have separate causes) and **C9-2** (synchrony is a signature, not the pairing mechanism). The naive
form "disruption degrades the scene and produces no illusory conjunctions" misstates FMT: under §5.1 illusory
conjunctions are expected.

## 1. Evidence

- **Esterman, Verstynen & Robertson 2007**, NeuroImage 35(3):1247–1255, 10.1016/j.neuroimage.2006.10.039 [CR], full
  author manuscript read (PMC1950932). n = 24 (8 per site: right IPS, right IPS/TOS, left IPS), offline 1 Hz rTMS,
  480 pulses, 115% MT, vs sham. Task: 4-character string ≤150 ms, report target letter + colour, titrated to ≥15%
  conjunction errors. **Right IPS rTMS REDUCED conjunction errors** (22% vs 26% sham, F(1,7) = 6.62, p < .05, 7/8
  participants), binding parameter α .73 → .77, left visual field only, no feature change; other sites null. Opposite
  of the authors' prediction; their readings (narrowed attention, release of inhibition, 1 Hz as excitation) all open.
- **Braet & Humphreys 2009** (online 2008), Visual Cognition 17:25–47, 10.1080/13506280802193318 [CR], abstract only
  (n and effect sizes UNCHECKED). Simultanagnosic patient: illusory conjunctions only at longer exposures. Healthy:
  right posterior parietal TMS (not occipital) **INCREASED** illusory conjunctions, at a later window than feature
  errors → late re-entrant binding stage. ⇒ the two right-parietal TMS studies point in opposite directions
  (different protocols).
- **Friedman-Hill, Robertson & Treisman 1995**, Science 269:853–855, 10.1126/science.7638604 [CR]: patient RM
  (bilateral parietal-occipital) miscombines colour and shape under free viewing; one cause proposed, an inadequate
  spatial representation.
- **Robertson, Treisman, Friedman-Hill & Grabowecky 1997**, J Cogn Neurosci 9:295–317, 10.1162/jocn.1997.9.3.295 [CR]:
  RM misbinds "even when he was looking directly at one of them and had as long as 10 sec"; cannot count more than one
  or two objects "even when he is aware that more are present"; implicit spatial information he cannot access.
- **Wojciulik & Kanwisher 1998**, Visual Cognition 5:157–181, 10.1080/713756779 [CR]: Bálint patient at chance on
  explicit colour–word binding while an RT measure showed correct bindings represented (implicit/explicit dissociation).
- **Coslett & Saffran 1991**, Brain 114:1523–1545, 10.1093/brain/114.4.1523 [CR]: simultanagnosia; single objects
  normal, linking structural descriptions to location fails. **Coslett & Lie 2008** (online 2007), J Cogn Neurosci
  20:36–48, 10.1162/jocn.2008.20.1.36 [CR]: one attribute reported; unreported ones still influence performance.
- **Cohen & Rafal 1991**, Psychol Sci 2:106–110, 10.1111/j.1467-9280.1991.tb00109.x [CR]: unilateral parietal, more
  contralesional illusory conjunctions under load.
- **Humphreys 2001**, Visual Cognition 8:381–410, 10.1080/13506280143000061 [CR]: binding is multi-stage; shape-to-
  surface-feature binding needs dorsal–ventral interaction and is parietal-sensitive.
- **Contrary: Donk 1999**, JEP:HPP 25:1207–1233, 10.1037/0096-1523.25.5.1207 [CR]: many "illusory conjunctions" are
  target–nontarget confusions → any test needs guessing-corrected α.
- **Moutoussis & Zeki 1997**, Proc R Soc B 264:393–399, 10.1098/rspb.1997.0056 [CR]: colour perceived before motion;
  the brain binds what is perceived together. Zeki 2003 TiCS 7:214–218 [CR, registry only]: microconsciousness view.
- Registry only: Treisman & Schmidt 1982 (10.1016/0010-0285(82)90006-8); Arguin, Cavanagh & Joanette 1994
  (10.1006/brcg.1994.1003); Cinel & Humphreys 2006 (10.3758/cabn.6.2.127 [OA]); Humphreys et al. 2000
  (10.1016/s0042-6989(00)00042-0); Luria 1959 (10.1093/brain/82.3.437).
- **Field unity when binding fails:** Robertson 2003 Nat Rev Neurosci 4:93–102, 10.1038/nrn1030 [CR] ("one object
  (but only one…) at any given moment"); Dalrymple, Barton & Kingstone 2013 Front Hum Neurosci 7:145,
  10.3389/fnhum.2013.00145 [CR]; Rizzo 2002 JNNP 72:162–178, 10.1136/jnnp.72.2.162 [CR]. No source reports a split or
  doubled field. Dreams: Revonsuo & Tarkko 2002 JCS 9(7):3–24 [OA, abstract summary only]. Consistent with FMT §5.1,
  but FIT/GNWT/IIT accept it too: non-discriminating.

## 3. Candidates

**C9-1 (recommended): misbinding and field unity have separate causes.** A focal manipulation that changes
feature-assignment accuracy (right-IPS rTMS, attention load, eccentricity) leaves object/scene unity and global regime
markers (PCI, Lempel-Ziv, DFA/branching) unchanged. A global manipulation (graded propofol short of unresponsiveness)
degrades the whole field (fewer items, lower clarity, loss) without raising conjunction errors at matched feature
accuracy, and never yields features experienced outside one field. Measures: guessing-corrected α, confidence, a
"one object or two / colour attached or floating" probe, TMS-EEG PCI, resting LZc. **Falsified if** (a) focal α shifts
move global markers in proportion; (b) sedation raises conjunction errors selectively at matched feature accuracy
before global degradation; (c) subjects report features experienced but not in one field. Rivals: FIT shares arm A
(postdiction); synchrony-binding accounts conflict on (b); GNWT largely agrees on (b) (our reading); IIT agrees on
direction; Zeki's microconsciousness allows (c) — sharpest conflict. Shared in part; candidate-test.

**C9-2 (riskier): synchrony is a signature, not the pairing mechanism.** In-phase vs anti-phase gamma HD-tACS between
colour- and form-coding cortex at matched arousal changes detection/clarity, not which features pair. **Falsified if**
anti-phase raises conjunction errors vs in-phase at matched feature accuracy (the temporal-binding prediction).
Contested against synchrony accounts. Feasibility of the phase control is uncertain; no direct prior study found.

**Postdictions (map "consistent with"):** high-confidence illusory conjunctions as single objects; implicit-correct /
explicit-wrong binding; colour–motion asynchrony; dream bizarreness in context relations.

## 4. Open risks
1. Braet & Humphreys full text unread (closed).
2. The illusory-conjunction construct is contested (Donk 1999).
3. **MG decision:** does §5.1 "binding" include feature-to-object binding? If yes, FMT owes an account of illusory
   conjunctions the master does not give, and the wording changes.
4. Simultanagnosia as channel occupancy (§3.6.1) is an undeveloped extension, not a derivation.
5. Field-unity phenomenology probes are unvalidated; C9-1(c) may be untestable in practice.
6. Several sources registry-only (list above). Two online-vs-volume year mismatches (Braet & Humphreys; Coslett & Lie).
