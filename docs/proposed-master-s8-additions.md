## §8.7 Prediction 6: A Simulated Generation Account Reduces Felt Mystery

**Statement.** Showing a participant a display of a plausible build-up of "their own processing" that is contingent on
a percept or decision and precedes it should lower the participant's reported mystery about how that experience came
about. Whether the display is veridical should make no difference. A display that is equally contingent and equally
timed but uninterpretable should leave the mystery unchanged or raise it.

**Mechanism.** *Derivation: Principle 3 → the meta-problem account (Section 3.8) → prediction. The explicit self-model
can represent that it is having an experience but not the mechanism that generates it, and its observational horizon
is bounded by the explicit world model (Section 3.4.3), so substrate information can reach it only as content inside
the simulation. The sense of mystery is the self-model's representation of an experience without a generation account.
Any input that places a generation account inside the simulation removes that absence, whatever its source; an
uninterpreted leak (Section 3.8) does not.* A fake display and a real one reach the self-model by the same route, which
is why the theory predicts no advantage for the veridical one.

**Testability.** Within-subject design with four arms: (1) a contingent display that precedes the experience; (2) the
same display mistimed or uncorrelated; (3) no display; (4) a contingent, preceding, uninterpretable display. Percept
trials use degraded-to-clear images; decision trials use cued free choices or primed outcomes (Wegner & Wheatley,
1999). A veridical sub-arm uses a real-time readiness-potential interface (Schultze-Kraft et al., 2016). Fake precursor
feedback is known to shift self-attribution (Valins, 1966; Banks & Isham, 2009; Aarts et al., 2005). No validated
instrument for felt mystery about one's own experience exists; per-trial ratings built on hard-problem intuition items
(Díaz, 2021; Gottlieb & Lombrozo, 2018) must be validated as part of the work. Arm 2 controls belief and content, arm 4
controls timing and predicts the opposite direction, which demand characteristics would not produce.

**Falsification.** If the preceding contingent display does not lower mystery relative to the mistimed one at matched
belief in the display, or the veridical display lowers it more than the fake one, or the uninterpretable display lowers
it as much as the interpretable one, the meta-problem account of Section 3.8 is wrong. Self-referential closure is not
touched by that failure.

**Distinguishing power.** Accounts on which the mystery tracks a real explanatory gap predict no effect of a fake
display. AST (Graziano & Webb, 2015) shares the explanation, a self-model that omits mechanism, but places it in an
automatic attention schema that an external display does not alter, so on our reading it predicts a weaker effect or
none; illusionism reads the same way. Neither has published such a prediction. Uniqueness: shared in part.

## §8.8 Prediction 7: Shortening the Echo Window Makes Experience Frame-Like in Every Modality at Once

**Statement.** A manipulation that shortens the window over which each frame's content persists into the next should
make experience frame-like ("single frames, step by step"), in all sensory modalities and in thought together, and the
size of that effect should covary with the shortening of the brain's temporal integration window.

**Mechanism.** *Derivation: Principle 3 → recursive reverberation under closure (Section 3.4.4) → prediction. Each frame
reverberates through the recursive levels and bleeds into the next, and that carrying, modelled as the recent past of a
perspective, is the experienced flow. Less carrying, less flow. Because the reverberation runs through the explicit
simulation as a whole, the loss should not be tied to one sensory pathway.* The theory leaves frame duration open
(Section 3.4.4), so the prediction targets the smearing window, not a frame rate.

**Testability.** Placebo-controlled THC at two doses, crossover, about 40 occasional users (frequent users show
tolerance in timing tasks; Sewell et al., 2012), with eye tracking. Neural measure: the autocorrelation window of EEG or
MEG (Honey et al., 2012; Golesorkhi et al., 2021), which already separates states of consciousness (Huang et al., 2018;
Zilio et al., 2021). Behavioural measures: two-flash fusion and simultaneity thresholds (Samaha & Postle, 2015) with
auditory and cognitive analogues. Report: per-modality items on frame-like experience built on Melges's construct of
temporal disintegration (Melges et al., 1970), which has to be validated. Control: TMS over V5 produces transient
motion blindness (Beckers & Zeki, 1995); the theory reads it as an implicit-model channel loss and predicts
vision-only discontinuity with no change in the global autocorrelation window. (The C5 proposal's "add only after an ACW pilot" was overruled by MG S339.)

**Falsification.** If frame-like reports occur without a shorter autocorrelation window, or the window shortens without
frame-like reports, or the reports are confined to vision or locked to saccades, the reverberation account of temporal
flow is wrong.

**Distinguishing power.** Discrete-perception accounts (VanRullen, 2016; Herzog et al., 2020) also predict stroboscopic
experience, but tie it to a slower perceptual cycle (lower alpha peak frequency). The theory ties it to the smearing
window and predicts that alpha frequency need not move. Contested on mechanism, shared in part on the phenomenon.

## In-silico program (§8.11 after renumbering), new planned-test paragraph

**A planned test of the temporal reverberation account.** In a closed architecture, cut the carrying of the self-model's
own state from one frame into the next while keeping within-frame closure. The self-model should then lose its
representation of its own elapsed time and of the order of its own past states, while the world model's representation
of external time survives. Three arms (closure with carrying, closure with carrying cut, recurrence without closure);
linear decoding of "time since my action" and "order of my last two actions" from self-model units versus world-model
units. The prediction is the interaction. It fails if the cut arm decodes its own elapsed time from self-model units as
well as the full arm does. It tests representation, not experience, the standard Section 3.4.3 applies to
self-recognition. Not yet run.

## C9 (MG S339: §5.1 binding = field unity only) — wording for approval

Proposed as map node (L4, brain-specific, status `proposed`, edge C9 → node) and, if approved, as part of the same
version's §8 or as a sub-prediction under §5.1:

**C9-1: Misbinding and field unity have separate causes.** A focal manipulation that changes feature-assignment
accuracy (right intraparietal rTMS, spatial-attention load, eccentricity) should leave the experienced unity of each
object and of the scene, and global regime markers (perturbational complexity, Lempel-Ziv complexity), unchanged. A
global manipulation (graded propofol sedation short of unresponsiveness) should degrade the whole field (fewer items,
lower clarity, then loss) without raising conjunction errors at matched feature accuracy, and should never produce
features experienced outside a single field. **Falsified if** focal shifts in guessing-corrected binding accuracy move
the global markers in proportion; or sedation raises conjunction errors selectively before the field degrades; or
subjects report features experienced but not in one field. Rivals: feature-integration theory shares the focal half
(postdiction); synchrony-binding accounts conflict on sedation; Zeki's microconsciousness view allows features outside
one field (sharpest conflict). Shared in part; candidate-test. Note: the two parietal-TMS studies disagree in direction
(Esterman et al. 2007 fewer, Braet & Humphreys 2009 more conjunction errors), which FMT does not need to resolve.

**C9-2 (riskier, recommend map only):** in-phase vs anti-phase gamma tACS between colour- and form-coding cortex at
matched arousal changes detection and clarity, not which features pair; falsified if anti-phase raises conjunction
errors at matched feature accuracy.
