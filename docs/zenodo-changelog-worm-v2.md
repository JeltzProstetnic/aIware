Supersedes v1 (2026-10-19).

*Corrections from a cross-check of all FMT publications.* A cross-check of the FMT corpus against the master paper and against itself led to these corrections:
- The other conjuncts of FMT's sufficient conditions (Section 4.3). A paragraph now treats free modelling, embodiment and the pain-pleasure axis as relations among realized states. FMT's clause that a substrate must actually perform open-ended computation (Gruber 2026a, §3.7.3) asks for a realized trajectory whose states depend on each other through an open-ended rule, and sets no condition on the states being produced one after another in time. Embodiment is the body content of the world-model and the pain-pleasure axis is valence states in the self-model. A realized record therefore meets the whole conjunction where the run meets it. Version 1 named these conjuncts and did not return to them.
- Model classes. "Two kinds of model, implicit and explicit" now reads "two model classes, implicit and explicit", so that "model kinds" keeps its meaning of the four cells of the implicit/explicit by world/self taxonomy.
- The reference to the Four-Model Theory no longer names a version; it cites the concept DOI 10.5281/zenodo.18669891, which resolves to the current version.

The argument, its conclusion and the abstract are unchanged.
