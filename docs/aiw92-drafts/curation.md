## 3. Scientific-accuracy risk register

### 3-A. THE SEIZURE QUESTION (load-bearing) — VERDICT: do NOT relabel generalized seizure as "ordered/Class-2." Keep a supercritical-or-route-independent framing. **Rating: AUTHOR-DECISION, but with a strong evidence-based recommendation.**

**What the three agents proposed:** reframe generalized tonic-clonic seizure as *hypersynchronous →
"one giant dumb wave" → Class 2 (ordered) → low Class-4 extent → unconscious*, and EDIT the existing
book+paper text that frames seizure as *supercritical/chaos (Class 5)*. They treated the existing
"chaos" framing as the error.

**What the literature actually says (this is the important correction):**

1. **Generalized seizures with loss of consciousness show a DROP in signal complexity** — reduced
   Lempel-Ziv complexity, reduced permutation entropy, reduced PCI-type diversity, with the magnitude of
   the complexity *decrease* correlating with the depth of impaired awareness. So the agents' *conclusion*
   ("Dial 2 / complexity crashes → unconscious") is **CORRECT and well-supported.** (Bertini/various;
   Scientific Reports 2022 "Consciousness alteration in focal epilepsy is related to loss of signal
   complexity"; arxiv 1701.07061 entropy in altered states.)

2. **But the DYNAMICAL regime is consensus-labelled SUPERCRITICAL, not "ordered/subcritical."** The
   neuronal-avalanche / branching-ratio literature finds seizures are reached by driving the branching
   parameter **m > 1** (supercritical): increased firing rate → increased branching → positive-feedback
   runaway; E/I imbalance destroys the power law and produces *more* large avalanches than critical;
   seizure dynamics "match theoretical predictions for networks driven away from a phase transition into
   disorder, with the emergence of chaos and loss of dynamic range / metastability." (Hagemann et al.
   bioRxiv 2021 "Single-cell networks reorganise to facilitate whole-brain supercritical dynamics during
   epileptic seizures"; Arviv/Meisel line; Why Brain Criticality Is Clinically Relevant, Front. Neural
   Circuits 2020, PMC7479292.)

3. **Hypersynchrony is real but is NOT the same as "ordered/Class-2."** Thalamocortical hypersynchrony
   (medial pulvinar) genuinely mediates ictal loss of consciousness (Bratu et al. 2025, Epileptic
   Disorders). Hypersynchrony co-occurs WITH supercriticality and WITH complexity collapse — it is the
   *correlation structure* (everything locked together), while the branching/avalanche measure is
   *supercritical*. The agents conflated "synchronous correlation" with "subcritical/ordered branching."
   These are different axes. A hypersynchronous state can be supercritical (m>1) and low-complexity at the
   same time — which is exactly the seizure.

**Verdict (defensible, evidence-based):**
- **The claim "synchronous → low Class-4 involvement → unconscious" is DEFENSIBLE** as a phenomenological
  statement: synchrony destroys differentiation, complexity collapses, consciousness is lost. The
  *negative-control* logic ("lots of brain active ≠ lots of brain conscious") is sound and is the strongest
  single sentence in the two-dials case — **keep it.**
- **The claim "generalized seizure = Class 2 (ordered), and the existing supercritical/chaos framing is
  wrong" is NOT defensible** and would be the kind of mis-signed dynamical claim a careful referee
  catches. The existing book/paper "supercritical/past-criticality" framing is the *better-supported*
  one. **Do NOT edit the canonical text to say "ordered/Class-2."**
- **Correct reconciliation = the FMT paper agent's "route-independent" framing** (the one good move in the
  three proposals): the invariant FMT commits to is **loss of Class-4 dynamics**, reachable *from above*
  (supercritical runaway, m>1, into chaos) — which is the dominant, literature-supported route — with
  hypersynchrony as the correlational signature and complexity collapse as the measurable consequence.
  The negative control then reads: "co-activation/synchrony is high, but differentiated Class-4 dynamics
  (the extent dimension) collapse, so consciousness is lost." This keeps the sharp didactic point WITHOUT
  asserting the wrong dynamical label. **Do not introduce "Class 2 / one dumb wave / too ordered" as the
  mechanism;** frame it as "synchrony + complexity collapse = exit from Class 4," which is regime-agnostic.
- **Focal vs generalized:** worth one clause. Some *focal/interictal* recordings show irregular/complex or
  near-critical-then-deviating dynamics; *generalized* tonic-clonic with LOC is the clean
  supercritical-runaway + complexity-collapse case. The negative control should be stated for the
  *generalized* seizure.

**Citeable sources for MG (authors/year/venue):**
- Hagemann, A. et al. (2021), *Single-cell networks reorganise to facilitate whole-brain supercritical
  dynamics during epileptic seizures*, bioRxiv (single-cell-resolution: seizures = whole-brain
  supercritical; E/I imbalance → loss of criticality).
- Zimmern, V. (2020), *Why Brain Criticality Is Clinically Relevant: A Scoping Review*, Frontiers in
  Neural Circuits 14:54 (PMC7479292) — review tying branching m>1, supercriticality, and seizures.
- Meisel, C. & Kuehn, T. (2012) and Meisel et al. (2015), *Critical slowing / deviation toward
  supercritical dynamics before seizures* (ECoG; sleep-deprivation → supercritical drift).
- "Consciousness alteration in focal epilepsy is related to loss of signal complexity and information
  processing" (2022), Scientific Reports 12 — complexity (LZ/entropy) drop tracks impaired awareness.
- (Synchrony/LOC mechanism, optional) Bratu, I. et al. (2025), Epileptic Disorders — thalamocortical
  (pulvinar) synchrony mediates ictal loss of consciousness; reducing synchrony restores awareness.

**Status: web-verified (not NEEDS-VERIFICATION).** The agents' citation placeholders Schindler 2008
(hypersynchrony) and Meisel 2012 (supercriticality) are real and appropriate, but the *interpretation*
must be the route-independent one above, with supercritical as the dominant generalized-seizure route.

**Bottom line for the author:** keep the seizure as a negative control (it is excellent), but reconcile
toward "**exit from Class 4 (synchrony + complexity collapse, dominant route supercritical)**," NOT toward
"ordered/Class-2." This *preserves* most of the existing canonical "supercritical/past-criticality"
wording rather than overturning it — a smaller, safer edit than all three agents proposed.

### 3-B. Pattern 5 — both-maxed → time-dilation: falsifiability + sits-on-top-of-IIT/PCI. **Rating: HEDGE-THEN-SHIP (books) / AUTHOR-DECISION (paper, as a numbered prediction).**

**Is it testable?** Yes, in principle, and it is genuinely distinctive:
- *Operationalizable:* integration-extent (spatial fraction at criticality / Φ-family / global
  integration) AND complexity (LZ, PCI) measured *together*, plus self-reported subjective duration.
  Salvia is tractable (controlled high-density EEG + time-perception instruments); NDE is opportunistic
  (cardiac-arrest EEG cohorts).
- *Falsifiable:* if extreme time-dilation occurs with high complexity but ordinary/low integration (or
  vice versa), or if the two keep trading off as in normal states, the both-maxed claim fails.

**The serious tension — "both maxed" vs the single-quantity tradition:** This is the real hazard, and the
agents under-weighted it.
- The integration↔differentiation tradition (Tononi & Edelman neural complexity C_N; IIT Φ) frames
  consciousness as a **single optimized balance** — high complexity *is* the optimal *synthesis* of the
  two; "too much integration without differentiation = undifferentiated; too much differentiation without
  integration = fragmented." On that view, "ride a frontier, both rarely maxed" is *exactly* the standard
  picture, and "**both simultaneously maxed**" is not obviously a coherent *additional* state — it risks
  reading as "maximal C_N," which is just "maximal consciousness," not a novel corner.
- **However**, the empirical altered-states data give the claim real support and a clean way to state it:
  **psychedelics reliably INCREASE LZ/signal diversity** (Schartner et al. 2017, Sci Rep — ketamine, LSD,
  psilocybin all show higher diversity; entropic-brain), i.e. they push *complexity* UP rather than
  trading it for integration; and the **dying-brain surge shows increased gamma coherence + anterior-
  posterior connectivity** (Borjigin 2013 PNAS; Xu et al. 2023 PNAS), i.e. *integration* UP. So there is
  prima facie evidence that some extreme states push BOTH observables up rather than trading them — which
  is precisely the prediction.
- **Required hedge (do not skip):** state the claim as *"both observables rise together, where ordinary
  states trade them off"* — i.e. these states sit at an **unusually high point on the
  integration×differentiation plane that the budget/lock-in governors normally forbid** — rather than as
  "both at their theoretical maxima." Framing it as "high-and-co-elevated, not anti-correlated" keeps it
  consistent with C_N/Φ as a *joint signature* and avoids the "isn't that just max consciousness?"
  objection. The paper draft's "distinguishing power" paragraph already does this reasonably; tighten the
  word "maximized" → "jointly elevated where ordinary states anti-correlate." The book drafts say
  "beide… ganz oben / both pegged" — soften to "both unusually high at once."

**Not already contradicted by data?** No direct contradiction found; the psychedelic LZ-increase and NDE
coherence-surge data are *consistent with* (not proof of) the joint-elevation claim. Honest framing =
"consistent with," not "confirmed."

**Recommendation:** Books — SHIP as prose with the hedge ("the theory makes this checkable: these states
should show both unusually high at once, where ordinary experience trades them off"). Paper — the
*content* is strong, but making it **Prediction 5** is a structural change (four→five, §4) AND the most
speculative (NDE EEG opportunistic/contested). Author decides whether it ships as a numbered prediction
now or stays prose/held. If it ships, apply the "jointly elevated" hedge and the honest-convergence
language already drafted.

### 3-D. Convergence-honesty spot-check (paper agent's drafts vs IIT/PCI/integration-differentiation). **Rating: mostly SHIP; two sentences to tighten.**

The paper agent's self-audit (its §"Convergence-framing audit") is thorough and the drafts are, on the
whole, honest — they credit the tradition and claim only the edge-of-chaos grounding + orthogonality +
seizure-discriminated extent. Spot-check confirms PASS on Patterns 2, 8, 9. Two items to tighten:

1. **Pattern 2 — "two orthogonal dimensions" vs the single-quantity tradition.** The draft says the
   two dimensions are "orthogonal" and that ordinary states "trade them off." *Orthogonal* (independent)
   and *trade off* (anti-correlated) are in mild tension, and the IIT/C_N tradition treats them as
   jointly optimized in a *single* quantity, not as two free axes. This is not strawmanning, but it
   slightly over-claims novelty ("the tradeoff is well-established… the contribution is the grounding" is
   the honest part; "two orthogonal dimensions read off one substrate" is the part that needs care).
   **Fix:** say the two are *separately measurable* observables that are *normally anti-correlated under
   resource limits* (not "orthogonal/independent"), whose *joint elevation* is the rare corner — this is
   consistent with C_N being a single optimized synthesis. One-sentence edit; no new claim.

2. **Pattern 5 — "No competing framework predicts a joint integration–complexity signature tied to
   subjective time dilation."** This is the strongest distinctiveness claim and is *probably* true, but
   "no competing framework predicts X" is a universal negative that referees attack. **Soften** to "we are
   not aware of a competing framework that predicts this specific joint signature," and keep the per-rival
   breakdown (which is fair to each rival). Avoids overclaim.

No sentence crosses into strawmanning IIT/PCI or claiming undue priority. The "claims no priority over the
integration–differentiation tradeoff" language is exactly right and should be preserved verbatim.
