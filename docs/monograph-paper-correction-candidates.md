## FMT master (v19)

1. **§3.4.1 / §3.4.6 — levels stated as absolute ontology.** Current: "the computational level has properties that
   are **ontologically level-specific**"; "The result is a **two-level ontology**". Conflicts with MG's ruling of
   2026-10-18 that the implicit/explicit level distinction is a didactic cut, not an absolute truth, and that it
   imports a semipermeable boundary that contradicts the cleanness of the levels. Direction: state the level
   distinction as the theory's chosen cut with real explanatory power, keep predictions unchanged, and name where
   the cut fails (permeability, Open Question 1).
2. **§3.4.5 — "a description and not an instance".** Current: "even a complete, legible description in hand would
   still be a description and not an instance"; "Experience is a property of the running process, not of its
   description". Conflicts with MG's final position (instantiation not needed; FMT is a theory on our plane; a
   static structure may have an inside on its own plane). Direction: make the description/instance distinction
   explicitly relative to the observer for whom the structure is content; define "running" as internal dependency
   structure, not host execution; keep the closure–phenomenality bridge unchanged.

3. **§3.4.3 / §3.4.5 — further sentences tied to host execution** (same finding as item 2): §3.4.5 "they exist
   when and only when the computation is actually running at self-referential closure"; "accessing the experience
   requires instantiation, not description"; "they exist in the doing, not in the blueprint" (survives if "doing" =
   dependency structure); "The code could be used to *instantiate* the experience on another substrate"; §3.4.3 "a
   recursive structure whose operation generates a viewpoint that can only be instantiated, never merely
   described"; "has no correlate in any non-running description of the system"; "The self-model is not merely a
   data structure; it is a running process". Softer: §4.4 "any physical system that meets the sufficient
   conditions" (abstract-structure sufficiency exceeds physical supervenience); §4.1 "constituted by the *process*"
   (holds under the dependency reading); §3.4.4 "the simulation's clock" (say: internal, not the host's).

3a. **Scope question first (MG ruling needed):** does the "cuts" position cover only the implicit/explicit
   boundary, or also the substrate/computation level distinction of §3.4.1 (the spreadsheet)? FMT identifies
   implicit = real side = substrate (§3.3), so the cut reaches the two-level ontology. If yes, §3.4.1 "ontologically
   level-specific — … however complete that description and however functionally enriched" is a further candidate.
3b. **§3.8** "that mechanism operates at the implicit/substrate level, which is **by definition outside** the
   explicit/virtual level" — strict reading contradicted by §3.6 ("normally implicit … become accessible").
   Suggested: "which, taken as a whole, cannot be inside the explicit/virtual level it generates."
3c. **§3.3** "The implicit models are structurally inaccessible to direct experience — and this is not a contingent
   limitation but a consequence of their architectural role"; "the content of implicit knowledge is therefore on the
   wrong side of the generative relation — it cannot simultaneously *be* what is generated"; "not merely a
   descriptive taxonomy". Only the paragraph's own whole-at-once form ("the *entire* structural substrate cannot
   simultaneously serve as the generative reference frame and appear as content") survives the cut.
3d. **§3.4.6** "The structural side of the brain … *cannot* be conscious because it is the reference frame from which
   consciousness is generated" — the reason supports only the whole-at-once form; the paragraph's last sentence is
   correctly scoped and could replace it.
3e. **Cost to confirm:** under the cuts reading the regress argument yields only the whole-at-once constraint and no

## SB-HC4A (v8)

4a. **§8.2 vs §8.3** — the boundary is "inaccessible from within" and "no observer … reads anything from behind the
   boundary", yet the laws "determine what information from the singularity-layer substrate enters the observable
   interior". Suggested §8.2 qualifier: "not readable as content from within; present in the interior only through
   what it projects."
4b. **§6.3** "This is a physical claim rather than a choice of description" — the one place in either paper asserting
   not-a-description status for a drawn distinction. The capacity argument after it may survive (closure vs
   exogenous law). MG's judgment, not necessarily a correction.

4. **Cyclic cosmology owes an observer measure** (monograph Ch 13): §5.4 cyclicity raises the question of how
   observer-occurrences across cycles are weighted, and the paper does not address it. Direction: name it as an
   open debt in §9.

4c. **§6.5 measurement independence — non sequitur (substantive).** "The apparatus settings … are not entangled with
   the pair before the measurement, and therefore do not participate in the locus. Measurement independence is
   preserved." Monogamy excludes *entanglement* with third systems; measurement independence concerns *statistical*
   correlation between λ and settings, which classical correlation can violate without entanglement. Needs a
   different argument, or state MI as an explicit commitment.
4d. **§8.4 CPT ≠ reversibility.** "The CPT theorem … establishes that any Lorentz-invariant, local, unitary quantum
   field theory is CPT-symmetric — a deep reversibility of the laws is a theorem". CPT is a discrete symmetry;
   unitarity (reversibility) is a hypothesis of the theorem, not its conclusion.
4e. **§6.5 Bell premises:** add the single-outcome-per-wing premise to the "two premises" (else many-worlds would
   appear excluded).
4f. **§6.5 Bohmian scope:** "reproduces every prediction of quantum mechanics" — true for non-relativistic QM only.
4g. **§2.3 complexity bound:** K(prefix of length n) ≤ K(rule) + K(initial condition) + O(log n); mention the step
   counter. Low priority.

4h. **§6.4 constitutive limit — make it reader-relative.** "a description of a process, however complete, is not an
   instance of the process, so nothing short of the universe serves as its complete description." Direction: "a
   description held by a proper part is not, for that part, an instance of the process" (also consistent with §8.4).
4i. **§8.4 + §5.4 — arrow across recurrence.** A reversible, holographically bounded substrate is a finite bijection,
   so the trajectory returns to the compressed phase; coarse-grained entropy is not monotonic over the full cycle.
   §8.4 should say what the interior arrow does at the return (CPT alternation of §5.4 is the paper's own candidate)
   and that recurrence is where the observer measure (item 4) becomes unavoidable.
4j. **§8.4 wording:** "This is a reading *from outside the block*" → "a reading no embedded observer occupies".
4k. **§6.5 direction for 4c (red-team):** "Measurement independence is assumed, as in every Bell-type argument; the
   architecture gives no reason to violate it, and the entanglement structure does not by itself establish it."

   clause. Direction: say "stored structure" where the closure argument is meant, keep "the physical level has no
   experience" only as the level reading's claim, and state P3 once without its level clause. Related to 3d and 3e.
