Supersedes v1 (2026-10-19).

*Corrections from a cross-check of all FMT publications.* A cross-check of the FMT corpus against the master paper and against itself led to these corrections:
- Closed loop. The list of properties that define the explicit, virtual side (Section 2.3) now reads "phenomenal character in the closed loop, unity, perspective": explicit content is phenomenal when it enters the closed loop of self-model and world-model, not merely by being explicit.
- Model classes. "Two kinds of models" now reads "two model classes" (implicit and explicit) in the Introduction, the heading of Section 2 and Sections 2.1, 2.3 and 6.4. Section 2.1 adds that the two classes in their two scopes give four model kinds.
- Stage. Section 7.3 called a locally recurrent sensory loop with no self-model in its update rule a Stage 2 system; it is a Stage 1 system, a simulation without self-reference.
- Prediction 1 (Section 7.2). The expected rise in neural complexity is now expected most strongly over the lesioned hemisphere, and the refutation conditions carry the matching clause: the model is incomplete if global complexity rises without a lesioned-hemisphere-specific increase.
- Predictive processing (Section 7.2). The coarse prediction that psychedelics should reduce anosognosia is now said to follow from REBUS and the predictive-processing account of anosognosia together, not from REBUS alone.
- Section 7 is now described as giving two empirical consequences with their refutation conditions and a contrast with recurrent processing theory; Section 7.3 is introduced as a contrast, not a consequence.
- References. The master paper is cited by its concept DOI (10.5281/zenodo.18669891) instead of a pinned earlier version. Integrated information theory is now also cited as Oizumi, Albantakis and Tononi (2014) in Section 4.3, with the reference added. The 2015 monograph entry notes the 2016 corrected reprint.

No claim, empirical consequence or refutation condition changed beyond the items listed.
