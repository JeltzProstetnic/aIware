# SB-HC4A — Novelty, Falsifiability, and Referee-Risk Assessment

*Independent cosmologist review of `paper/cosmology/sb-hc4a.md` ("Emergent Spacetime from Self-Referential Computation"), 2026-06-10. Candid by request.*

---

## 1. Relationship to established cosmology

**What the paper engages with:** The manuscript situates itself almost entirely within the digital-physics / quantum-foundations corner — 't Hooft's cellular automaton interpretation, Wetterich's CA↔fermionic-QFT equivalences, Wolfram, holography (Bekenstein, Susskind, Bousso), and the cyclic-cosmology cluster (Penrose CCC, Boyle–Turok, Steinhardt–Turok, Caldwell's Big Rip). Within that corner, the literature handling is mostly accurate and unusually well-sourced for an outsider paper: the Wetterich summary is fair, the Wolfram Physics Project paragraph correctly notes what has *not* been derived (gauge group, particle identification), and the Jow–Scott–Sievers null result against Penrose's Hawking points is cited against interest. That is rare and creditable.

**What it does not engage with:** ΛCDM as a quantitative framework is absent. There is no Friedmann equation, no inflationary epoch (inflation appears once, in passing, as a caveat to the author's own CMB null result), no confrontation with a single measured parameter — Ω_Λ, n_s, r, η_B, σ8, nothing. The paper is not in dialogue with working cosmology; it is in dialogue with the *interpretive* literature about computation and information. A cosmologist reading it will conclude, correctly, that it is a philosophy-of-physics architecture paper wearing a cosmology title.

**Misrepresentations and overreaches:**

- **Bell's theorem is never mentioned.** This is the single largest omission. Any deterministic automaton substrate underlying quantum mechanics must confront Bell/CHSH; 't Hooft spends much of his 2016 book on exactly this (superdeterminism). Worse, the paper's most original section (§6.5, James–Stein) explains entanglement correlations via a *shared prior on a common substrate* — which is structurally a common-cause / hidden-variable account, precisely the explanation class Bell excludes for quantum statistics. The section derives no Tsirelson bound, no CHSH violation, and never notices the tension. A competent referee will see this in five minutes.
- **Conformal time error (§5.3):** "In conformal time, the Big Bang maps to t = −∞" is false in standard radiation- or matter-dominated FRW, where conformal time converges at the singularity (η → 0 finite). Only a past de Sitter phase pushes η to −∞. As stated, the "asymptotic unreachability" of the Big Bang — load-bearing for the unification claim — rests on a technical mistake.
- **Scale inconsistency in the particle claim:** §5.5 says particles are *Planck-scale* singularities; §5.7's Kerr–Newman electron (Carter, Burinskii) has a ring structure at the *Compton* scale (~10⁻¹³ m), 20 orders of magnitude larger, and is super-extremal (a ≫ M) — a naked singularity with no horizon, hence no Bekenstein-saturated boundary in the sense the paper needs. The two sections contradict each other and neither notices.
- **ER=EPR and Complexity=Action** are presented as "independent support." Both are conjectures about entangled black holes / AdS holography; extending them to all entangled particles and to the paper's substrate/simulation split is Maldacena–Susskind's own most speculative speculation, not support.
- **Heat-death-as-Bekenstein-saturation (§5.4)** half-acknowledges the standard objection (dilute-gas entropy ≪ holographic bound) and patches it with the de Sitter horizon — but in eternal de Sitter the horizon entropy is *constant*; nothing "saturates," nothing triggers, and the paper supplies no mechanism, timescale, or dynamics for the proposed "decompression." The cyclic engine is asserted, not derived.

## 2. Falsifiability

The paper's own §9.4 is the honest answer: testability is thin. Of the three stated prediction classes, (a) singularity universality is untestable in practice (it predicts that already-expected information conservation holds everywhere, including in places we cannot observe); (b) cosmological criticality is the only genuinely cosmological prediction, and the author's *own* MFDFA analysis of Planck 2018 returned null — reported honestly, then deflected to unspecified future observables ("primordial gravitational waves, higher-order non-Gaussianity") with no predicted amplitudes; (c) the nine neural predictions belong to the companion consciousness paper, not to cosmology.

There is **not one number** in this manuscript that an observer could measure and compare. The single quantitative criterion offered for "Class 4" (σ ~ 1, λ_max ~ 0) is never connected to any cosmological observable. The CPT-alternation "resolution" of baryon asymmetry predicts no η_B and is explicitly demoted to "a natural possibility, not a firm prediction" (§9.6). And §9.5 argues the core claim may be *unfalsifiable in principle* for Gödelian reasons. By Popperian lights this is framework-building, and the framework itself says so.

## 3. Riskiest claims (where fire concentrates)

1. **The Class 5 elimination (§3.2).** Standard QM already treats measurement outcomes as irreducibly random while keeping physics spectacularly successful — deterministic evolution of probabilities plus stochastic outcomes. "Genuine randomness makes physics fundamentally impossible" conflates lawless dynamics with random *outcomes under lawful dynamics*. The whole elimination argument leans on this conflation.
2. **The five-class taxonomy itself.** Wolfram's classification is informal and formally undecidable (Culik–Yu); the "refinement" is self-citation to a 2015 German book. Building a cosmological necessity argument on an informal, non-peer-reviewed taxonomy inherits all of its softness.
3. **Identity of Indiscernibles for singularity unification (§5.2).** The argument proves too much: by the same logic any two unobservable interiors of anything are "identical." The anticipated objection (observable differences are boundary properties) makes the identity claim empirically empty by construction — a referee will say the unification is now unfalsifiable *by design*.
4. **§6.5 (James–Stein).** Most original, most vulnerable (Bell, plus the admitted CAT(0) gap, plus shrinkage being a statement about *estimators*, not about *physical states* — the entangled state is not an estimate of anything).
5. **Heavy self-citation** (Gruber 2015, 2026a, 2026b, 2026c — four of the load-bearing references) plus reliance on Burinskii (fringe-adjacent) and Vopson 2025 (widely criticized) will pattern-match to crank heuristics regardless of the surrounding care.

## 4. Predicted referee response and venue

- **PRD / JCAP:** desk rejection, near-certain. No dynamical equations, no data confrontation, wrong genre for both journals.
- **CQG:** desk rejection likely; CQG tolerates speculation only with technical development (the formalization roadmap exists in this repo but is not in this manuscript).
- **Foundations of Physics:** the only realistic mainstream target. It publishes 't Hooft's CAI papers and Elze's work, so the genre fits. Expect it to survive desk review and then draw one sympathetic and one hostile referee. The hostile report writes itself: Bell omission, conformal-time error, Compton/Planck inconsistency, unfalsifiability-by-design, self-citation. Verdict: reject or "major revisions" amounting to a different, narrower paper — e.g., §6.5 alone, developed rigorously with the Bell question confronted head-on, could be a Foundations or *Entropy* paper.
- **Realistic acceptance routes:** *Entropy* or *Universe* (MDPI — lower prestige, foundations-friendly), philosophy-of-cosmology venues for the §9.5 epistemic argument, or FQXi-style essay competitions, where the paper's actual strengths (architecture, honesty, breadth) are scored.

## 5. Honest call

**Speculative-but-interesting — not numerology, not yet a contribution to cosmology.** It is not numerology: there is no coincidence-hunting, and the author flags his own weak points with a candor most professionals don't manage (§9 and the self-reported null result are genuinely admirable). But it is not cosmology either: it makes no contact with cosmological data, derives no observable, and contains at least two checkable technical errors in its load-bearing sections. Its genuine novelty is concentrated in two places — the James–Stein/entanglement bridge (original as far as I can tell, but currently Bell-naive) and the indiscernibility argument for singularity unification (philosophically sharp, empirically self-sealing). The synthesis of Wetterich/Wolfram/Levin–Wen/CCC into one map is good scholarship that would serve well in a review-essay format.

**Recommendation:** Do not spend submission cycles on PRD/JCAP/CQG. Either (a) extract §6.5, confront Bell explicitly, and develop it as a standalone foundations paper, or (b) accept the paper's real genre — foundations/philosophy of cosmology — and target Foundations of Physics or an essay venue with the conformal-time and Kerr–Newman-scale errors fixed first. Those two errors are fixable in a week; the Bell omission is the one that decides whether §6.5 is the paper's crown jewel or its refutation.
