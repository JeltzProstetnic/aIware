# Fair Adjudication: FMT vs. the Field on a Level Playing Field

*Adjudicator's brief: a prior panel ranked the Four-Model Theory (FMT) below IIT on formalism, GNW on empirical grounding, and predictive processing (PP) on differentiation, and charged it with merely "relocating" the Hard Problem and being derivative of a convergent cluster. The author objects that these rankings apply a double standard — holding FMT to ideals the incumbents themselves fail. My task is to re-judge each head-to-head holding every theory to its own known weaknesses, and to separate fair-and-must-fix criticisms from double-standard artifacts. This is adjudication, not advocacy: where FMT genuinely loses, it loses.*

---

## 1. FMT vs. IIT on formalism

**Distinguish two things the panel conflated: formalism-as-such, and the scientific health that formalism is supposed to buy.**

On formalism-as-such, IIT wins outright. Φ, the qualia-space geometry, and the exclusion postulate are real apparatus; FMT concedes it has none of its own and calls this its "primary disadvantage." That is an honest, fair loss, and it generates the first must-fix item: FMT must deliver a quantitative permeability→Lempel-Ziv model with predicted effect sizes, not a "formalization roadmap."

But formalism is *instrumental*. Its scientific point is to yield tractable, discriminating, falsifiable predictions. On that terminal metric IIT's formalism does not pay off: Φ has never been computed for any biological system; Aaronson (2014) showed it is intractable for realistic systems; Barrett et al. (2026) showed it is mathematically *undefined* for the non-Markovian dynamics brains actually have; and a 124-author 2023 letter called the program pseudoscientific. IIT's empirical work runs on proxy measures (PCI) whose relation to the theoretical quantity is assumed, not derived.

So the decisive comparison is not "elaborate math vs. prose" but "an uncomputable-in-principle quantity vs. a cheap-but-unrun experiment." FMT's P1 (sub-dissolution psychedelics transiently relieve disconnection-type anosognosia, dose-dependently, with EEG-complexity correlation) is operationalizable, directionally specific, discriminating, and runnable today on existing safety data. An untested-but-runnable prediction is in *better* scientific shape than a quantity that cannot be evaluated even in principle.

**Verdict: IIT wins on formalism-as-such (a fair FMT demerit). On the metric that matters — testable discriminating predictions — it is a tie that slightly favors FMT. The panel's ranking treated IIT's formalism as a settled scientific virtue FMT fails to meet; that is half a double standard. Keep the "deliver the math" demand; discount the "less formal, therefore worse science" inference.**

## 2. FMT vs. GNW on empirical grounding, post-Cogitate

GNW owns a decades-long tested track record (ignition, P3b, masking). The 2025 Cogitate adversarial collaboration wounded its flagship predictions — prefrontal decoding weaker than predicted, posterior cortex stronger — without erasing the apparatus. FMT has no independent empirical record at all; its distinctive risk rests on essentially one runnable prediction (P1) plus one hard-population prediction (P3), none tested.

A tested-and-partly-disconfirmed theory is more empirically grounded than an untested one. That much of the panel's ranking is correct and not a double standard. Two qualifications, however, narrow it. First, post-Cogitate, GNW's grounding is "extensively tested, now wobbling," not "confirmed" — the panel should not bank it at full value. Second, FMT is not *incompatible* with the data: its "becomes conscious = implicit→explicit transfer, indexed by P3b/ignition" inherits GNW's empirical wins rather than contradicting them. FMT's deficit is therefore *youth*, not *conflict with evidence*.

**Verdict: GNW retains a clear empirical-grounding advantage on the track-record axis — fair, and must-fix only in the sense that FMT must get P1 run. But this is a maturity gap, not a defect in FMT's content, and it shrank in 2025. Fair criticism, easily over-read as a double standard; bank GNW's win at a discount.**

## 3. FMT vs. PP on differentiation

PP/active inference is itself the textbook case of unfalsifiable over-generality. So "FMT is thinly differentiated from PP" must be asked symmetrically: does FMT make *more* discriminating commitments than PP, or fewer?

Where FMT overlaps PP, the overlap is real and the panel is right: permeability is precision-weighting with a structural relabel; REBUS is more mechanistically specified for altered states; Seth's interoceptive self overlaps the ESM "substantially"; the real/virtual split is a philosophical commitment, not a mechanism. FMT's claim that permeability is "architecturally prior" to REBUS is asserted, not derived.

But FMT also makes commitments PP does not generate: P1's *global×local permeability interaction* (no REBUS derivation is obvious), P3's self-referential gradient, and — sharpest of all — the ESM/EWM double dissociation, which the empirical critique itself calls "the sharpest discriminator against PP." These discriminate. And here the symmetry bites: if PP is faulted for explaining everything and forbidding nothing, then a theory that forbids more than PP is, on the falsifiability metric, *ahead* of it, not behind. Penalizing FMT for resembling a theory whose signature flaw is over-generality — while FMT sharpens exactly where PP is vague — is the clearest double standard in the set.

The fair residue: FMT's differentiators are real but *untested and under-promoted*. The double dissociation is demoted to an "open question"; P2's modality-specificity discriminator has no quantitative threshold.

**Verdict: A wash that tilts toward "knock on the framing." FMT adds genuine discriminating commitments PP lacks — enough to defeat the "mere PP variant" dismissal. Discard "derivative of PP"; keep "the differentiation is asserted, not demonstrated." Must-fix: promote and operationalize the ESM/EWM double dissociation out of the open-questions bin.**

## 4. The qualia "relocation, not dissolution" charge

Two charges hide inside one. They must be judged separately.

**As a comparative demerit, "FMT relocates rather than solves the Hard Problem" is a double standard.** No physicalist theory derives phenomenality from physics. Illusionism (Dennett/Frankish) does not solve the problem — it *denies the explanandum*. Metzinger's SMT is a representationalist *dissolution*, not a derivation. IIT identifies experience with Φ *axiomatically* — a posit. Every one of them bottoms out in a primitive. FMT says so on the record ("a foundational commitment... not deducible from physics alone") and is, if anything, *more* explicit about its bedrock than its rivals. Singling FMT out for an explanatory gap that is universal — while crediting illusionism and SMT for "dissolving" it — is unequal treatment.

**But the internal-coherence charge is fair and survives the level field.** FMT oscillates between (A) deflationism (full supervenience, "nothing missing from the physical picture," Hard Problem = level confusion) and (B) realism ("reading the code is not having the experience"; experience "cannot be extracted from any description, however complete"; accessible "only by instantiation"). You cannot hold full metaphysical supervenience *and* an experience not fixed by the complete physical description. The "instantiation exceeds description" move is either trivial (running ≠ describing — true, but yields no phenomenal realism) or property-dualist (a fact not fixed by the description — which Section 4.3 denies). That is a genuine bug, independent of any double standard. Relatedly, the abstract's "dissolves / category error" overstates the fine print's "transformed, not dissolved."

**Verdict: On the comparative question — is it fair to single FMT out? — FMT wins; the charge is a double-standard artifact when used as a ranking demerit. On internal coherence, FMT loses a real point: the supervenience-vs-anti-reduction tension is a must-fix. Pick a horn (deflationism plus an explanation of the realist intuition is the cleaner option) and soften the headline verbs to match the body text.**

## 5. Priority / novelty

The novelty critique's strongest move is that FMT "enters in 2025-26 precisely as the field converges," making it one more entrant in a saturated cluster. That reading silently assumes FMT is *downstream* of the convergence. If the core genuinely dates to the 2015 monograph (conceived ~2005), it cannot be derivative of Beautiful Loop (2025), Bach (2026), RIFT (2026), or IWMT — it is prior to or independent of them. Multiple thinkers independently arriving at self-modeling-at-criticality, with FMT among the earliest, is *corroboration of the shared constraint*, not evidence FMT copied anyone.

The asymmetry is the tell: the same convergence is read as *validating* for Beautiful Loop and Bach ("independent arrival") and *deflating* for FMT ("derivative"). That is a double standard. The timeline converts convergence into corroboration, and the strong form of the "derivative" charge does not survive it.

What does survive is an *evidential-weight* caveat, not a *content* defect. The 2015 source is self-published, German, non-peer-reviewed — the paper concedes this in Section 11. In a formal priority dispute a reviewer cannot verify it, so the priority claim, though plausibly true, carries little forensic weight. Note also FMT claims no priority over the *older* criticality program (Beggs & Plenz 2003, Carhart-Harris 2014 predate 2015), and says so. The honest novelty is concentrated where the panel undervalued it: the permeability mechanism, the 2×2 organizing taxonomy, and the discriminating prediction set — not metaphysical originality.

**Verdict: FMT wins the fairness point. The timeline converts convergence into corroboration; "derivative / same cluster" does not survive in its plagiarism-flavored form. Residual fair caveat: anchor novelty claims in the predictions and the 2×2, and hedge priority given non-peer-reviewed provenance.**

## Bottom line: where FMT actually stands

On a level field, FMT is **a legitimate, current member of the leading self-modeling-at-criticality cluster** (Metzinger, Seth, Safron, Bach, Beautiful Loop) — not below IIT, GNW, and PP on the metric that ultimately decides scientific standing (testable, discriminating predictions), and arguably slightly ahead of IIT there by virtue of one cheap, runnable, surprising prediction. It sits *below* the incumbents on track record, formalism, and institutional grounding — but those are **maturity and sociology gaps, not defects in the theory's content**. It is **not** a paradigm-level breakthrough and does **not** solve the Hard Problem; it reframes it, and honestly says so. The corrected ranking is not "FMT third or fourth" but "FMT roughly level with the leaders on predictive content, behind on maturity, ahead on candor."

**Fair-and-must-fix (keep — these survive a level field):**
1. Deliver the formalization (quantitative permeability→LZc with effect sizes), not a roadmap.
2. Resolve the (A) deflationism vs. (B) realism supervenience contradiction — commit to one horn.
3. Soften the headline rhetoric ("dissolves," "category error") to match the body ("transformed; foundational commitment").
4. Promote and operationalize the ESM/EWM double dissociation — the sharpest discriminator against PP — out of "open questions."
5. Get P1 run by an independent lab. Until then FMT is a generative research program, not a confirmed theory, and its abstract should not imply otherwise.

**Double-standard artifacts (discard or heavily discount):**
1. "Less formal than IIT, therefore weaker science" — IIT's formalism is intractable, undefined for real brains, and unfalsified; formalism is instrumental, not terminal.
2. "GNW out-grounds FMT" read as a content defect — it is a track-record gap that narrowed post-Cogitate; fair as description, not as a strike against FMT's claims.
3. "Derivative of PP / mere PP variant" — FMT forbids more than PP does; penalizing resemblance to an over-general theory while FMT sharpens it is unequal treatment.
4. "Relocates rather than dissolves the Hard Problem" as a unique FMT failing — every physicalist theory relocates or denies; FMT is the more explicit about it.
5. "Convergence deflates FMT's novelty" while the same convergence validates Beautiful Loop and Bach — given the 2015/2005 timeline, convergence is corroboration for FMT too.

The single thread tying the genuine fixes together: FMT's weaknesses are **executional and rhetorical** (unbuilt formalism, an unresolved metaphysical horn, oversold verbs, an undertested prediction set), not **comparative**. Most of the comparative demotions dissolve once the incumbents are held to their own known failures.
