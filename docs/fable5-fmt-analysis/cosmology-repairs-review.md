## C3 (detail)
Works because it *withdraws* the verificationist reading rather than defending it. The replacement ("no existence independent of its boundary encoding" + one-surface/many-reflections) has genuine pedigree: black-hole complementarity, Bousso's observer-dependent holographic screens, Raju's holography-of-information (already cited §8.2). Dissolves all three legs of the contradiction. Non-circular **iff** presented as an explanatory postulate (identity = numerical identity of the surface, asserted then defended by explanatory unification, not derived from indiscernibility). **§5.2 Step 4 must be rewritten** — the PII burden-shifting argument becomes decorative next to the new ontology; a referee will notice. The intricacy gradient adds three obligations: (i) an **inter-observer consistency clause** — laws are universal surface-properties, only *reflections* are indexical (like coordinates) — state it explicitly or "physics is observer-relative" is an open flank; (ii) the gradient's observational content (near=intricate, far=smooth) is trivially true (resolution, lookback, averaging) so frame it as *consistent-with*, never as evidence; (iii) since the observer is itself a projection, "observer↔region correlation" must be cashed out as correlation between *surface loci*. Solipsism is defused (indexical, not anthropocentric). Keep scale-as-real-dimension substance (RG flow, §4.3 horizon hierarchy); **cut the "next Copernican step" self-billing** — referees punish it.

## C4 (detail)
The ontic/effective distinction does real work for exactly one case: genuine lawlessness (no expressible generator) vs algorithmically incompressible *output* of an expressible rule are genuinely different (Kolmogorov complexity of the sequence vs expressibility of the generator), and abductive elimination of true lawlessness is respectable. It does **not** by itself eliminate stochastic-but-lawful (face-value Born-rule QM); that is removed by the **declared determinism assumption**. Honest, not question-begging, **on condition** that Proposition 1 and §10 A3 are restated conditionally ("given a deterministic substrate, Class 4 by elimination") and the taxonomy's exhaustiveness scoped to deterministic ontologies. Cross-checked with C6: "QM indeterminism is effective" is defensible because the package lands in the deterministic-**plus-nonlocal** corner (Bohm's corner) — Bell excludes local deterministic completions, not deterministic ones. Adjacent unfixed hole: §2.3's "Rule 30 is a high-complexity fractal" equivocation (out of C4's scope; handled as NEW-2).

## C6 (detail)
Explicitly faults the prior critique: "only escape is superdeterminism" was **wrong**. Denial of locality/factorizability is the standard other escape — **Bohmian mechanics is the existence proof** of a deterministic, nonlocal, non-superdeterministic theory reproducing QM incl. Tsirelson. The repair's nonlocality is supplied by commitments the paper already had (ER=EPR §5.2, Van Raamsdonk §6.3) — never wired into §6.5. (1) One-boundary-locus **breaks the CHSH derivation at the root** — no two systems with independent local λ to factorize over; valid in kind. (2) Does **not** reach Tsirelson — removing the cap admits the whole no-signalling polytope up to PR-box S=4; explaining why nature sits at 2√2 (information causality, macroscopic locality) is open for *every* substrate program; flagging it as unmet converts a refutation into a frontier (correct). (3) Deterministic + nonlocal + not-superdeterministic is coherent (Bohm). (4) **Sharpest residual** — the smuggling worry: if observer/apparatus/pair all live on one surface, why doesn't ρ(λ|settings)≠ρ(λ)? Principled answer = **entanglement monogamy**: the one-locus identification tracks the (selective) entanglement structure; apparatus settings are *not* entangled with the pair pre-measurement. **Put this in the text.** The determinism-as-process vs block-universe distinction is real but **irrelevant to Bell** (Bell cares about measurement-independence, not unfolding) — don't present it as the anti-superdeterminism argument. (5) James-Stein → "description non-separability" is legitimate *here* (interior states ARE decodings of the surface, so estimation-theory-on-physical-states is no longer a category error — C3 rescues C6), but it **guts the quantitative skeleton** (d≥3 anchored to "17 SM types" as separate parameters is now uninterpreted) — demote the skeleton to "heuristic, pending CAT(0)/discrete formalization." Owe one **no-signalling** sentence.

## Single most dangerous remaining hole
**§5.4 saturation-trigger mechanism** (original critique point (h), still fully live until NEW-1 applied). "de Sitter horizon entropy *is, by definition,* Bekenstein saturation → triggers a phase transition → decompresses." The horizon entropy (~10¹²² k_B) is constant and dominant from early times; nothing dynamical changes at the trigger moment. With Bell and conformal time fixed, this is the fastest five-minute referee kill — and §5.4/§9.6/§11/§12 all hang on it. Runner-up: §2.3 Rule-30 equivocation (quickest find, foundational).
