# FMT: Novelty and Field Positioning — A Candid Assessment

*Independent review, novelty lens. Benchmarks the Four-Model Theory (Gruber, 2015/2026) against the major contemporary frameworks. No flattery intended.*

## Where FMT sits

FMT is, by its own admission (Sec. 1.3, 3.1), a **self-modeling theory** in the Metzinger–Damasio–Hofstadter–Graziano–Seth lineage, fused with the neural-criticality program (Beggs & Plenz, 2003; Carhart-Harris et al., 2014; Algom & Shriki, 2026). Its claimed novelty is not any single ingredient but the *integration* of five: a cortical automaton, a two-level real/virtual ontology, a Wolfram-derived criticality requirement, variable implicit-explicit permeability, and a recursive-depth consciousness hierarchy. The paper correctly states the right test for a recombination — does the combination generate predictions the components cannot? — and then largely passes or fails on that basis. My verdict: the contribution is real but **modest and concentrated in the predictions, not the metaphysics**, and several headline claims are relabelings the manuscript itself half-concedes.

## Theory-by-theory benchmark

**IIT (Tononi).** Overlap: both locate phenomenality in intrinsic/structural properties rather than functional role, and FMT explicitly clusters with IIT (via Ellia & Tsuchiya, 2025) on the structural side. FMT claims *more* parsimony — phenomenality without panpsychism or Φ's intractability/undefinedness (Barrett et al., 2026). Marginal contribution: it trades IIT's formalism for a verbal "level distinction." That is a genuine cost, not a win: IIT can compute (in principle) and be wrong; FMT's two-level ontology is currently unfalsifiable as metaphysics. Hostile IIT reviewer: "You want intrinsicality without doing the mathematics that makes intrinsicality a theory. The spreadsheet analogy is rhetoric, not a postulate set."

**GNW (Baars, Dehaene).** Overlap is larger than the paper admits. FMT's operational definition of "becomes conscious" is *information transfer from implicit to explicit*, indexed by P3b and frontoparietal ignition (Table 1) — i.e., GNW's ignition relabeled. FMT adds the real/virtual gloss to answer "why broadcasting feels like something," but the answer (self-referential closure) is an unevidenced posit. Hostile GNW reviewer: "Your access story is ours; your phenomenality story is a philosophical bolt-on with no new lab handle. None of your four predictions requires abandoning ignition."

**Higher-Order Theories (Rosenthal, Lau).** Self-referential closure *is* a higher-order representation by another name. FMT claims to explain *why* higher-order representation yields phenomenality — but the explanation is the contested closure posit, and the paper never engages the empirical HOT literature (Lau's perceptual reality monitoring, empty/misrepresenting higher-order states). Hostile HOT reviewer: "You inherit HOT's central mechanism, ignore HOT's hardest empirical problems, and rebrand."

**Recurrent Processing Theory (Lamme).** FMT declares itself "compatible with RPT at the mechanistic level." That compatibility is a liability for a novelty claim: it predicts nothing RPT doesn't, then layers untestable ontology on top. Marginal contribution here is essentially nil.

**Attention Schema Theory (Graziano).** FMT borrows AST's Meta-Problem account wholesale (Sec. 3.8 concedes this) — the self-model's structural opacity to its own substrate — then claims to surpass AST by insisting qualia are *real at the computational level*. Graziano's reviewer would call that exactly the phenomenal realism AST dissolves: "You use my deflationary move to explain why people *think* there's a mystery, then re-inflate the mystery by declaring the qualia real after all. Pick one."

**Predictive Processing / Seth / Laukkonen.** This is FMT's most crowded and most threatening neighbor. The paper concedes that REBUS (Carhart-Harris & Friston, 2019) is *more mechanistically specified* for altered states, that Seth's interoceptive self overlaps "substantially" with the ESM, and that Prediction 2 (ego-dissolution content) is "partially derivable" from PP. FMT's permeability is precision-weighting with a structural relabel; its self-referential closure is Laukkonen, Friston & Chandaria's (2025) "Beautiful Loop" without the free-energy formalism. FMT's defense — permeability is "architecturally prior" and REBUS "derivable as one implementation" — is asserted, never demonstrated. Hostile PP reviewer: "You arrived at our 2025 loop, in prose, and call the precision-weighting we formalized a special case of your parameter. Show the derivation or withdraw the priority."

**Metzinger (SMT).** FMT ≈ SMT + criticality + real/virtual gloss. Metzinger's transparency thesis already explains why the self-model isn't experienced *as* a model; FMT's "structural inaccessibility" is transparency relabeled. The paper's claim that SMT "does not address the Hard Problem" is contestable — SMT is a representationalist dissolution. Metzinger's reviewer: "Your 'qualia are real at the computational level' is a regression toward the phenomenal realism I spent a career dissolving."

**Dennett (MDM).** FMT keeps multiple-drafts parallelism and the "unified narrative... confabulations" framing (Sec. 3.1) outright. It then strains to *not* be illusionism while making an illusionism-adjacent levels move. The manuscript's own footnote concedes the distinction from Frankish's weak illusionism "cannot be settled by empirical observation from outside the system" — which is fatal for claiming it as a novel ontological position. Dennett's reviewer: "Your 'real at the computational level' is either my real-patterns realism (not new) or an attempt to rescue qualia you can't cash out."

## (1) Genuinely new vs recombination

**Plausibly new:** (a) the explicit 2×2 taxonomy (scope × mode) as an organizing frame, and its mapping to DMN/DAN anticorrelation and procedural/declarative dissociations (Sec. 8.1) — though the paper concedes "four is the floor, not the ceiling," softening it from architecture to heuristic; (b) the **four cross-domain predictions** (psychedelic→anosognosia, ego-dissolution content control, DID self-referential gradient, lucid-onset criticality), which are falsifiable and not generated by competitors; (c) the temporal-echo mechanism (Sec. 3.4.4) — idiosyncratic but speculative and untested.

**Recombination/relabeling:** the Hard-Problem "dissolution" (levels-of-description physicalism, a computationalist commonplace, plus an illusionism-adjacent flavor); criticality (an existing empirical program — novelty is *derivation path*, not content); permeability (REBUS/precision-weighting relabeled); self-referential closure (Hofstadter/Metzinger/Beautiful Loop/Bach); the graduated hierarchy (Damasio's levels + higher-order recursion).

## (2) Is the novelty claim defensible? Incremental or substantive?

Defensible **only in the narrow sense the paper itself defines**: the combination yields four predictions no single component theory generates, and that does constitute independent explanatory content. That is the strongest, most honest part of the case. But three things cap it:

- The **philosophical core is incremental at best.** The paper's own ‡ footnote calls it "a reframing rather than a dissolution... rests on a foundational commitment not deducible from more primitive principles." That is an admission that the Hard-Problem contribution is not a solution and is hard to distinguish from weak illusionism or vanilla levels-physicalism.
- The **criticality priority claim is weak.** The 2015 source is a self-published, non-peer-reviewed German book (Sec. 11 concedes this), and the content is shared with the entire criticality program. Priority that can't survive peer-reviewed scrutiny carries little evidential weight.
- **Field timing cuts against it.** FMT enters in 2025-26 precisely as Beautiful Loop, Bach's Machine Consciousness Hypothesis, Safron's IWMT, Northoff's TTC, and Bieberich's RIFT converge on the *same* self-modeling-at-criticality cluster. The paper's long "convergent developments" section is double-edged: it shows the ideas are in the air (deflating novelty) while arguing convergence validates the constraints. A skeptical reviewer reads it as: one more entrant in a saturated convergence, distinguished mainly by vocabulary and a set of untested predictions.

**Bottom line:** Substantive *if and only if* the four predictions survive testing — Prediction 1 (psychedelic-anosognosia) is the genuine cross-domain surprise and the theory's best asset. Absent confirmation, the contribution is **integrative and incremental**, not a new paradigm. The manuscript oversells the metaphysical originality (Hard Problem, closure, criticality priority) and undersells where it is actually strongest and most defensible: a clean organizing taxonomy that happens to generate falsifiable, otherwise-unmotivated experiments. I would advise reframing the novelty claim around the prediction set and the 2×2, and dropping or heavily hedging the priority and Hard-Problem-solution rhetoric, which invites exactly the hostile responses catalogued above.
