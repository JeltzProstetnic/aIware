# FMT Under an Empirical-Grounding and Falsifiability Lens

*Reviewer stance: empirical neuroscientist. Brief: are the predictions real, is the theory falsifiable, and does the neuroscience carry the claims it is asked to carry? Brutal, not flattering.*

## 1. The predictions FMT actually makes

Stripping the rhetoric, FMT offers three tiers of empirical content.

**Tier A — four "novel" predictions (Sections 8.2–8.5).**

- **P1: Psychedelics alleviate anosognosia.** Sub-ego-dissolution psilocybin → measurable, dose-dependent, transient improvement on anosognosia scales, correlated with EEG complexity increase over the lesioned hemisphere. *This is the strongest prediction in the paper:* operationalizable, directionally specific, genuinely surprising, and cross-domain. Safety data exist. It is the one place FMT sticks its neck out.
- **P2: Ego-dissolution content tracks dominant sensory input.** Vary visual/auditory/tactile dominance during ego dissolution, measure correspondence to reported identity (Ego Dissolution Inventory, blinded raters). Operationalizable, but the author concedes REBUS/PP generates "nearly identical" predictions; the only discriminator is "modality-specific tracking" vs "general updating" — a distinction never given a quantitative threshold. Confounds (suggestibility, set-and-setting, demand characteristics under psychedelics) are severe and unaddressed.
- **P3: DID alter switches show a self-referential representational gradient.** Cosine distance d ≥ 0.5 in self-referential regions vs d < 0.2 sensorimotor; alter-specific, session-reproducible, distinguishable from method actors. *This is the most rigorously stated prediction* — it carries actual numbers and a clean null (no gradient → wrong). Weaknesses: the 0.5/0.2 thresholds are asserted, not derived; the population is small, controversial, and "on-command" switching is itself contested; if the sociocognitive model is right, the "role-play" control and the condition are the same thing.
- **P4: Lucid-dream onset is a criticality phase transition** in self-referential networks. *Currently empty.* The author admits it is untestable until real-time criticality tracking in sleep matures, and the one relevant dataset (Voss et al. 2014) shows *gradual* gamma increase — the opposite of the predicted step-change — which the theory pre-emptively dismisses as "the wrong measure." That immunizing move is the most unfalsifiable maneuver in the paper.

**Tier B — "empirical convergence" (Section 8.1):** criticality tracks consciousness, waking degrades / sleep restores criticality, sleep onset as bifurcation, psychedelic visual hierarchy, split-brain degradation, 2×2 neural mapping, propofol vs ketamine. These are **retrodictions or shared predictions**, not risky novel ones (see §4).

**Tier C — theoretical implications (8.6–8.7):** artificial consciousness. The author correctly labels these non-testable.

## 2. Falsification: is the theory exposed?

FMT has the structure of **a falsifiable peripheral belt around an unfalsifiable metaphysical core** — a textbook Lakatosian protective belt.

The core claim — *self-referential closure constitutes phenomenality; qualia are computational-level properties* — is explicitly conceded to be "a foundational commitment ... not deducible from physics alone." Its stated falsifier ("a system achieving closure at criticality showing no signatures of experience") is inert: we cannot build such a system, and "signatures of experience" is precisely what the other-minds problem (which the author concedes) makes unmeasurable. So the core is not falsifiable in practice — it is metaphysics, honestly flagged but still metaphysics.

The **cleanest single falsification experiment** the theory does expose is the criticality requirement (Table 1b, row 1): find an awake, reporting, behaving subject whose cortex is *convergently* subcritical (σ < 0.85, α < 0.55, no power-law scaling). One such case kills the necessity claim. But two problems: (a) the falsification thresholds are set so conservatively that essentially every conscious brain clears them, making the falsifier nearly impossible to trigger; (b) criticality is *shared* with the whole criticality program — falsifying it refutes ConCrit/Entropic Brain, not FMT specifically.

The genuinely FMT-specific falsifiers are P1, P3, and the ESM/EWM double dissociation (Open Q4 — arguably the sharpest test in the document, oddly demoted to "open question" rather than promoted to a numbered prediction). **None has been run.** And the falsification language is repeatedly softened: a P1 null means the model is "incomplete," not "wrong"; permeability is conceded to "vary across channels, regions, and histology," giving any regional null an escape; the P1 "boundary condition" (works only for disconnection-type, not structural-destruction anosognosia) is post-hoc-deployable against a null. The author tries to pre-empt this by noting lesion mapping can classify patients — good — but the construct remains elastic.

## 3. Is the criticality neuroscience load-bearing, or consistency-shopping?

This is FMT's central empirical gamble, and it is weaker than presented.

**The "independent convergence" narrative is real but non-discriminating.** The author's own caveats are damning by accumulation: the convergence is "shared with other criticality-based frameworks"; the psychedelic hierarchy "validates the permeability principle without uniquely distinguishing FMT"; split-brain degradation is "not unique to FMT"; Katlowitz is "consistent with any framework that separates processing from awareness." Honest — but the cumulative effect is that the entire convergence dossier contains almost nothing that separates FMT from REBUS, GNW, or the criticality program at large. The credit FMT claims is *borrowed* from a field-wide trend.

**The Wolfram derivation is the soft underbelly.** The evidential weight rests on a "derivation path": Class 4 dynamics derived in 2015 from Wolfram, later confirmed empirically. But the chain "self-referential simulation is universal computation → universal computation requires Class 4 → consciousness requires criticality" is *asserted at every link*, never proven. Wolfram's four classes are an informal qualitative taxonomy of 1D cellular automata, not a theorem about brains. Deriving a neuroscientific necessity from it is numerology-adjacent, not first-principles physics.

**The "cortical automaton" claim is hollowed out by its own concessions.** It is declared "not a metaphor ... a literal description," then immediately conceded that the cortex is not a 1D two-state lattice, that finite systems are technically Class 2, and that the classification applies to "behavioral regime" not formal computability. After those retreats, the literal CA claim reduces to "the cortex shows power-laws and long-range temporal correlations" — i.e., the standard criticality literature with new vocabulary. The automaton adds no testable content beyond σ, α, and avalanche statistics that the criticality field already measures.

**Where claims outrun data:** (1) Chowdhury et al.'s thalamic "20–45 Hz" band is called "precisely" the dual-loop prediction — a 25-Hz-wide band is not "precise," and the 20-Hz "conscious frame rate" is itself a soft inheritance from Llinás/Engel-Singer. (2) σ ≈ 0.98 subcriticality is *accommodated* ("expected optimum"), but the theory would equally have accommodated σ = 1.0 — accommodation, not prediction. (3) The DFA α = 0.6–0.9 "primary measure" is a band wide enough to be near-unfalsifiable. (4) The paper credits Touboul & Destexhe (2017) — that power-laws need not imply criticality — then retreats to "convergence of three signatures," which reduces false positives but remains correlational and shared.

**Verification flag:** an unusually large share of the load-bearing confirmations are 2026 in-press/preprint papers (Katlowitz, Chowdhury, Mago, Toker, Alnagger, Algom & Shriki, Barrett) that a reviewer cannot independently verify and whose interpretations are being actively shaped to fit. Katlowitz (implicit learning under propofol) and ConCrit are doing especially heavy lifting; if either says less than claimed, the dossier thins fast.

## 4. Risky vs merely consistent

- **Merely consistent (retrodiction or field-shared):** all of Tier B — criticality/consciousness link, anesthesia dissociation, propofol-vs-ketamine, psychedelic visual hierarchy, REM/NREM dreaming, split-brain, sleep bifurcation, 2×2 mapping. Every one is post-hoc or co-predicted by rivals.
- **Genuinely risky and FMT-specific:** P1 (surprising, testable now, discriminating), P3 (testable, discriminating, but small/contested population), the ESM/EWM double dissociation. P2 is risky but a weak discriminator; P4 is currently untestable and partly immunized.

So a 36,000-word theory rests its distinctive empirical risk on **essentially one currently-runnable, surprising, discriminating prediction (P1)** plus one hard-population prediction (P3). That is thin, and none of it is tested.

## 5. Verdict

**FMT is a philosophically sophisticated architectural framework wearing empirical clothing — not yet a scientific theory with teeth.** Its metaphysical core (closure → phenomenality; level-relative qualia) is explicitly non-deducible and practically unfalsifiable. Its empirically grounded layer (criticality) is real but not proprietary — borrowed credibility from a field-wide program. Its genuinely own, risky content reduces to four predictions, of which one is currently empty, two are weakly discriminating or population-limited, and exactly one (P1) is the kind of surprising, runnable, theory-specific test that earns scientific standing — and it has never been run.

To the author's real credit, the paper is *unusually honest*: it labels its escape hatches, its shared convergence, and its untested status. But candor about unfalsifiability is not falsifiability.

**What would give it teeth:**
1. **Run P1.** It is the make-or-break, safety data exist, and a clean null would genuinely wound the theory. Everything else is secondary until this is attempted.
2. **Quantify permeability.** Provide the formal LZc → permeability mapping with *predicted effect sizes and CIs*, so nulls cannot be absorbed by "varies across channels/regions."
3. **Promote and pre-register the ESM/EWM double dissociation** with quantitative covariation thresholds — it is the sharpest discriminator against PP and is buried in "open questions."
4. **Tighten falsification language:** "wrong," not "incomplete"; point predictions, not 0.6–0.9 bands; pre-commit (not post-hoc) the criticality measure and discontinuity criterion for P4, and stop pre-dismissing the existing gradual-gamma data.
5. **Operationalize self-referential closure independently of behavior** — or concede in the abstract that the core is metaphysics and only the belt is science.

Until P1 (or the double dissociation) is actually tested, FMT should be read as a generative *research program*, not a confirmed theory — and its abstract should not imply otherwise.
