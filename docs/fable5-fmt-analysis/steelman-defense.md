# FMT — The Defense: The Strongest Honest Positive Case

*Independent review, defense lens. Philosophy of mind / theoretical neuroscience. The prior panel was briefed adversarially and produced uniformly critical reads applying standards it did not apply to FMT's rivals. This is the steelman a tough skeptic should still respect: I concede what is genuinely weak, but I will not let manufactured negativity or asymmetric scoring stand.*

## The framing the panel suppressed: there is no level field in their reviews

Every adversarial knock against FMT's metaphysics — "relocates the mystery," "posited not derived," "category error renames it" — is true. It is also true of **every physicalist theory of consciousness ever proposed**. The Hard Problem is, by construction, the demonstration that phenomenality cannot be *derived* from more primitive physical principles. So when the coherence reviewer writes that FMT "redescribes and relocates the Hard Problem rather than dissolving it," the correct response is: *yes — and so does IIT, so does GNW, so does Predictive Processing, so does illusionism.* IIT relocates phenomenality into intrinsic causal power (Φ) and then **stipulates by axiom** that integrated information *is* experience — a posit no less foundational than FMT's closure commitment, but carrying panpsychist baggage, an unresolved Combination Problem, and (Barrett et al., 2026) a Φ that is mathematically *undefined* for the non-Markovian dynamics real brains exhibit. GNW and PP "relocate" by declining the question outright — the "real problem" move (Seth) — which is a respectable scope choice, not a solution. Illusionism relocates by denying the explanandum.

The panel scored IIT "addresses" the Hard Problem (●) and FMT "addresses" with a critical footnote, then in prose treated FMT's posit as a disqualifying weakness and IIT's axiom as legitimate theory. **That is the asymmetric standard.** On a level field, FMT's foundational commitment (self-referential closure at criticality constitutes phenomenality) is the *same species of move* as IIT's axiom set — and it is the cheaper one: no panpsychism, no Combination Problem, no intractable-or-undefined scalar, full supervenience retained. A theory that buys comparable explanatory reach at lower ontological cost has done something, and the honest reviewer should say so.

## 1. The qualia account, judged fairly, is genuinely illuminating

Two things FMT does that its deflationary and self-model neighbors do not:

**It keeps phenomenal realism without dualism, and the difference has teeth.** Against Frankish's weak illusionism the manuscript draws a line that is *not* merely verbal (§3.4.5): weak illusionism says there is ultimately nothing it is like (the seeming is the end of the story); FMT says there genuinely is, but at a level the substrate-focused Hard Problem cannot reach. The decisive payoff is ethical: a system built to FMT's spec would be a *moral patient*; on weak illusionism there is no fact of the matter whether it "really" experiences. **Same physics, same behavior, different ethics.** That is a real, discriminating consequence of holding computational-level realism — not rhetoric — and as artificial systems approach the specified architecture it stops being academic. Illusionism cannot generate that obligation; FMT can.

**The description/instantiation asymmetry is a substantive anti-functionalist claim.** "Reading the code is not having the experience, any more than reading a score is hearing the music" (§3.4.5). The coherence reviewer says this is "either trivial or dualist." It is neither. It is the claim that *some properties of physical processes are constitutively processual* — they exist in the running, not the blueprint — while supervenience holds (the blueprint fixes everything). That is a coherent, non-standard position: a decompiler could extract the code and *instantiate* the experience elsewhere, yet the code is not the experience. This is the precise move standard functionalism cannot make (it treats description as exhausting the phenomenon), and it is what lets FMT say qualia are real *without* putting them in the substrate. One may reject it; one cannot dismiss it as empty.

Where FMT is most clearly ahead is the **Meta-Problem**, and even the hostile coherence reviewer conceded the account is "well-integrated." The ESM's structural opacity to the ISM (§3.8) explains *why the Hard Problem feels intractable from the inside* — and FMT does not merely accommodate the intuition, it **predicts** it: a virtual process with a mostly-opaque but imperfect boundary to its own substrate would experience exactly this sense of irreducible depth, complete with the "leaks" (phosphenes, blind-spot filling) the architecture entails. Chalmers (2018) named the Meta-Problem a central desideratum. FMT discharges it from architecture, integrated with — not isolated from — its Hard-Problem treatment, which is more than AST does.

The standard objection ("the spreadsheet sum has a transparent functional cash-out, qualia do not — so the analogy begs the question") is the panel's best point, and it lands against the *bare* analogy. But §3.4.4 anticipates it: the temporal-echo mechanism is offered precisely as the (partial) functional cash-out — why there is flow (recursive reverberation at criticality), why modalities differ (distinct implicit-model architectures echoing through), why the present has the structure it has. The gap is *narrowed, not closed*, and the paper says exactly that. A reframing that narrows the gap and states honestly how far it gets is worth more than a "solution" that smuggles the gap into an axiom.

## 2. What the 2×2 + permeability + closure architecture actually buys

The recurring deflation — "the working engine is just criticality + one permeability axis; the self/world axis is decorative" — undersells the integration. Three things rivals do not jointly deliver:

- **One mechanism spans the altered-state zoo.** Variable permeability unifies psychedelics, dreams, meditation, anesthesia, *and* anosognosia under a single modulable boundary. REBUS — which the paper fairly credits as more mechanistically specified — covers psychedelics; it does not, by itself, reach anosognosia. FMT's structural-boundary framing is what makes the global/local permeability interaction (Prediction 1) thinkable at all.
- **The implicit/explicit dissociation fits the anesthesia data cleanly.** Suppress dynamic integration, preserve synaptic structure, abolish consciousness entirely (Alkire et al., 2008; Massimini et al., 2005). Were consciousness a property of the structural substrate it should survive; it does not. The dissociation tracks *exactly* the boundary FMT draws — a clean architectural prediction, not a post-hoc fit.
- **The redirectable ESM is a genuine generative primitive.** It is the single source of three otherwise-unmotivated predictions (ego-dissolution content control, DID self-referential gradient, lucid onset). "Software-like" properties of the virtual models (fork, clone, redirect, reconfigure) are not idle metaphor — each maps to a named clinical phenomenon (DID, split-brain, psychedelics, CBT) and the mapping is productive.

The §3.4.3 Stage 1→2→3 progression (simple sim → monitored sim → closed loop) does real conceptual work the reviewers skated past: it explicitly rules out the "self-monitoring = consciousness" naïveté (thermostats, OS kernels) and isolates *closure* as the distinguishing feature. That is a sharper articulation of the self-model tradition than "second-order representation," and it answers the "closure is cheap" objection: closure alone is not the claim — closure **plus criticality plus the four-model generative base** is, and requiring jointly-necessary conditions is ordinary mechanistic science, not burden-shuttling.

## 3. Convergence is corroboration, not derivativeness — because FMT got there first

This is where the adversarial framing inverts the evidence. The novelty reviewer reads the long "convergent developments" section as "the ideas are in the air, deflating novelty." That reasoning is backwards for a **2015** framework (conceived ~2005). If FMT's core — self-referential closure at criticality, world/self self-modeling — predates Laukkonen/Friston/Chandaria's "Beautiful Loop" (2025), Bach's Machine Consciousness Hypothesis (2026), Safron's IWMT, Northoff's TTC, and Bieberich's RIFT, then five independent traditions arriving at the same structure is **corroboration of the structure**, not evidence FMT borrowed it. The criticality case is the cleanest instance: a requirement derived from Wolfram's computational classification in 2015, confirmed a decade later by programs with no connection to the theory — Hengen & Shew (2025, 140 datasets), ConCrit (Algom & Shriki, 2026), Toker et al. (2026, 680k recordings), Bhatt et al. (2024). Convergent validation *from an independent derivation path* is, as §7.1 argues, arguably stronger than within-paradigm replication. Penalizing a theory for the convergence it anticipated is precisely the manufactured negativity the panel was warned against.

## 4. The predictions are the real teeth — and they out-discriminate IIT and PP

The panel conceded this, and it is the honest heart of the positive case. A theory's scientific worth is its capacity for *risky, discriminating* prediction (Popper, Lakatos), and here FMT decisively outperforms the field leaders:

- **Prediction 1 (psychedelics alleviate anosognosia)** is a true cross-domain surprise: it links psychopharmacology and clinical neurology through one principle, is dose-dependent, falsifiable, and rides on existing psilocybin safety data. **No** competing theory generates it — IIT, GNW, HOT, PP, AST are all flatly silent on why a 5-HT2A agonist should help a patient recognize their paralysis. That is the signature of a theory with independent content.
- **Discriminating power, head to head:** IIT's Φ has *never been computed for any brain* and is undefined for non-Markovian dynamics — its empirical work runs on proxy measures (PCI) it does not own. PP's precision-weighting is so flexible it accommodates almost any altered-state result post hoc — low discriminating power by design. FMT's P2 is explicitly constructed to *distinguish* itself from PP via modality-specific input tracking; P1 has no PP analog at all.
- **P3 (DID self-referential gradient)** even the hostile coherence reviewer called "both distinctive and cleanly operationalized": a representational-dissimilarity *gradient* (RSA, cosine-distance thresholds) across the self-referential-to-sensorimotor axis, distinguishable from method-acting controls. That is a falsifiable, theory-specific, modularism-avoiding prediction — a model of how to make a self-model claim testable.

A prediction set that is more discriminating than the two best-resourced theories in the field, generated from a pre-existing architecture, is not a footnote — it is the case.

## 5. The honest ceiling — stated plainly, without conceding the unfair knocks

What FMT genuinely does **not** yet have:

- **No formalization.** The companion roadmap (Gruber, 2026b) is a roadmap, not a theory of the temporal-echo dynamics or the permeability family. This is the real disadvantage and the paper says so (§7.4, §9).
- **No tested prediction.** Every prediction is open; Prediction 1 is make-or-break. Absent confirmation, the contribution is integrative, and the theory's standing rests on a promissory note.
- **The deflationism-vs-realism tension (§3.4.5/4.3) is real and unresolved.** FMT wants full supervenience *and* "experience cannot be extracted from any description." It names the tension rather than dissolving it — better than ignoring it, but it is a genuine open seam, and it is shared by every non-illusionist physicalist.
- **Two derivations are overstated.** "Universal computation requires Class 4 dynamics" conflates Turing-universality with self-organized criticality and should be withdrawn or hedged; the criticality *requirement* survives on independent empirical convergence regardless. The "four is the floor" concession does soften the 2×2 from discovered architecture to organizing idealization — though a well-motivated idealization that maps onto DMN/DAN anticorrelation and procedural/declarative dissociations (§8.1) is still earning its keep.

## Verdict

FMT is not a paradigm shift and does not claim to be ("one model among several"). But the adversarial panel scored it against a standard it exempted its rivals from. Judged on a level field, FMT's qualia account is at least competitive with illusionism and Metzinger and carries a distinctive ethical payoff neither has; its Meta-Problem integration is a real success its own critics concede; its 2015 priority converts a crowded field into corroboration rather than derivativeness; and its prediction set — Prediction 1 above all — out-discriminates IIT and PP, the two theories with the most formalism and the most lab time. The honest weaknesses are formalization and the absence of a single confirmed prediction. Those are reasons to test it, not to dismiss it. A theory that generates four falsifiable, otherwise-unmotivated experiments from a pre-existing architecture is exactly what a stalled field should be funding.
