## 5. Risks & guardrails

- **Establishment-contamination risk (biggest):** leading a community of LLM-assisted indies could tar FMT with the "AI-slop" brush precisely with the establishment audiences MG is submitting to (BBS, NoC). **Guardrail:** keep two audiences strictly separate — *lead* the indie community, *publish to* the establishment. The group must never become MG's primary academic identity; the falsifiability bar is what keeps it respectable.
- **Time / opportunity cost:** convening + hosting is a large ongoing commitment vs. the core work (papers, AC implementation, book). Scope deliberately; do Phase 0–1 only until it proves itself.
- **Curation burden:** "100 most useful" is unjudgeable from subject lines — use a self-selection bar, not hand-picking.
- **Ivoclar boundary:** this is MG's personal academic brand, not Ivoclar. Keep clean (Hirt's Davos book-giveaway sponsorship is a separate, company-facing thing).
