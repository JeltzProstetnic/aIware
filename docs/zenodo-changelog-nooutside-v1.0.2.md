<p><strong>v1.0.2 (2026-10-22) — revisions to v1.0.1</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Bell's theorem (§10.2) is now introduced through the CHSH game, so that it can be followed without prior knowledge of the theorem: two separated players win at most three rounds in four with shared plans, about 85.4 per cent with entanglement, and every round only with hypothetical boxes that nature does not supply. The formal premises follow the game.</li>
<li>The objection that determinism makes measurement independence unattainable is answered in plain terms: determinism fixes every variable, while correlation concerns frequencies across runs; mixing is effective at the observer's scale and loses nothing at the substrate; and independence needs no free will (Bell, 1977). A misattribution of that objection to the author and an unsuitable example are removed.</li>
<li>The perfect-score case (§10.4) and the theorem of Bancal et al. (§10.5) are explained before they are scoped.</li>
<li>Chapter 11 discusses the black-hole interior, where the Schwarzschild coordinates exchange roles while the signature stays invariant (Sylvester's law of inertia; Kantowski–Sachs; Pathria, 1972; Smolin, 1992; Popławski, 2010), and states which layer Tegmark's dimensionality argument concerns. The cosmology paper's reversibility commitment is cited in its current form: reversibility of the realised substrate dynamics.</li>
<li>§15.2 adds a caveat on reading structures recovered from similarity judgments as the structure of experience, after the 2015 caveat on factor models of intelligence (Gruber, 2015, p. 45).</li>
<li>The prose was revised throughout for readability; no argument or citation was removed. Page citations of Gruber (2015) follow the Lulu print edition, and the 2015 book is cited by its concept DOI.</li>
<li>Nine references added.</li>
</ul>
