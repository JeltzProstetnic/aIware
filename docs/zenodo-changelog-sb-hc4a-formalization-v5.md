**v5 (2026-10-08)**

Supersedes v4 (2026-10-07). Module 2 (Section 2) revised against the algorithmic-information literature on cellular-automaton classification.

*Prior work (§2.1).* Cites Dubacq, Durand & Formenti (2001), Zenil (2010), Zenil & Villarreal-Zapata (2013) and Culik & Yu (1988), and states three constraints from that work: compressibility does not separate complex from random-looking behaviour, the compressibility of an orbit depends on the initial condition as well as the rule, and no sharp computable class boundary exists.

*Class definitions (§2.2, §2.3).* The Kolmogorov complexity rate is restated as a property of a rule and an invariant measure (Brudno, 1978) and removed from the class table, where it could not separate Classes 3–5. Logical depth (Bennett, 1990) replaces it as the signature of structured complexity. Zenil's transition coefficient is named as an empirical candidate for the Class 4/5 boundary, with his conjecture that universality implies a positive coefficient marked open.

*Expressibility ceiling (§2.4).* The former corollary, that Class 5 requires a rule that cannot be written down, is withdrawn: a one-line shift rule on random initial data produces maximally incompressible output. It is replaced by a Proposition (a finite rule on a computable initial condition gives a compressible orbit) and a Conjecture locating the ceiling in logical depth. §7.2 and the Phase 2 build-order entry are aligned: the target is a definition in the limit with a computable approximation and stated error, not a proof of sharp boundaries.

Six references added: Bennett (1990), Brudno (1978), Culik & Yu (1988), Dubacq, Durand & Formenti (2001), Zenil (2010), Zenil & Villarreal-Zapata (2013).
