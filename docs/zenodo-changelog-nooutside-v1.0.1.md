<p><strong>v1.0.1 (2026-10-20) — corrections to v1.0</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>The book's central principle is restated. v1.0 said the generator never appears completely in what it generates, which overstates the case: by Kleene's recursion theorem a program can produce a complete description of itself. The principle now reads: the generator can appear in what it generates, even completely, but only as a finished state; the generating does not appear in its own product. Corrected wherever it is stated; Chapter 7 is retitled "The Apparatus Not Seen at Work".</li>
<li>Chapter 5 now shows how a self-reproducing program avoids regress (von Neumann, 1966), and that Kleene's theorem and Lawvere's fixed-point theorem are one result read in two directions, with non-well-founded sets (Aczel, 1988) as the set-theoretic counterpart.</li>
<li>Explicit content is experience where it enters the closed loop of self-model and world-model, as in the current Four-Model Theory master.</li>
<li>The coin-toss example now says a shorter description may exist and still be unfindable. The chapter on the worm on the disk covers the rest of FMT's sufficient conditions and cites FMT's own conditional.</li>
<li>Exponents that printed as literal carets now print as superscripts. FMT and SB-HC4A are cited by concept DOI.</li>
</ul>
