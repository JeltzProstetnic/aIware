## The Sugiura papers (Roig et al. refs [44–46], all Crossref-verified)

- [44] Sugiura, S., Ariizumi, R., Asai, T., & Azuma, S. (2024). Existence of reservoir with finite-dimensional output for universal reservoir computing. *Scientific Reports*, 14, 8448. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-024-56742-7 — proposes/uses the neighborhood separation property as a sufficient condition.
- [45] Sugiura, S., Ariizumi, R., Asai, T., & Azuma, S. (2024). Nonessentiality of reservoir's fading memory for universality of reservoir computing. *IEEE Transactions on Neural Networks and Learning Systems*, 35(11), 16801–16815. https://doi.org/10.1109/TNNLS.2023.3298013 — no abstract in Crossref.
- [46] Sugiura, S., Ariizumi, R., Asai, T., & Azuma, S. (2025). Necessary and sufficient reservoir condition for universal reservoir computing. *Mathematics*, 13(21), 3440. https://doi.org/10.3390/math13213440 — **the necessary-and-sufficient theorem; this is the one cited.**

Two notes for MG. (1) The "continuous-time" qualifier comes from Roig et al.'s description of Sugiura's framework; the Sugiura 2025 abstract does not state the time domain. (2) The Sugiura 2025 abstract also says the equivalence implies "a universal model must have a 'pathological' property that can only be achieved or approached by chaotic reservoirs". That bears on L2 (a universality ⇒ dynamical-regime result, in the chaotic direction) but I did not add it: only the abstract was read, and the meaning of "chaotic" there needs the body. Worth a ruling on whether L2(v) should carry it.
