## Special issue
- **Title:** *"Is There More to Consciousness Than Computation?"*
- **Journal:** Neuroscience of Consciousness (NCON), Oxford University Press — fully open access.
- **Submission deadline:** **31 December 2026** (confirmed).
- **Guest editors:** **Yaïr Pinto** (U. Amsterdam), **Adrien Doerig** (FU Berlin), **Krzysztof Dołęga** (Ruhr-U. Bochum).
- **Scope (paraphrase from OUP CFP page):** Critically examines whether consciousness depends *solely*
  on computation or requires a specific physical substrate and causal structure — computationalism
  (consciousness determined by functional organization regardless of medium) vs implementationism
  (consciousness depends essentially on physical implementation). Responds to recent influential
  publications; aims to systematically evaluate whether implementationist theories meet empirical standards.
- **Key topics:** whether computation alone suffices for consciousness; digital vs analogue computation
  relevance; coherence/testability of implementationist positions; implications for machine consciousness / AGI.

## Accepted article types (this SI)
Research Article · Review Article · Brief Perspective · Methodology Article · Registered Report ·
Letter to the Editors and Reply.

## Limits & cost (from on-disk `paper/trimmed/noc/journal-guidelines-noc.md` + OUP author guidelines)
- **Research Article:** ≤ **9,000 words**; abstract ≤ **250 words** (no citations, no abbreviations).
- **Alt text REQUIRED**, in-manuscript, directly under each figure legend, prefixed "Alt text:".
- **APC (open access):** **$3,625 USD** (before tax). NoC is fully OA — there is no non-OA route.
