# RIM Reframing — Consciousness as the Third Factor of Intelligence

*(MG directive, S265 2026-07-17. This note is the substance for the AIW-118 Wittmann email ("the current RIM reframing") and the AIW-81 RIM revision. It also unifies RIM with FMT.)*

## The move (MG's words, S265)
> "generalization of RIM from motivation to motivation as **example for conscious effect**, with **consciousness being the effect in the triangle instead of motivation** as sub-aspect. other consciousness factors like play inclination, risk aversion etc etc also come into this third factor of intelligence."

RIM currently frames intelligence as a recursive loop/triangle in which **motivation** is the third factor — the recursive driver linking implicit knowledge and performance. MG generalizes: **motivation is not the third factor itself — it is one *example* of a broader third factor, and that third factor is CONSCIOUSNESS.** Consciousness is the *effect* at the apex of the triangle; motivation is a sub-aspect/facet of consciousness's contribution to intelligence, not the whole of it.

## The generalized third factor
Third vertex of the intelligence triangle = **consciousness** (the explicit self-simulation; in FMT terms the ESM⊆EWM closure). Its contribution to intelligence expresses through multiple facets, of which motivation is only the most-studied:
- **Motivation** — the original RIM third factor; now demoted to one facet.
- **Play inclination** — ties directly to the AIW-110 modelling-taxonomy (**free-modelling = PLAY**; play↔consciousness). Exploration that builds richer world-models.
- **Risk aversion / risk-taking** — Wittmann's finding that Risikobereitschaft mediates crystallized intelligence via new learning opportunities (Wittmann & Hattrup 2004, already cited in RIM); the exploration/exploitation driver.
- **…"etc etc"** — other consciousness-derived facets (curiosity, boredom-avoidance, effort allocation) unified by their common *source*: the conscious self-model steering the implicit↔explicit loop.

## Why this matters (MG's core + synthesis — synthesis flagged)
- **RIM↔FMT unification:** consciousness (FMT's subject) becomes the decisive **third factor of human intelligence**. RIM stops being a *parallel* application of the implicit/explicit distinction and becomes the intelligence-side consequence of the same conscious architecture FMT describes. (MG already stated this to Ettinger, correspondence Msg 30: "consciousness is decisive for human intelligence, motivation the key facet.")
- **Principled answer to Wittmann's critique** *(synthesis — verify with MG):* Wittmann flagged "motivation" as too imprecise (McClelland? Openness? RIASEC?). The reframe dissolves the imprecision: "motivation" was never meant to be a single construct — it is a *family* of consciousness-derived facets (motivation, play, risk-attitude) unified by their source. That is a stronger position than defending "motivation" as one clean variable.
- **Schmiedek convergence:** Schmiedek's endorsement (motivation constitutive, not noise) + his point that COGITO lacks a **task-specific** daily motivation measure and shows large within-person heterogeneity → the facets vary by state/day (Wittmann's traits/states/ATI decomposition), consistent with a consciousness-driven third factor modulating the loop dynamically. Pitch: a "COGITO 2" should measure task-specific motivation **and** play/risk facets.
