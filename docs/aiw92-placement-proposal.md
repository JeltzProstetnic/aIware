- **Soon, He, Bode & Haynes 2013** (*PNAS* 110:6217) — ABSTRACT non-motor choice (add vs subtract) decodable ~4 s ahead (medial PFC + precuneus).
- **Koenig-Robert & Pearson 2019** (*Sci Rep* 9:4520) — "decide what to imagine": content + vividness of chosen imagery decodable up to **11 s** before the voluntary choice (V1–V4, lateral PFC, thalamus/pons). ⚠️ 2-alternative forced choice, PRESET images, one small fMRI lab study, authors agnostic on whether precursors were conscious.
- **Schultze-Kraft et al. 2016** (*PNAS* 113:1080) — RP can be VETOED until point of no return ~200 ms pre-movement (precursor ≠ uninterruptible chain).
- **Schurger, Sitt & Dehaene 2012** (*PNAS* 109:E2904) — RP may be a stochastic-accumulator crossing threshold, not a deterministic unconscious "decision."
- **Passage verdict:** precursors established for limbs → abstract operations → *chosen* imagery content, but **open-ended free steering of arbitrary mental content is untested** → "how free the inward steering is" is a real, citable open question.

## 3. Scientific-accuracy risk register

### 3.1 Seizure (Pattern 4) — the load-bearing finding. ⚠️ Agents' first instinct was WRONG.
All three target agents independently proposed relabelling generalized seizure as **"hypersynchronous = Class 2, too *ordered*, NOT chaos"** and rewriting the existing book/paper "supercritical/Class-5" wording to match. **The literature does not support that relabelling.**
- Generalized seizures with loss of consciousness are consensus-labelled **supercritical** (branching ratio m > 1, runaway cascade, departure from criticality) **accompanied by a collapse in complexity** (reduced Lempel-Ziv, reduced entropy, impaired PCI).
- **Hypersynchrony is real but is the *correlation/coupling* signature — it is NOT "subcritical/ordered branching."** The agents conflated *synchrony* with *subcriticality*.
- **Verdict:** keep the negative-control point (it is the single strongest sentence in the two-dials case — "lots of brain active ≠ lots of brain conscious"), but reconcile toward **"hypersynchrony + complexity collapse → exit from the Class-4 regime (dominant route: supercritical) → Class-4 *involvement* drops → unconscious."** This is the paper agent's **route-independent** framing: a *smaller, safer* edit that preserves the existing supercritical wording instead of overturning it.
- **Sources to cite (for the author's decision, not yet inserted):** Hagemann et al. 2021 (bioRxiv — whole-brain supercritical seizure dynamics); Zimmern 2020 (Front. Neural Circuits, PMC7479292); Meisel & Kuehn 2012; "Consciousness alteration in focal epilepsy…" 2022 (Sci Rep); Bratu et al. 2025 (pulvinar synchrony → LOC). **Verify before citing.**

### 3.2 Both-maxed → time dilation (Pattern 5) — needs hedging.
"Both dials maxed" collides with the integration↔differentiation tradition (Tononi/Edelman neural complexity C_N; IIT Φ), which treats it as a *single optimized quantity* that ordinary states already balance. **Reframe as "both unusually high *at once*, where ordinary waking states anti-correlate"** — NOT "both at their maxima." So hedged, it is falsifiable and supported in spirit by psychedelic LZ-increase data and NDE coherence-surge reports. Falsifiability is OK; the wording is the risk.

### 3.3 Lock-in / "peak consciousness = minimum reality-contact" (Pattern 6).
