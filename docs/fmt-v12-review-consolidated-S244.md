### T1-2 [BLOCKER ×2 — Opus + Fable IIT] §7.2 "transfer of information" (l.742)
"consciousness *is* the ongoing transfer of information into the explicit model" — (a) contradicts the same sentence's "adopt its core: …*not mere information processing*"; (b) contradicts the GNW paragraph (l.744), which demotes "moving info into the workspace" to a non-constitutive "substrate optimization"; (c) misrepresents IIT, whose founding *intrinsicality* postulate rejects extrinsic/Shannon information transfer — an IIT referee reads it as GNW-broadcasting relabeled. → **FIX (Fable's exact text):** "…consciousness *is* the ongoing self-referential updating — sustained at criticality — by which the explicit models are continuously rewritten on the implicit substrate. This integration is not the extrinsic transfer of a signal into a workspace but the closure of a modeling loop upon itself." (Kills T1-2 + the GNW cross-contradiction in one edit.)

### T1-3 [HIGH — Fable whole-paper] §1.3 vs §3.4.2/§3.7.2 — is the automaton consciousness or not?
§1.3 el.1 (l.50): "Consciousness *is identified with* … the Class 4 regime … The identification is **not metaphorical**." vs §3.4.2 (l.272) & §3.7.2 (l.448): "the cortical automaton … **is not consciousness**. → **FIX:** rewrite §1.3 el.1 — consciousness = the self-referential self-simulation *running on* the Class-4 automaton, not the automaton's dynamics themselves. Align with §3.7.2/§3.4.2.

### T1-4 [BLOCKER — both citation reviewers] Casarotto (2016) inverted (l.148)
"PCI achieves 100% sensitivity/specificity … **versus unresponsive patients (vegetative state, coma)**" **inverts the study.** The 100% was conscious states (waking/dreaming/ketamine) vs **NREM sleep + anaesthesia (midazolam/xenon/propofol)**; VS/coma is the group PCI did **not** cleanly separate (9/43 VS had high PCI). 94.7% MCS-detection is correct. (Fabricated "91.2%" confirmed absent.)
→ **FIX:** "…100% sensitivity and specificity in the benchmark cohort of conscious states (waking, dreaming, ketamine) versus unconscious conditions (NREM sleep and anaesthesia with midazolam, xenon, or propofol), and 94.7% sensitivity for identifying the minimally conscious state."

### T1-5 [MAJOR — both citation reviewers + both IIT reviewers] Barrett mis-sourced (l.740 + .bib)
"Barrett et al. (2026) *demonstrate*…" — the 2026 item (arXiv:2604.11482) is a *review* that attributes the result to **Barrett & Mediano (2019)** (arXiv:1902.04321, JCS 26:11–20), which is **absent from references.bib**.

### T1-6 [MAJOR — both IIT reviewers] §7.2 exclusion postulate backwards (l.742)

→ **FIX:** "…that any *maximally irreducible* substrate with Φ > 0, however simple, already has experience."
