**v5 (2026-10-19)**

Supersedes v4 (2026-10-07). Aligned with the Four-Model Theory v21 and with the current verbal theory of recursive intelligence (Gruber, 2026a).

*Corrections from a cross-check of all FMT publications.* A cross-check of the FMT corpus against the master and against itself led to these corrections:
- Motivation as allocation policy. The abstract, claim (c) of Section 1.1, Section 3.2 and the conclusion now give motivation as the policy that allocates the Knowledge–Performance loop's time, as the verbal theory does. Its grounding in FMT is stated as a conjecture that the verbal theory keeps separable from the recursive model (2026a §§5.3, 7.4). Reading motivation as explicit optimization, and mapping Wissensdrang to the world model and Handlungsdrang to the self-model, are marked as this paper's own proposals. Section 3.2 now says that the scalar M(t) is a deliberate simplification that departs from the verbal theory.
- Necessity and cost. "Motivation requires consciousness", "cannot self-improve" and "if and only if" are replaced by the conjecture's own wording and by cost claims: a system without an explicit self-model has no cheap route to the behaviour in question and pays for each investigated gap separately (Sections 3.3, 6.1–6.3).
- Model kinds. The four models are now the minimum necessary set of model kinds the architecture forces, without a restriction to human-level consciousness, where the abstract called them a minimum sufficient set. Section 1.2 describes them as four model kinds along two axes: mode, an orthogonal contrast, and scope, a nesting.
- Closed loop. Content above the mode threshold is part of the running simulation and is conscious where it enters the self-model's closed loop; explicit content outside that loop is preconscious (Section 3.1). The fixed point of self-representation is now written Ψ, and R(t) is the self-prediction measure of the FMT formalization, used as a graded proxy for closure (Section 3.3).
- The ignition threshold is stated as this formalization's own posit, not as a claim of the verbal theory (Sections 5.1, 5.5, 9.1 and the conclusion).
- Notation. The branching-ratio proxy is now σ_br and age-related decline δ_age, so that neither shares a symbol with another quantity. Equations [5] and [9] are named as alternative definitions to be reconciled in Phase 4. The build-order entry for Modules 1 and 2 now matches the abstract.
- References. Two references that were no longer cited were removed (Driver & Tomasik, 2023; Murayama & Jach, 2025), and the Gruber (2015) entry names the 2016 corrected reprint.

No prediction was added or removed. The roadmap is 18 pages.
