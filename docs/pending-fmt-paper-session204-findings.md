## Theoretical Corrections to Embed

These are user's corrections from Session 204 that should be reflected in any revision:

1. **Criticality ≠ consciousness**: Criticality is necessary for computation, not for consciousness itself. An insect at criticality isn't human-conscious. The architecture (richness of self-model) determines consciousness level. The paper's §3.7.3 says this, but the framing elsewhere drifts.

2. **Continuous model space**: The brain uses a model space, not 4 discrete models. The 2×2 is a taxonomy of model *kinds*, not discrete units. Implications: developmental transitions and lucid dream onset may be washed out in measurement even if theoretically threshold-like.

3. **Context-dependent framing**: The paper must be humble (peer review). Web/social can be honest about actual explanatory scope — FMT accounts for nearly every consciousness phenomenon.
