## PRIMARY — Templeton World Charity Foundation (TWCF)

- **Foundation:** Templeton World Charity Foundation, Inc. — distinct from the larger John Templeton Foundation (JTF); related, both fund consciousness/intelligence work.
- **Program:** **Accelerating Research on Consciousness (ARC)** — a **$30M** commitment. Signature instrument: **Structured Adversarial Collaboration (SAC)** — pre-registered, forecast-based experiments jointly designed by proponents of *competing* theories, run across independent labs with theory-neutral judges. Planned up to five large SAC projects.
- **URLs:**
  - Hub: https://www.templetonworldcharity.org/our-priorities/discovery/accelerating-research-consciousness
  - SAC projects: https://www.templetonworldcharity.org/our-priorities/discovery/accelerating-research-consciousness/our-structured-adversarial-collaboration-projects
  - Flagship COGITATE (IIT vs GNWT): https://www.arc-cogitate.com/project
  - Feb-2025 ARC awards: https://www.templetonworldcharity.org/blog/templeton-world-charity-foundation-announces-recently-awarded-accelerating-research
- **What they fund:** (i) flagship SAC consortia (COGITATE tested IIT vs GNWT across six labs); (ii) a broader ARC track for teams "seeking to generate novel evidence to substantially oppose or support existing theories… or to develop new scientific tools and frameworks."
- **Grant sizing (from the consciousness grants DB, MG S271):** individual consciousness projects ~**$170–260k**; programs (e.g. CIFAR Brain/Mind/Consciousness winter school) $1M+. Realistic FMT ask ≈ **$200–260k**.
- **Status (July 2026):** ARC active + still awarding (Feb–Jun 2025 activity confirmed). No open *unsolicited* ARC call surfaced — flagship funding is proactive/invitation-shaped around adversarial collaborations. TWCF site 403s to automated fetch → **reconfirm live call state directly before applying.** JTF runs a conventional open cycle (OFI→invited full proposal; 2026 cycle: OFI decisions ~Oct 9 2026, full proposals ~Jan 15 2027) and lists consciousness/intelligence/agency among 2026 priorities + a >$60M **Intelligence Venture** — a parallel door.
