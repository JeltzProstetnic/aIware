## Score 3: direct hits

### P53 Christina Murphy (Independent)
**"Recoverability geometry beneath early warning signals in adaptive oscillator networks"**
Probability Phase Oscillation (PPO): adaptive networks survive perturbation by preserving access to viable futures (a recoverability field φ) rather than by maximising synchrony. In minimal adaptive networks, critical-slowing-down precursors (rising variance and autocorrelation) predict node tipping under slow drift but carry little signal under shock, and under drift their predictive power is largely absorbed by φ. Early-warning signals are therefore regime-dependent, not universal.
- **Relation:** a methodological caveat that bears directly on FMT. Does not contradict the theory; it constrains how the criticality-signature predictions should be tested.
- **Touches:** Prediction 4 (§8.5, lucid-dream onset as a threshold crossing, falsified if criticality measures show no discontinuity at onset), and the §8.1 sleep-onset-as-bifurcation convergence (Li et al. 2025, which rests on critical slowing). If lucid onset is shock-like (cue-triggered, e.g. in induction studies) rather than drift-like, Murphy's result predicts **no** critical-slowing precursor even if the transition is real. P4's falsification clause would then misfire.
- **Follow-up:** cite in §8.5 and add a regime qualifier: test for critical slowing only in spontaneous (drift-type) lucid onsets, and treat cue-induced onsets separately. Contact her; she was a direct poster neighbour (field guide already lists her). Ask whether φ can be estimated from EEG.

### P55 Chunjing Yu (Bridge of Consciousness (Ponscient))
**"The Fiber Bundle Theory of Consciousness: A Falsifiable Geometric Framework Connecting Neural Dynamics and the Structure of Phenomenal Experience"**
FBTC posits three coupled bundles over a coarse-grained neural state space: a gating bundle over slow subcortical dynamics, a content bundle, and a latent experience-structure bundle. A "creation map" generates experience from content, and a "report map" sends it to language. Binding becomes a gluing residual, path dependence a holonomy residual, and the experience-report gap the kernel of the report map. It claims to recover IIT, GNW and attractor accounts as facets, with three exclusive predictions: rate-invariant holonomy, positive report-experience dissociation under minimal report bandwidth, and endpoint-matched but path-dependent effects.
- **Relation:** competes, with a formalisation opportunity. It is the closest geometric/formal rival to FMT's planned category-theoretic formalisation, and makes the same move of absorbing the major theories as special cases (FMT §7). Structural parallels: the gating bundle corresponds to the enabling substrate threshold (§3.7.3 two thresholds); the content and experience bundles to the implicit→explicit split (§3.3, §3.6); the report-map kernel to FMT's presence-vs-access distinction. Its "experience is not primitive, a creation map generates it" is close to FMT's virtual-qualia generation (§3.4).
- **Touches:** §3.4 virtual qualia, §5.1 binding, §7 comparative analysis, and the category-theory formalisation announced in the P56 abstract. Fibre bundles with connections are naturally expressible categorically (functors to a groupoid), so a translation between FMT objects and FBTC bundles is a concrete joint paper.
- **Follow-up:** direct poster neighbour. Get the full paper and cite it in §7.3 (Emerging Frameworks 2024–2026). Propose a correspondence note: FMT's implicit→explicit natural transformations ↔ FBTC's creation map. Check whether "path-dependent effects" overlap with FMT's temporal echo (§3.4.4).

### P45 Asselya Krymova (Kazalh-British Technical University [sic])
**"Self-Referential Processing and the Unified World Model: Computational Account of Volitional Access"**
SUPA-V is a closed inferential loop grounded in the Free Energy Principle, with an internal configurable world model and an explicit volitional layer extending LeCun's autonomous machine intelligence blueprint. It uses a three-tier cost ontology: Tier 1 is the inaccessible NESS density, Tier 3 trainable priors, and Tier 2 "pseudo-intrinsic costs", high-precision priors that experience consolidates until they are unavailable to volitional revision. Tier 2 is "the consciousness gap". It predicts that depressive avolition is pseudo-intrinsic cost migration, measurable as reduced Tier-2 precision-weighting.
- **Relation:** competes / collaboration opportunity. It is architecturally the closest poster to FMT in this range: closed self-referential loop + unified world model + a structural account of why some priors are not available to explicit revision. That last point maps onto FMT's implicit models (learned, substrate-level, not explicitly accessible) and onto variable permeability (§3.6, §3.6.1). The Volition Engine resembles FMT's dual evaluation architecture (§4.2.1).
- **Touches:** §3.6 implicit-explicit boundary, §4.2.1–4.2.3 (dual evaluation, free will, the two causal roles of the self-model), §6.5 clinical dissociations. Her depression prediction is a permeability-type claim that FMT could restate (an implicit self-model consolidated past the reach of explicit revision).
- **Follow-up:** direct poster neighbour (field guide). Exchange papers. Cite as convergent in §7.3. Possible joint note: "Tier 2 = permanently low-permeability implicit priors". Check whether her LeCun extension is implemented in code (relevant to §8.9).

### P68 Jaya Caporusso (Jožef Stefan Institute)
**"An Ontology of the Phenomenological Self for the Analysis of Self-Experience in Text"**
A computational ontology of self-aspects (body, thoughts, self-narrative, …), their relations, and the modes in which each is experienced (degrees of identification, ownership, agency), built from psychology, empirical phenomenology and neuroscience. It covers dissolution experiences, in which aspects lose coherence and disappear. The ontology is designed to annotate self-aspects in text and support automatic detection.
- **Relation:** collaboration opportunity, giving FMT a ready-made measurement instrument.
- **Touches:** Prediction 2 (§8.3, ego-dissolution content tracks dominant sensory input). P2 needs a reliable coding scheme for "what the subject reports being or becoming", and this ontology plus an automated text classifier is exactly that, for trip reports and interview transcripts. It also touches Prediction 3 (§8.4, DID alter self-aspects) and the ESM as a multi-aspect, redirectable model (§3.2, §3.4.3).
- **Follow-up:** contact Caporusso. Propose coding existing ego-dissolution report corpora with her ontology to test whether identity content tracks the sensory setting (a cheap first test of P2). Cite in §8.3 Testability.

### P39 GuanZe Liao (National Tsing Hua University)
**"Constructing Artificial Consciousness as a Two-Domain Necessary-Condition Framework: AC ⟹ {F·B·S} × {I↔(E×P)}"**
Repurposes Gero's Function–Behaviour–Structure schema as conjunctive, exclusionary necessary conditions (third-person domain), coupled through an asymmetric entailment to a first-person domain (Interiority, Experience, Phenomenality). Missing external conditions exclude interiority, but their presence cannot guarantee it. That boundary is "operationally non-traversable by design" and places current LLMs firmly in the epistemic category.
- **Relation:** contradicts on artificial consciousness. FMT holds that meeting the structural conditions *is* sufficient (substrate-neutral sufficiency, §4.4, §8.7), whereas Liao builds the hard problem in as a permanent barrier. The two agree that LLMs as they stand do not qualify.
- **Touches:** §2.1/§2.2 hard problem and explanatory gap, §3.4 virtual qualia (FMT's dissolution of exactly the boundary Liao makes non-traversable), §8.7–8.8.
- **Follow-up:** cite as the "necessary-only" position in §10.1. Short rebuttal point: the asymmetry follows from his stipulation that interiority is a separate ontological domain. FMT's two-level ontology (§3.4.6) shows why that stipulation is not needed.

### P40 Jeremy Slater (MERLN LLC / Colorado State University)
**"The Chrono-Resonant Field Model: A Theory of Temporal Binding in Consciousness"**
Consciousness as an interference pattern between top-down "Offer Waves" (theta/alpha) and bottom-up "Confirmation Waves" (gamma), established prospectively through theta–gamma phase-amplitude coupling. Binding is quantified as a Temporal Binding Index TBI(S) = γ(t)·cos(θ(t) − φ). It addresses Libet antedating, flash-lag and the thickness of the present, and treats schizophrenic passivity as a failed handshake.
- **Relation:** competes on temporal binding and the specious present.
- **Touches:** §3.4.4 temporal echo mechanism, §5.1 binding, §6.5 clinical dissociations (passivity phenomena vs FMT's ESM account of agency).
- **Follow-up:** compare TBI with the temporal echo. If the echo makes a PAC-type prediction, CRFM's EEG measure is a ready test. Low priority for contact.

### P44 Mike Land, with Kilsharion Land (Independent)
**"Nine Constraints on Any Viable Theory of Consciousness: Deriving conditions from First-Person Experience"**
Derives nine "Inevitabilities" from first-person givens, among them experiential boundaries between perspectives, the irreducibility of appearance to structure, and the dependence of temporal ordering on experiential actualisation. Together they act as a filter that any theory must pass. Several leading models are claimed to fail, and a constraint-complete candidate framework is introduced.
- **Relation:** competes with FMT §2 ("Eight Requirements for a Theory of Consciousness"), which uses the same filter-first methodology. "Irreducibility of appearance to structure alone" probably conflicts with FMT's structure-only reading of qualia (Prediction 5, §8.6).
- **Touches:** §2 entire, §7.1 scoring matrix.
- **Follow-up:** get the full list of nine and score FMT against it. Where it fails, rebut in §2 or add a row to the §7.1 matrix. Citing a competing constraint list strengthens the §2 methodology.

### P46 Daniele Pecci (LUXION)
**"Claim-Firewalled Severe Testing for Consciousness-Candidacy Metrics"**
A Consciousness Evaluation Testbed with preregistered endpoints, adversarial baselines, negative-control suites, leakage audits and arbiter-gated evidence levels (L1–L4 plus a subjective-anchor level). It reports a Level-2 Sleep-EDF replication candidate for a coherence/integration proxy. A CHB-MIT AUROC of 0.98 was classified as inconclusive because the negative controls failed (n_pos = 2), and that refusal is presented as the central result.
- **Relation:** collaboration opportunity, and a defence against the confirmation-bias critique, which is MoC7's dominant theme (field guide).
- **Touches:** §8.1 empirical convergence. FMT's anesthetic- and sleep-criticality convergence claims are exactly the "candidacy proxies" his testbed grades, and running a criticality proxy (DFA, branching ratio, LZc) through it would turn "converging support" into a graded evidence level.
- **Follow-up:** contact. Propose a near-criticality proxy as a testbed entry, which pairs with the in-silico programme (§8.9). Cite in §10.3 Limitations as the standard FMT's convergence evidence should meet.

### P47 Erik Chevalier (Independent)
**"Building a Predictive Global Neuronal Workspace for a Continuously Running Synthetic Mind"**
A continuously running architecture of sixteen modules, each with a generative model publishing precision-weighted prediction errors. A shared workspace broadcasts the most confident coalition once it crosses a threshold, with no central executive, and the LLM is demoted to a speech organ. It claims access only, not phenomenal experience. There are seven preregistered falsifiable experiments with a shared seed, including ablation of an oscillatory precision layer, and it runs on consumer hardware.
- **Relation:** competes / collaboration opportunity for the in-silico programme (§8.9). It is the same engineering stance as FMT's AC work (process rather than model; LLM not central), but has no explicit self-model.
- **Touches:** §8.7–8.9, §7.2 (GNW and PP comparison), §10.1. FMT's distinguishing claim is that adding an ESM with self-referential closure (§3.4.3) is what turns access into experience, which is a direct ablation he could run.
- **Follow-up:** direct poster neighbour. Propose an ESM-on vs ESM-off ablation on his reproducible stack as a cross-theory test. Compare with crucible / mirror-box designs.

### P51 Hye-Eun (Selly) Yoon (Independent Researcher)
**"The Recurrent Causal-Process Theory of Consciousness: Minimal Causal Agency under Biological Constraints"**
UBCAT defines consciousness as minimal causal agency: orthogonal self-referential processing (Axis A) plus environment-mediated causal intervention (Axis B), under three biological constraints (homeostatic viability, metabolic economy, developmental implementability). It locates consciousness in the closure of recurrent sensory–interoceptive loops. A developmental stress test reinterprets early social behaviours as regulatory rather than conscious. It proposes Biological Signals of Consciousness as a complement to NCCs.
- **Relation:** competes. It shares FMT's self-referential loop closure but adds an agency axis and a biological-constraint filter. The biological constraints contradict FMT's substrate independence (§4.4) if read as necessary.
- **Touches:** §2.3 boundary problem, §3.5 graduated levels, §6.4 animal consciousness. Her developmental phases could be mapped to when ESM closure becomes available.
- **Follow-up:** cite in §7.3. Ask whether "biological constraints" are offered as necessary or only as realisation constraints for living systems.

### P52 Daan Schetselaar (Open University of The Netherlands)
**"Consciousness as the dissipation of information"**
Five postulates. The first defines consciousness as perception with no behavioural effects, which yields externally unobservable information. By analogy with thermodynamic dissipation, under behavioural flexibility some perceptual information is not transformed into action and stays inactionable, and that residue is consciousness.
- **Relation:** contradicts. Defining consciousness as causally idle information is near-epiphenomenalism, while FMT assigns the self-model two causal roles (§4.2.3) and treats the causal role as a requirement (§2.7). The parallel is that his "inactionable residue" resembles FMT's distinction between presence and access.
- **Touches:** §2.7 causal role, §4.2.3, §3.1.1 operational definitions.
- **Follow-up:** low-effort rebuttal sentence in §2.7 if cited. No contact priority.

### P57 Andrea Signorelli (Università di Trento)
**"A Review of Phenomenological Interview Methods in Contemplative Science"**
A methodological review of 141 of 602 studies. The field is 85.8% qualitative and dominated by semi-structured interviews, IPA and generic phenomenology. Micro-phenomenology, neurophenomenology and experimental phenomenology are rare, and only 17.3% of studies explicitly elicit specific experiential episodes.
- **Relation:** methodology for FMT's report-based predictions. **Microphenomenology flag.**
- **Touches:** Prediction 2 (§8.3) and Prediction 4 (§8.5), which both need episode-specific elicitation (identity content during ego dissolution; the moment of lucid onset). Her finding that episode-specific elicitation is rare marks a gap FMT's test protocols should fill explicitly.
- **Follow-up:** cite in §8.3 Testability as the reason to prescribe micro-phenomenological elicitation. Probably not the same person as Camilo Signorelli, the MoC7 host (unverified; see field guide).

### P58 Francesco Tormen (Italian Buddhist Union Research Center)
**"What Kind of Mathematical Object Could Mirror Non-Dual Awareness?"**
Extracts constraints for a mathematical model of "luminous", self-knowing awareness (rang rig) with no observer behind it and no higher-order act. Selfhood, the subject–object divide and thought can fade while experiencing remains (Metzinger's "minimal phenomenal experience"). It asks which mathematical objects could serve and which are excluded from the outset.
- **Relation:** potentially contradicts, and a formalisation opportunity. **Minimal-self / MPE flag.** FMT makes experience constitutively depend on the ESM's self-conflation ("the self-model confuses itself with the being it occupies"). Selfless MPE is therefore a direct challenge: FMT must say either that a residual, contentless ESM is still running (the closure without content), or that MPE reports are reconstructive. Reflexive self-knowing without a higher-order act matches FMT's self-referential closure (§3.4.3) better than HOT, which is a point in FMT's favour.
- **Touches:** §3.4.3 self-referential closure, §6.1 psychedelic ego dissolution, §9 open questions. The category-theory formalisation: a fixed point / terminal object as the formal counterpart of "appearing that knows itself" is exactly the kind of object he asks for.
- **Follow-up:** contact and offer FMT's closure-as-fixed-point as a candidate object meeting his constraints. Add MPE to §9 open questions if it is not already there.

### P59 Hongyu Chen (University of Toronto)
**"Sleeping, Dreaming, and the Boundaries of Consciousness"**
Argues against internalist accounts of dream immersivity (neural generative world-simulation; Revonsuo) and for an enactive account in which sleep is a distinct mode of embodied agency, selectively responsive to risks and opportunities. The altered organisation of agency is what makes imaginative capacities unusually immersive. Computational mechanisms render neural organisation tractable without settling what experience consists in (Chirimuuta).
- **Relation:** contradicts. FMT is a paradigm internalist simulation account: dreaming is the EWM/ESM running with reduced sensory permeability (§6.3).
- **Touches:** §6.3 dreams and lucid dreaming, Prediction 4, §4.1 process physicalism.
- **Follow-up:** direct poster neighbour (field guide). Rebuttal angle: FMT can absorb his point, since sleeper "selective responsiveness" is residual implicit→explicit permeability, without giving up internalism. Cite in §6.3 as the enactive objection.

### P66 Adrian Romeo (Nelson Mandela University)
**"The importance of the minimal self in Schechtman's narrative theory of identity."**
Argues that Zahavi's minimal self is a necessary component of narrative self theories. Against Schechtman, co-consciousness does not hold diachronically, and experiences do not persist through time except as memories and anticipations. The minimal self is offered as the experiencing subject her narrative account needs. (The abstract is truncated in the booklet.)
- **Relation:** supports FMT's layering in part. **Minimal-self flag.** FMT's ESM is a transient, momentary model, while the narrative self is content stored in the implicit self-model and replayed. That matches Romeo's "experiences do not persist except as memory/anticipation".
- **Touches:** §3.2 (ISM vs ESM), §3.4.4 temporal echo, §4.2.2 personal identity.
- **Follow-up:** cite as consistent with §4.2.2. Optional contact; philosophy audience, moderate value.
