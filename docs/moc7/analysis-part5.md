## Score 3

**P2 — Michiel van der Vlag (SDL Neuroscience)** — "Self-Reflexive Adaptivity: A Meta-Reservoir Framework for Minimal Autonomy and Emergent Self-Reference". Conceptual (not implemented) meta-reservoir: a higher-order dynamical structure regulating lower reservoirs near critical regimes; world and self representations said to emerge from that self-regulation, with measurable complexity predictions.
- Relation: competes + collaboration opportunity. Same ingredients as FMT but no implicit/explicit split; criticality in the requirement slot.
- FMT: §3.7, §3.4.3, §8.9.
- Follow-up: contacts.md #58 (FZ Jülich) mailed 2026-10-07, no reply. Confirm affiliation. Follow up with the §8.9 ESN finding (optimum ordered, not edge); propose meta-reservoir as closure testbed.

**P4 — Mario Martinez-Saito (Independent)** — "A quantitative measure of consciousness: The intermittent information content of self-relevant internal model variables". Active-inference foraging agent with a "d-self" (position, morphology, metabolic reserves) activated in "blips" only when needed; s(t) = share of free energy spent on self-model parameter complexity, → 0 during habitual movement.
- Relation: formalisation opportunity + competes (s(t) cannot separate implicit from explicit self-model).
- FMT: §3.1, §3.6/§3.6.1 (channel occupancy), §3.5, §8.9.
- Follow-up: contact; propose implicit-vs-explicit (closure) split of s(t) as in-silico test; cite.

**P31 — Damaris Kroeber (Independent)** — "Screening Theories of Consciousness: Inhabited Structure and Zeroth-Person Data". Consciousness as a running process recovering a picture of itself from within its own execution; sorts theories by running/inhabited vs extracted structure (objects, self, qualia). Deep-meditation "zeroth-person" states (self dissolves, presence remains) as screening criterion.
- Relation: supports §3.4.5–3.4.6 (processual, instantiation not description); challenges the self-model definition. **Key input for the minimal-self paper (AIW-280).**
- FMT: §3.1, §6.1, §8.3, §2.
- Follow-up: contact. Rebuttal line: ego dissolution changes content/permeability of the ESM; the loop persists as "presence".

**P11 — Edmundo Lopez-Sola (University of Oxford)** — "The Complex Brain Hypothesis: resolving the entropy-content conundrum in Minimal Phenomenal Experiences". MPEs show high entropy, low content; complexity set by the "grain of inference" indexes richness (fine for psychedelics, coarse for minimal experiences).
- Relation: supports + formalisation opportunity; grain parameter ↔ FMT permeability.
- FMT: §6.1, §5.3, §3.6.1, §8.3.
- Follow-up: cite; contact for joint framing.

## Score 2

- **P12 Joachim Nicolodi (Cambridge)** — "Conscious Access in Large Language Models". Chalmers-style credence analysis: access consciousness more likely than not in frontier LLMs. Competes (FMT criterion = closure, not availability). §8.7–8.8, §10.1, §7.2. Cite in §10.1.
- **P22 David Reichert (Google DeepMind)** — "Empirical science cannot decide between theories of phenomenal consciousness". Theories agreeing on physics can't be decided by measurement; those differing physically reduce to physical theories. Contradicts → rebut: FMT takes the functional horn via level-identity (§3.4.6); §8.6 already concedes "◐ not ●". §1.2, §10.2. Possible contact.
- **P10 Avishag Deborah Pelosi (Braude College of Engineering)** — "Toward a Generative Theory of Consciousness". Two axioms + five operations → 32 configurations on a 5-D hypercube; A2 = recursive self-articulation. Competing formalism; note for fmt_formal.
- **P18 Vasileios Basios (ULB)** — "Self-Reference, Entanglement, and Lived Experience: An Operational Probabilistic Approach to the Hard Problem". Kauffman eigenform X=T(X) in an operational probabilistic framework; lists "categorical unification" as open (**category-theory flag**). Formal precedent for §3.4.3 closure. Cite in fmt_formal.
- **P15 David Smith (Independent)** — "If You're Conscious and You Know It, Clap Your Hands: Constraining the Explanandum in Consciousness Research" (truncated). Fix the explanandum first via five characteristics (Presence, Situatedness, Temporal Immediacy, Significance, Evidencing); self-modelling should be judged by how well it explains experience, not used as its definition. Challenges FMT's definition — **input for AIW-278/279.** §2, §3.1.
- **P3 Jake Flower (Bristol / NHS)** — "Addressing the elephant in the room: Pure Awareness from a metaphysically neutral standpoint". Husserlian Pure Awareness; "neural correlates of Pure Awareness" make no sense. Minimal-self challenge; bundle with P31/P11 into one FMT position on contentless states.
- **P5 Davide Aldé (Monash)** — "A transcendental argument for the isomorphism between consciousness and its physical correlates". Supports §8.6, §2.4. Cite.
- **P9 Hans-Jürgen Graf Grote (Independent)** — "Who Needs Color Anyway? A Structurally Rich Translation into Monochrome". Colour-mixing algebra, structure-preserving colour→texture map (Achromator app). Supports §8.6; teaching-device candidate (AIW-95).
- **P14 Antonio Prisco (Bologna)** — "Mapping the structure of Qualia: a phenomenological study of conscious experience in visual agnosia". Interviews + Hebart odd-one-out similarity spaces in agnosia, IIT reading. Data for individual-level arm of prediction 5; IIT comparison (§7.2), §6.5. Contact.
- **P6 Emil Olsson (UC Irvine)** — "Re-identifying your own inner states: a decoding criterion where there is no referent". Subjects pick own decoded state among decoys; predicts chance in aphantasia while content still decodes. §8.6, §3.6. Cite, possible contact.
- **P13 Leyla Loued-Khenissi (Lausanne University Hospital)** — "Anterior insula encodes uncertainty selectively during conscious inference". §3.6, §8.1. Cite as "consistent with".
- **P33 Khanh-Dung Tran (Rayo Lab Pte. Ltd.)** — "Can Computation Substitute for the Substrate? Natural Experiments on Computationalism in ALS, Parkinson's disease, and Sleep." Aperiodic exponent data + breakeven-recall argument against substrate independence. Rebut in §4.4 (degraded medium ≠ alternative substrate; §3.7). Sleep data fits §5.3.
- **P27 Dita Maleckova (Independent)** — "Threshold Intelligence: The Daimonic as Ontological Model for LLM Intermediacy". Daimon agent on Claude; recursive self-reference without subject. §10.1, §8.7.
- **P29 Tonglin Yan (Paris-Saclay)** — "Belief Without Behavior: A Sally-Anne-Inspired Benchmark for Testing Theory of Mind Beyond Verbal Report in Vision Language Models". VLMs state false beliefs but don't act on them. Supports §8.8. Cite.
- **P30 Luca Dstasio (Michigan)** — "Visual Force Fields: Gaze-Driven Motion Adaptation and the Limits of Testing AST". AST gaze aftereffect replicates but confounded by head position. Note only (§7.2).
- **P35 Michael Timothy Bennett (Independent)** — "Is Anything Value Neutral?". Formal persistence ordering → representations made of valence. Competing formalism, low priority. §4.2.3, §2.7.
- **P37 Maria Antonietta Castaldi (Salerno)** — "Gradual Emergence of Consciousness: A Top-Down Hylomorphic Emergentist Neuroscientific Framework" (truncated). Four developmental stages of self-knowledge; parallels §3.5 / erweitert ladder, conflicts with §4.3 (no strong emergence). Note.
