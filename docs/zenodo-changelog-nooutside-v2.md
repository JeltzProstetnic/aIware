**v2 (2026-10-19)**

Supersedes v1.0 (2026-10-19).

*Corrections from a cross-check of all FMT publications.* A cross-check of the publications that draw on the Four-Model Theory, against the theory's master paper and against each other, led to these corrections in this book:
- Conscious content (Chapter 8). The book said that on the theory the explicit models are what experience is. It now says that experience is their content where it enters the closed loop of self-model and world-model, and that implicit content becomes conscious when it enters that closed loop. This follows the master paper's distinction between content that is accessible and content that is accessed.
- Model kinds (Chapters 3, 8, 13 and 15). The implicit/explicit split is now called two classes of models, world and self two scopes, and the theory's first open question asks whether all four model kinds are virtual, where it previously said four models.
- The record on the disk (Chapter 14). A paragraph was added on the remaining parts of the theory's sufficient conjunction. The requirement that a substrate actually perform open-ended computation asks for a realized trajectory whose states depend on each other through an open-ended rule, with no condition on the states being produced one after another in time; embodiment and the pain-pleasure axis are patterns among the same states. The record therefore meets the whole conjunction wherever the run meets it. The first horn is now said to make the theory take on a dependency the master paper already names as a conditional (Section 4.3), where the book said the theory did not state it.
- Stomach bacteria (Chapter 6). An unsourced clause saying that Palmer's 1954 study used a method later judged unsuited to finding the organisms was removed.
- References. The Four-Model Theory and the cosmology paper (SB-HC4A) are now cited by their concept DOIs (10.5281/zenodo.18669891 and 10.5281/zenodo.18698605), which always resolve to the latest version, in place of DOIs of superseded versions. A quotation from the master paper no longer carries a version label.

No argument, limit or conclusion of the book changed. One page was added.
