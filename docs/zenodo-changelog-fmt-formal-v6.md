**v6 (2026-10-19)**

Supersedes v5 (2026-10-07). Companion to the Four-Model Theory v21.

*Corrections from a cross-check of all FMT publications.* A cross-check of the FMT corpus against the master and against itself led to these corrections:
- Minimum set, not sufficient set. The four model kinds are now the minimum set the architecture forces, not a "minimum sufficient set" (abstract, Sections 2.3, 10). Section 4.5 adds the parent theory's sufficient condition, the conjunction of its Section 3.7.3 (the four model kinds under closure, free modeling, embodiment and at least one pain-pleasure axis), and says that this paper formalizes only the two necessary thresholds.
- Any consciousness, not human-type consciousness. The restriction to consciousness "of the human type" is removed from the necessity claims (abstract, Sections 1.2, 2.3, 4.5). The minimum-mass thresholds are lowest at the floor of minimal consciousness. The depth table of Section 6.3 adds a row for minimal consciousness below basic, and Section 6.5 says that simpler organisms support only minimal or basic consciousness.
- Explicit versus phenomenal. The mode axis now runs to "fully explicit", and activity above the explicit threshold is part of the running simulation, phenomenal where it enters the self-model's closed loop (Sections 2.1, 2.2, 2.5, 7.1). Section 3.5 separates total explicit content C_exp(t) from the closed-loop quantity C(t) ≤ C_exp(t); explicit content outside the loop is preconscious (Gruber, 2026, Section 3.4.6). The PCI prediction and Section 4.7 now refer to the closed-loop quantity.
- Self-referential closure. The fixed-point map Ψ is now defined on the joint state of self-model and world-model as their coupled update, and the fixed point is the state in which each enters the other's update (Section 6.4). The quotation from the parent paper is corrected to "the computation *is* the thing being computed" (its Section 3.4.3).
- Criticality as signature. The remaining passages that tied consciousness to criticality now speak of free modeling, and the operating band is "the signature band the theory associates with free modeling in cortex" (Sections 4.1, 4.3, 6.1). Module 3 of the build order is retitled *Criticality signature mapping*.
- Hard Problem. "Hard Problem dissolution" now reads "the theory's transformation of the Hard Problem", matching the parent paper (Sections 6.1, 6.4, 7.2, 9.1).
- Selection, not gating. Permeability is now described as a family of channel-specific selection mechanisms, not gating mechanisms (abstract, Sections 1.3, 3.2, 3.3, 6.2, 7.3, 8). Section 6.2 says that substrate processes are drawn into the generation of the explicit models, where it said they were transferred across the implicit-explicit boundary, and Section 2.5 speaks of the generation of the explicit model from the implicit one, where it spoke of implicit-to-explicit transfer.
- Predictions. The semidirect-product prediction for ego dissolution is now marked as a prediction of this formalization, not of the parent theory (Section 2.5). Controllable ego-dissolution content is now "one of the theory's most specific predictions", where it was "the most distinctive" (Section 5.1).
- Evidence for criticality. The ConCrit framework (Algom & Shriki, 2026) is described as proposing criticality as a unifying mechanism, and the Hengen & Shew (2025) meta-analysis of 143 datasets as consolidating the setpoint premise; neither establishes that σ covaries with conscious state (Section 4.2). The paper previously credited ConCrit with that result across 140 datasets. The DFA convention in Section 4.2 is corrected: α in 0.6–0.9 indexes long-range temporal correlations, α ≈ 0.5 uncorrelated fluctuations and α > 1 non-stationary drift.
- Build order. The modules of Section 8 are renumbered 1–6 to match the abstract. Computational validation is reported in the companion (Gruber, 2026d), and the specification does not depend on those results (Sections 8, 10).
- Smaller corrections. Section 2.5 refers to the parent theory's account of binding as an emergent property of the dynamics (its Section 5.1), and Section 3.3 cites the criticality-rhythm relationship as a limitation the parent paper lists (its Section 10.3). The permeability table is captioned Table 1 and the substrate equation of Section 4.1 is numbered [1]. Smithe is cited as St Clere Smithe (2024), and Wolfram (2002) is cited for Class 4. The Algom & Shriki reference now gives the first author's initial as I., the full title, volume 180, article 106483 and the DOI. The cosmology paper is cited by its concept DOI (10.5281/zenodo.18698605).

No formal module was added and no reference was removed.
