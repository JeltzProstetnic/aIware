## Key requirements
- **Portal:** Editorial Manager — https://www.editorialmanager.com/wspc-jaic/ (pre-register first).
- **Section (mandatory choice):** **"Assessing AI Consciousness"** — "Philosophical, mathematical, or theoretical studies… criteria, tests, indicator properties, or formal frameworks on machine consciousness." Natural fit.
- **Format:** MS Word OR LaTeX2e — WS style files downloadable from the submission-guidelines page. We have a LaTeX pipeline → use the WS LaTeX2e style.
- **Length:** NO published word/page cap. 14k words is plausible but unconfirmed. Optional: one-line pre-submission email to editorial office (Dr. Robert A. Harper, WS London) if certainty matters. Strategy = fire-and-forget → just submit.
- **Review:** SINGLE-BLIND → do NOT anonymize. Author identity visible to reviewers; ≥2 external reviewers; iThenticate plagiarism check.
- **Abstract ≤150 words. Keywords ≤6.** Title on front page.
- **AI-use disclosure REQUIRED:** state *which* AI tool and *how* used, in Methods/Acknowledgements/Introduction. No fixed boilerplate. (AI cannot be an author.)
- **Cover letter:** not mandatory but advisable — confirm originality + exclusivity + section fit + suggested reviewers.
- **Suggest up to 4 reviewers** at submission.
- **Fees:** FREE subscription route (paper goes behind paywall; corresponding author gets a free PDF). Optional OA = **US$2,000 + VAT** (CC BY). → submit FREE.
- **Exclusivity statement required:** manuscript not under consideration elsewhere.
- **Figures:** EPS preferred, ≥300 dpi, ≥3.5in wide, in-figure text ≥9pt Times Roman.
