# Journal of Sleep Research — author guidelines (read from the journal page 2026-11-09, S380)

Source: https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/page/journal/13652869/homepage/forauthors.html (Wiley; ESRS). Submission portal:
https://authors.wiley.com/journal/JSR (Wiley Authors; ORCID required). No submission fee. Editorial office: johanna.ell@esrs.eu.

**Free format submission.** One editable file holding text, figures and tables; required sections **Abstract, Introduction,
Methods, Results, Conclusions**; figures and tables with legends; any consistent reference style; supporting information in
separate files.

**Research Article:** 3,000–5,000 words *including abstract, keywords and main text*; abstract **unstructured** (confirmed by a
checkbox; no word limit stated on the page); **Data Availability Statement**; **IRB statement**; **at most 50 references**.
(Short Report: 2,000 words, ≤ 4 figures/tables, ≤ 15 references.)

**Title page:** co-authors' affiliation and institutional e-mail; statements as applicable — data availability, funding,
conflict of interest, ethics approval, patient consent, permission to reproduce material, clinical trial registration.

**Reporting checklist (mandatory, upload):** the one that fits best — ARRIVE, CONSORT, **STROBE** (observational), PRISMA,
SPIRIT, CARE, STARD; if none applies, say why.

**Cover letter (mandatory):** "printed on an official institutional" [letterhead]; significance, fit to scope, impact; 1–2
sentences each on *what is known* and *what the article adds*; confirmation the manuscript is not submitted elsewhere and
whether it is on a preprint server; **disclosure of AI use** (Wiley AI Guidelines).

**Suggested reviewers:** 5–10 independent reviewers with contact details, strongly encouraged.

**JSR Section checkbox:** Basic Science of Sleep (Animal) / **Basic Science of Sleep (Human)** / Sleep and Biological Rhythms /
Sleep, Insomnia, and Mental Health / Sleep and Neurology / Sleep Disordered Breathing / Sleep and Public Health / Sleep and
Pediatrics / Sleep Technology & AI / Not applicable.

**After acceptance:** licence CC-BY / CC-BY-NC / CC-BY-NC-ND or standard copyright; self-archiving allowed under conditions;
proofs within 48 h. Graphical abstract optional (.tiff/.eps, 50 × 60 mm, 300 dpi).

**Not on the page, checked elsewhere:** single- vs double-anonymised review — the S379 ranking recorded single-anonymised
(`docs/papers-book/venue-ranking-s379.md`).
