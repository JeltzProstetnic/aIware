# Minds and Machines — submission requirements (read 2026-10-25)

- **Format:** manuscript in Word; plain font (e.g. 10 pt Times Roman); automatic page numbering; no field functions.
- **Double-blind:** remove names, affiliations and identifying information from the manuscript and figures; **separate Title Page** with title, names, affiliations, corresponding author e-mail, ORCID, acknowledgements, disclosures, funding; avoid self-citation that reveals identity; suggest **up to six reviewers** in Editorial Manager.
- **Abstract:** 150–250 words, no undefined abbreviations or unspecified references. **Keywords:** 4–6.
- **Statements and Declarations:** under that heading (competing interests, funding, …); missing declarations → returned as incomplete. Author contributions go on the title page.
- **LLM use:** LLMs cannot be authors; document use in Methods or a suitable alternative section; pure AI copy-editing need not be declared.
- **References:** APA 7, name–year citations, alphabetical, full DOI links; only published/accepted works in the list.
- **Figures:** EPS preferred for vector, TIFF for halftones, MS Office acceptable; fonts embedded; files named `Fig1.eps` …; Arabic numbering, parts as lowercase letters (a, b, c); lettering Helvetica/Arial ~8–12 pt, consistent size; lines ≥ 0.3 pt; **do not include titles or captions within illustrations**.
- **Language:** British or American English, consistently.
