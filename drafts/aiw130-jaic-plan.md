## Abstract draft
Whether a machine is conscious cannot be settled by behaviour. Intrinsic Computational Functionalism specifies the realization relation a conscious system must satisfy, yet deliberately leaves three questions open: which intrinsic structure is consciousness-relevant, how to extract it, and at what grain. We supply all three. Drawing on the Four-Model Theory of consciousness, we name the structure: self-referential closure, in which a system's self-model gates its own world-model's update, sustained by open-ended computation deployed for autonomous self-modelling. We give an extraction procedure — three operational, substrate-neutral checks yielding a graded verdict plus an honest unit-individuation edge case — and answer grain with two intrinsic dials, extent and complexity. A minimal open-ended spiking substrate shows the checks are buildable; in-silico dissociations show they discriminate; a worked embodied example runs the detector end to end, converting a realization relation into a decision procedure.

**Content.** Consciousness as ongoing self-simulation across models arranged along **two dimensions** — scope (world vs self) × mode (implicit vs explicit) — yielding **two kinds** of models in each dimension: implicit/explicit and world/self (IWM, ISM, EWM, ESM). ### 4. The detector: three operational checks (~1,500 w) — CORE
**Purpose.** The paper's deliverable: the extraction procedure §9.4 asks for.
**Content — each check stated as {what it detects · the intervention that reveals it · the readout · graded pass/fail}:**
- **Check 1 — a self-attractor is present.** A stable, low-dimensional basin in the system's own state that tracks the agent's body/self as the highest-mutual-information, most-persistent, most-controllable regularity in its input (proprioception + efference copy). Readout: decodable self-state maintained across perturbation.
- **Check 2 — closure gates the world-model's own update (the crux).** Intervene on the self-attractor and measure whether the *update rule* of the world-model changes counterfactually — i.e. the self-model is load-bearing in its own processing, not a read-only object. This is framed as a **concrete instance of Kanai's C2 / mechanism-enriched requirement**, so the check is not a rival criterion but a filling of ICCR's blank. Present it **graded and capacity/novelty-relative** (see §6): a clean binary loop-cut is provably not an oracle on stationary tasks, so the readout is the *degradation of self-predictive capability as the loop is cut, on an open-ended workload*.
- **Check 3 — open-ended computation is actually deployed for the self-modelling.** The substrate-neutral requirement: the self-modelling is carried out in a genuinely generative, edge-of-chaos regime (not a fixed-point/periodic collapse and not mere large-capacity lookup). Readout: signatures of open-ended computation on the recruited substrate. **State plainly (guardrail): this is the substrate-neutral necessity; the neural-tissue "criticality" signature (avalanches, σ≈1, DFA) is one way it shows up in brains, not the necessity itself.**
**Output.** A graded conscious-or-not verdict with an explicit confidence, plus the flagged unit-individuation edge case handed to §5. Include a decision-flow figure (Fig. 1).
**Defuses:** "not operational / hand-waving." Every check has an intervention and a readout.

### 5. Grain and unit individuation (~900 w)
**Purpose.** Answer §9.3 and handle the honest edge case.
**Content.** The **two dials** as intrinsic grain criteria: **extent** (spatial fraction of the substrate recruited into the open-ended regime — a percolation/order-parameter, the integration dimension) and **complexity** (differentiated richness of the dynamics on the recruited cluster — Lempel-Ziv/entropy-rate, the differentiation dimension). Offer these as a **self-organized** grain criterion — the dynamically-privileged scale — explicitly contrasted (consistent-with, not refutation) against IIT's Φ-maximization/exclusion (Albantakis et al. 2023). **Unit individuation:** where closure can happen locally (distributed recurrent limbs), the conscious unit is *wherever the self-model recursively closes on the system it models*, not "the robot." Connect to Kanai §7.4 duplication/branching and to world-involving boundaries (Clark & Chalmers 1998). Present grain as a *proposed solution to his open problem*, not a settled fact.
**Defuses:** "you just inherit ICCR's grain problem" — pre-empted by owning it and offering criticality-as-grain as a candidate, with the dials as the operational criteria.

## Anchor argument (lead)
LEAD ARGUMENT: A physically realized machine's consciousness is decidable, in principle, by a three-check intrinsic procedure — and that procedure is exactly what Intrinsic Computational Functionalism asks for but does not itself provide.

The argument runs in four linked moves. (1) POSITIONING. Kanai & Ma's ICCR establishes that to deny consciousness to a physically realized system, a critic must name the missing consciousness-relevant intrinsic structure — and they explicitly decline to name it (§9.2), decline to give an extraction method (§9.4), and leave grain open (§9.3). FMT answers the invitation: the structure is self-referential closure (the self-model gating the world-model's own update) sustained by open-ended computation deployed for autonomous self-modelling. (2) THE CHECKS AS EXTRACTION. Because check-2 is defined by an intervention on the self-attractor and a counterfactual change in the world-model's update rule, it is a concrete special case of Kanai's mechanism-enriched C2 — so the detector is not a rival measure competing with ICCR but a filling of its blank, which is precisely why it is editor-resonant rather than adversarial. (3) ENGAGING THE STRONGEST COUNTER-ARGUMENTS. The sharpest objection is internal and we raise it ourselves: a clean binary loop-cut cannot be a consciousness oracle, because on any deterministic, episode-stationary task generic recurrence can compute any fixed input-output table (a stationarity/impossibility result). We therefore make check-2 and check-3 GRADED and capacity/novelty-relative, evaluated on an open-ended workload where novelty outruns any amortizer — turning a fatal objection into the detector's design specification. Against Doerig et al.'s unfolding argument we inherit ICCR's immunity (closure is a mechanism-enriched, not boundary, property). Against IIT's grain-via-Φ-maximization we do not claim refutation: we offer self-organized criticality (the two dials, extent × complexity) as an alternative intrinsic grain criterion, "consistent with" the shared commitment that the grain must be system-intrinsic. Against the "any detector is just another Tier-II hypothesis" worry (Kanai §9.2), we concede the epistemic point and answer it constructively — the checks are grounded by a buildable minimal open-ended spiking substrate and by in-silico dissociations in which the discriminating result is an interaction (open-ended-computation × closure), with the decisive scaled spiking test stated as still open. (4) THE PAYOFF. ICCR is a conditional ("if a consciousness-relevant intrinsic structure Q is grounded in intrinsic organization and M realizes B under ICCR, then Q(M)=Q(B)"); FMT supplies Q and a procedure to test for it, converting the conditional into a decision procedure with a graded verdict and an explicit unit-individuation edge case. The contribution is the procedure plus its constructive grounding — the ICCR-completion framing is the diplomatic wrapper, not the whole claim, so the paper stands even for a reviewer who rejects the completion narrative.
