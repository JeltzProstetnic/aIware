# Interior Fable Review — Greek (el)

This document consolidates the nine interior-review section files (el-001 through el-009) covering the full Greek manuscript. Across all sections, reviewers logged **90 findings: 5 High, 42 Med, and 43 Low**. The High findings are load-bearing terminology drifts and one dropped negation (a coined-term split φλοιικό→κορτικό αυτόματο, a meaning-inverting "unrecoverable"→«ανακτήσιμο ποτέ», appendix-lettering and computational-domain drift, and two broken cross-chapter callbacks). The Med findings are dominated by terminology and proper-name consistency, heading capitalization, number orthography, and quotation-mark style; the Low findings are register, typography, and optional-gloss polish. No meaning inversions beyond the one High negation, no dropped sentences, and no number/date errors were found outside the items listed.
