## Bottom line

Kanai & Ma build a **realization relation** (ICCR) that says *what must be preserved* for a simulated /
artificial system to share a biological system's consciousness-relevant organization. They are explicit
that their framework **(a) is silent on the hard problem, (b) is NOT a measure of consciousness, (c)
does not provide a method to *extract* the relevant intrinsic structure, and (d) leaves grain-selection
open.** They state (§9.2, §9.4) that a *substantive theory* must supply which structure is
consciousness-relevant, and that developing structure-extraction methods is "the next step."

**FMT is a filler for that exact gap.** FMT names a specific, intrinsic, intervention-defined structure
— **self-referential closure (the ESM recursively gating the world-model's own update) sustained at
criticality (Class-4 / edge-of-chaos)** — and proposes the two dials (extent × complexity of Class-4
recruitment) as intrinsic grain criteria. So the JAIC paper should be positioned as **completing ICCR,
not competing with it**: ICCR says *what realization must be preserved*; FMT says *which structure to
look for* and *how to detect/build it* (AIW-91 minimal substrate, AIW-104 embodied SpikingMCU).

This is the most editor-resonant framing available: the EiC literally published "someone must supply
structure X and an extraction method"; FMT supplies X and the method.

## What Kanai & Ma actually argue (compressed)

- **ICF (Intrinsic Computational Functionalism):** if consciousness is computationally constituted, it
  depends on computational structure the system realizes *in virtue of its own physical/causal-dynamical
  organization*, not observer-imposed labels. Two criteria: **C1** system-intrinsic instantiation, **C2**
  causal-dynamical organization under intervention.
- **Mechanism-enriched canonical structure (MCan):** extends their earlier Canonical Functionalism from
  boundary input-output to *internal* interventions + readouts. Consequence: lookup tables and unfolded
  feedforward nets pass boundary equivalence but **fail** mechanism-enriched equivalence; recurrent
  realizers pass. **The unfolding argument (Doerig et al. 2019) loses its grip against ICCR** (it proves
  boundary equivalence, not mechanism-enriched equivalence). Worked toy model §4.7.
- **ICCR (Def. 11):** M ⊨_ICCR B iff there exist *intrinsic* partitions + structure-preserving bijections
  over states/inputs/interventions/readouts satisfying physical realization, transition preservation,
  counterfactual preservation, boundary adequacy. **Conditional preservation theorem (Thm 1):** if a
  consciousness-relevant property Q is grounded in intrinsic causal-computational organization and
  M ⊨_ICCR B, then Q(M)=Q(B). Approximate version Thm 2.
- **Dialectical payoff:** "simulation ≠ instantiation" is true of static descriptions, arbitrary
  semantic maps, trace players, boundary-output devices — but to deny consciousness to a *physically
  realized* artificial system, the critic must **name the missing consciousness-relevant intrinsic
  structure**. Burden shift, not a proof that simulations are conscious.
- **Explicit limitations (the openings FMT walks through):**
  - §6.1 — "ICCR is silent on the hard problem."
  - §9.4 — ICCR is a *realization constraint*, **not a rival measure**; IIT is "more developed" at
    structure extraction; the next step is "methods for extracting effective intrinsic structures."
  - §9.2 — Tier-II constructs (Global Workspace, recurrent processing, higher-order, predictive/active
    inference, Butlin et al. 2023/2026 indicators) are "disciplined hypotheses" about where to look; the
    framework operationalizes the *search* but needs a substantive theory to fix the target.
  - §9.3 — **grain-selection problem**: an intrinsic partition doesn't pick a single scale; ICCR depends
    on the grain; ICF has **no maximization principle** (contrast IIT's exclusion). Open.

## Convergences (use "consistent with" framing — honest-convergence rule)

1. **Anti-both-extremes.** ICF rejects naive computationalism (observer-relative mapping insufficient)
   AND naive anti-computationalism / substrate chauvinism. FMT's substrate-neutral qualia (virtual, sim-
   internal) sit in the same slot: consciousness travels with realized structure, not the material.
2. **Intrinsic + intervention-defined.** Both locate consciousness in intrinsic causal-dynamical
   organization revealed by *intervention*, not by input-output behavior. FMT's core criterion —
   *closure gates the world-model's own update* — is exactly an intervention/counterfactual structure
   (perturb the self-attractor → world-model updating changes), i.e. a specific instance of C2.
3. **Boundary equivalence is not enough.** Kanai: MCan ≠ Can separates tables/unfolded nets from
   recurrent realizers. FMT: a lookup table / feedforward map has no self-referential closure → not
   conscious. **FMT's closure requirement is a concrete special case of Kanai's mechanism-enriched
   requirement.** The unfolding-argument immunity is shared.
4. **Agent-body-world boundary.** Kanai §7.4 accepts the target may be an embodied agent-world system;
   FMT's AIW-104 embodied SpikingMCU + unit-individuation is the same move made constructive.

## Where FMT ADDS (the contribution the JAIC paper sells)

1. **Names the structure.** ICCR is a blank realization relation over "whatever the consciousness-
   relevant structure is." FMT fills the blank: **self-referential closure at criticality.** This is the
   "substantive theory" §9.2 asks for.
2. **Supplies an extraction/detection procedure.** §9.4 flags the missing extraction method as the next
   step. FMT's detector = three operational checks (self-attractor / closure-gates-world-model-update /
   Class-4 dynamics) → a verdict — i.e. a structure-extraction recipe, grounded by AIW-91 (minimal
   critical spiking substrate, existence proof) and AIW-104 (worked example).
3. **Answers the grain-selection problem.** §9.3 is ICF's admitted weak point (no scale-selection
   principle). FMT proposes **criticality as the dynamically-privileged grain** — not IIT's exclusion/Φ-
   maximization, but a *self-organized* edge-of-chaos attractor — with the two dials (extent = fraction
   recruited into Class-4; complexity = richness of Class-4 patterns) as the intrinsic grain criteria
   §9.3 says "could play that role." This is a direct, citable answer to his open problem.
4. **Dissolves the hard problem** (qualia = virtual 6th sense, sim-internal). ICCR is silent here.
   **For JAIC, keep this as FMT's bonus, not the pitch** — the venue cares about the AC assessment
   criterion, not metaphysics; lead with the detector, mention the dissolution as a downstream payoff.

## JAIC positioning recommendation (Lane B / AIW-130)

- **Frame:** "ICCR specifies the realization relation for machine consciousness but deliberately leaves
  the consciousness-relevant structure, its extraction, and grain-selection open. FMT supplies a
  concrete, intrinsic, intervention-defined candidate — closure-at-criticality — plus a detector and a
  minimal buildable substrate." = *completing* ICCR.
- **Answer the closing challenge head-on:** Kanai's dialectical burden ("name the missing structure").
  FMT names it AND makes it constructively testable → converts his conditional into a decision procedure.
- **Diplomacy caution (EiC will read this):** position as instantiation/completion, NOT supersession.
  Do **not** write "ICF is incomplete, FMT is better." Write "FMT is the substantive theory ICF's own
  §9.2/§9.4 calls for." Cite Kanai & Ma 2026, Kanai & Ma 2026 (Canonical Functionalism, arXiv:2605.21506),
  Kanai & Fujisawa 2024 (NoC), Baltieri & Kanai 2025 generously.
- **Watch the grain problem cutting both ways:** if a reviewer doubts criticality uniquely fixes the
  grain, FMT inherits §9.3. Pre-empt: present criticality as a *proposed solution* to his open problem,
  with the two dials as the operational grain criteria, not as a settled fact.
