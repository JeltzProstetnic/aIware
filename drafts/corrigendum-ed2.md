# A Note on This Printing

**The Simulation You Call "I"** · First Printing

Thank you for reading an early copy. A few small corrections have been made since this printing — none of them changes the argument, but for the record:

- **Chapter 10** (conversion disorder): the callback should read **"Chapter 8,"** not "the last chapter."
- **Chapter 12** (brain vs. computer): the brain loses about **85,000 neurons a day** — roughly one per second — not "a million." (85,000 is the figure the book uses everywhere else.)
- **Chapter 13** (anosognosia): the cross-reference is **Chapter 6,** not "Chapter 8."
- **Chapter 17** (Leibniz's Identity of Indiscernibles): we first met it in **Chapter 13,** not "Chapter 1."

Everything else stands as written. Thank you for reading it early — that means more to me than a flawless copy ever could.

— Matthias
