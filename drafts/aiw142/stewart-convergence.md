## The claim, in five lines

Six theories (HOT, GWT, IIT, RPT, PP, AST) disagree about mechanism but share one success condition:
each must deliver phenomenal experience for a subject or it has changed subject (Part III). Read for
what each must *put to work* below its central claim, all six import the same seven things — substrate,
mechanism, temporal dynamics, a subject-and-agency side, semantic capacity, phenomenological character,
bounded unity (Part V). At the level of central claim, none states a constitutive mechanism from its
operation to phenomenal character, nor a subject (Part IV, "two uniform gaps"). He reads the subject side
as an "experiencer-receiver identification": the side that undergoes the experience and the side that
receives the cycle's own output as its next input are one architectural slot (Parts I, VII).
