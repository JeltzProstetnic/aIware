# Humor pass brief (MG, 2026-10-18 S336)

MG's instruction: *"add a humor pass … it must never change what the book is saying scientifically, but opportunistic
jokes should be made"*. His tone sample, verbatim — use it in Ch 2, at the rationals paragraph ("division can go on as
long as one likes, which is false for loaves and sheep"), smoothed into the book's prose but keeping its punch:
*"as much as it may hurt the sheep, you can cut it in half, and quarter it, and sell small portions of it on a market,
but at some point you end up with mush, and the market value suddenly drops to zero"*.

## The voice
- MG's first person. Dry, deadpan, Vorarlberg-dry — not Viennese-theatrical. Dark humor is welcome. Understatement over
  exclamation. The joke is usually a concrete, slightly absurd image of the abstract point (the sheep), a deadpan
  consequence taken one step too far, or an aside that deflates pomposity — including the author's own.
- Opportunistic: a joke goes where the material offers one. Typical density 1–3 per chapter, never more than one per
  section; some sections get none. Quality over count — a flat joke is worse than none.

## Hard constraints (a separate reviewer will check every one)
1. Never change what the book asserts, hedges, concedes, cites or quantifies. No claim may become stronger, weaker or
   different. No hedge ("conjecture", "proposal", "as far as anyone knows", "provided that…") may be removed or softened.
   No citation, number, date or technical term may change.
2. Add jokes; edit existing sentences only minimally to host one (e.g. append a clause). Do not rewrite arguments.
3. A joke must not be readable as an additional claim. If an image could be mistaken for an argument, cut it.
4. No jokes at the expense of named living scholars or real people other than the author. Historical figures may be
   treated with affectionate irony (Hobbes and the horn), never mockery of a person.
5. Obey `.claude/knowledge/prose-register.md`: no AI-tell register, no "not X but Y" reflexes, no throat-clearing,
   no self-narration ("as a joke…", "humorously"), no exclamation marks, no emoji, no winks to the reader.
6. Nothing in headings, references, or quoted material.

## Report
List every insertion: file, section, the full new or changed sentence(s), and — for any edited existing sentence —
the old text beside it.
