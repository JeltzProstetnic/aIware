## Cross-cutting principle (from holes 1–3)
- **The outside-the-universe view is not a perspective.** Under it, every long enough bit string contains every mind:
  it can draw no distinctions, so it is no standpoint. Triviality, observer-measure debts and counts over "the space of
  structures" belong to that view and fall with it.
- **A third-person view from WITHIN the experienceable universe is legitimate** — the stance we take to other brains,
  machines, or a file on a disk. MG verbatim: *"not an outside the universe view, only an outside a brain-like
  computation device as seen from within the experiencable universe"*. Ch 14's claim that a structure with FMT's
  properties has an inside is of this kind.

**Hole 1 — Ch 13 "The observer measure" + Ch 15 "A working arrangement" (05-ch12-13.md, 06-ch14-15.md).**
Scope the observer-measure debt to theories that count observers across a space no inhabitant can reach (canonical
Boltzmann with its thermal measure, Tegmark, many-worlds weighting, "everything occurs somewhere", cyclic cosmologies
counting observers across cycles). Theories applied from inside are tested by refutation against admitted input

cosmology paper owes a measure only insofar as it counts observers across cycles from outside).
