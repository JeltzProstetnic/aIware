## Part I — front matter, Preface, Ch 1–3 (`01-front-ch01-03.md`). Verdict: depositable after the blocker. ALL APPLIED S339 except #9 (MG).

1. **BLOCKER** §3.4 geometry. "When mathematicians in the nineteenth century dropped it, they obtained consistent geometries of curved space, with no physical application in view. General relativity later found that the space around masses is curved in exactly this way." → "When mathematicians in the nineteenth century dropped it, they obtained consistent geometries of curved space, and Riemann generalized them to spaces whose curvature varies from place to place. General relativity later found that spacetime around masses is curved in that more general way." (GR uses Riemann's variable curvature on spacetime; Lobachevsky/Gauss/Riemann did consider physical space.)
