# Interior Fable Review — Dutch (nl)

This document consolidates the nine interior-review sections covering the full Dutch (nl) manuscript, in order (source lines 1–2475: front matter and chapters through the appendices A–H). Across all nine sections the review recorded **80 findings: 5 High, 34 Medium, and 41 Low severity.** The 5 High-severity items are meaning-level defects — a deleted comparative claim and an invented testability claim (Section 1), broken coined-term/motif callbacks ("patchwork hologram", "fixpunt" vs "vast punt"), an event-horizon terminology split that undercuts a core argument (Section 6), and untranslated English appendix headings in a Dutch book (Section 9). The Medium tier is dominated by terminology drift on load-bearing defined terms (event horizon, computational class/domain, reducible/irreducible, permeability, DIS/DID) and pervasive closing-quotation-mark inconsistency (straight ASCII vs curly). The Low tier collects register, spelling, cross-reference casing, and minor nuance-loss items. High and Medium findings should be resolved before sign-off; several Medium terminology items require a single book-wide decision applied across chapters.
