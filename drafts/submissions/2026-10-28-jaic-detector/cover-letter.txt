Re: Research Article submission — "An Operational Detector for Machine Consciousness: Self-Referential Closure and Open-Ended Computation as an Intrinsic, Substrate-Neutral Test"
Author: Matthias Gruber (independent researcher; ORCID 0009-0005-9697-1665)

Dear Editors,

I am submitting the enclosed Research Article. The journal invites criteria, tests and formal frameworks for machine consciousness, and the paper delivers one: an operational, substrate-neutral test.

Behavior cannot settle whether a machine is conscious. The paper supplies an intrinsic, structural procedure that reads the organization of the system itself: three intervention-defined checks — a self-attractor, self-referential closure in which the self-model enters the world-model's own update, and open-ended computation actually deployed for the self-modeling — yielding a verdict with graded confidence and an explicit unit-individuation edge case. The procedure is grounded twice over: by a buildable, biologically close spiking substrate and by toy-scale in-silico dissociations, with the decisive scaled test stated as open.

The paper answers the three questions that Intrinsic Computational Functionalism (Kanai & Ma, 2026b) leaves open: it names a consciousness-relevant structure for that framework's §9.2, gives an extraction procedure of the kind its §9.4 names as the next step, and offers a candidate grain criterion for its §9.3. The three checks and their grounding are motivated on their own dynamical-systems terms.

Originality and exclusivity. The manuscript is original, has not been published previously, and is not under consideration at any other journal.
