# Cover letter

Dear Editors,

Please consider the enclosed manuscript, "More Than Computation, Made of Computation: Free Modelling as a Necessary Condition, and a Self-Model That Takes Itself for Its Bearer", for publication as an original research article in Minds and Machines.

The paper asks whether there is more to consciousness than computation and answers that the extra ingredient is itself computational: a second-order regime in which a system's modelling takes its own modelling as an object. It defends a necessity claim (consciousness requires free modelling, open-ended computation a system recruits for its own world- and self-models), states a falsifier for it (a matched-capacity feedforward comparator on novel input of growing compositional depth), places the account on the computationalism–implementationism axis, and answers the unfolding argument and the Kleiner–Ludwig no-go theorem from that position. The near-critical dynamics of cortex enter as a contingent signature of the requirement in one substrate.

The broader framework the paper draws on is available as a preprint (Zenodo, https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.18669891), as are two related preprints cited in the manuscript (https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21610993 and https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23594247). The present paper's central claim and argument do not duplicate them. The manuscript itself has been posted as a preprint (Zenodo, https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23637343). A separate manuscript by the same author on a different question (physical implementation of computation) is under review at Minds and Machines (MIND-S-26-01918); the two papers share no argument.

The manuscript has been anonymised for double-blind review; the title page with author details and declarations is uploaded separately. The use of a large language model is declared in the manuscript and on the title page. I would like the subscription publication route.

Yours sincerely,
Matthias Gruber
