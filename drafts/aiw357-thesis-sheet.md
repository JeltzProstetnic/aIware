# A Beginner's Guide to Thinking — thesis sheet (aIware → simbook brief)

This sheet settles the theory before the book moves to simbook (D-S363-9). Each thesis gets four things:
- a **canonical sentence** (the claim as MG holds it; the book tests and cites this, never a headline);
- a **"what I do not claim"** line;
- a **two-axis verdict**: *literature* (confirmed / contradicted / silent / not empirical) × *argument* (valid / premise contested / invalid);
- the **evidence to carry** (kept from the one-off Perplexity sweep, `docs/sources/perplexity-2026-10-26-beginners-guide-thinking/`) and **open items**.

Rule for simbook: a thesis is weakened only where evidence or a valid argument contradicts it. Where the literature is silent, the book proposes the test. MG's books are where each thesis comes from, not evidence for it.

All canonical sentences below are drafts. MG approves or rewrites each one.

## What the book is about (MG S363, verbatim)
> *"the book is about thinking and building efficient, useful and correct world models, and making yourself the yourself you want to be, and have success in life as you would define it, and learn to define your success as something achievable that wont kill you or drive you mad."*

**The nine theses are seeds, not chapters.** MG: *"the book will not have 9 chapters just because i started you with 9 thinking patterns on top of my mind."* simbook builds the structure from the book's purpose above. The theses are material for it.

## 1. The untrained threat system (2015 core, revised S363) — WALKED THROUGH S363

**Canonical (draft v2).** A threat system that never meets real danger is left uncalibrated. Lacking a scale, it overreacts to small threats; its unused readiness attaches to imagined and social objects. People who train against real challenge (martial artists, climbers, people in dangerous trades) acquire a rational relation to fear. The safe, civilisation-spoiled average more often develops anxiety disorders, phobias and aggressive disorders. Training changes this, over years, through three channels: the threat system is calibrated; the body's biochemistry is that of a trained, healthy body; and, foremost, the person gains the felt sense of being strong and having skills, which makes them self-confident and proactive.
**Origin.** *Emergenz* p.199 (2015: the "unterbeschäftigte Amygdala"). The book does **not** name the amygdala (MG S363: based on very old literature, not meant in the strict anatomical sense).
**Not claimed.** One brain nucleus as the cause. A daily fear quota. That danger is good in itself: the claim is about training against real challenge.
**Verdict.** Population gradient: literature *consistent* (GAD lifetime prevalence 5.0% in high-income, 2.8% middle-, 1.6% low-income countries; Ruscio et al. 2017, *JAMA Psychiatry* 74(5), doi:10.1001/jamapsychiatry.2017.0056; WHO World Mental Health surveys, 26 countries, n = 147,261). The diagnosis-rate objection is named once. Aggression: *consistent* (martial-arts interventions reduce aggression in children and youth; Harwood, Lavidor & Rassovsky 2017, *Aggression and Violent Behavior*, doi:10.1016/j.avb.2017.03.001). Self-efficacy channel: *confirmed* (Bandura). Calibration and displacement: *silent*, proposed mechanisms. Argument *valid*. Selection is answered by the within-person change MG observed.
**Evidence to carry.** The three sources above; Craske 2014 inhibitory learning (fits "years of re-training"); exercise and mood as the biochemistry channel (pick a meta-analysis when drafting).
**Proposed test.** Longitudinal: anxiety, aggression and self-efficacy measured in novices before and after years of training, against matched non-training controls.
**Decided S363.** T1-A keep aggressive disorders, via displacement. T1-B the 2026 learning/memory extension is dropped and replaced by the self-efficacy channel. T1-C keep the population claim with the WHO data.

**MG context, S363 walk-through (verbatim where quoted):**
- **No amygdala in the book.** *"dont mention amygdala, it was based on very old literature, i didn't literally mean amygdala in the strict sense."* The thesis is about the threat and fear system as a whole, not one nucleus.
- **Mechanism: both.** Calibration (a threat system that never meets real danger has no scale and overreacts to small threats) plus displacement (unused threat capacity attaches to imagined or social objects).
- **Observation beyond selection.** *"not only in students, also in co students at other martial arts schools and in general. young people gain selbstvertrauen and calmness from training. not just the threat system but simply just biochemistry of a healthy body alone. but foremost the sense of 'being strong' and having skills … mainly observation in students who started with 'labil' tendencies and ended up self confident and proactive."* This is a within-person change, which selection cannot explain. The book uses it as teacher's observation, not data, and names the longitudinal test.
- **Three channels named by MG:** (1) calibration of the threat system; (2) the biochemistry of a trained, healthy body; (3) foremost, the felt sense of being strong and having skills (self-efficacy).

## 2. The structure of knowledge is a discipline — WALKED THROUGH S363

**Canonical (draft v2).** Every field rests on primitives from the layers below it: logic, mathematics, physics, chemistry, biology, then the life and human sciences. The order is one of method (what you learn and check first), not of certainty: logic is the most general frame, but still a frame (*No Outside*). Lacking a lower layer's primitives costs you errors you cannot see from your own layer: biologists misusing statistics, energy claims that break thermodynamics, the 10 %-brain myth. Emergence does not loosen this. Lower layers do not let you derive the higher laws, but they constrain them: emergence adds to the layer below and never breaks it. Knowing what a thing emerges from is also what protects you against mystical explanations, such as quantum mysticism.
**Not claimed.** Reduction or deducibility (MG argues emergence). That logic is an absolute foundation. That nobody can contribute without every lower layer (Darwin did; the claim is about the cost). That the dependencies form a strict chain: they form a web, and the layers give the order in which to check.
**Patch error (unchanged).** *No Outside* §1.3–1.5: a formalism rescued by adding an object usually stays consistent but loses its reading. Test: does the added object predict something new (Neptune) or only absorb the discrepancy (Vulcan)?
**Verdict.** Literature: the layering is *not empirical*. The cost claim is *consistent* (statistical-error literature in biomedicine; prerequisite-knowledge research). Argument *valid*.
**Evidence to carry.** Anderson, "More Is Different", as consistent with MG; Feynman on knowing the name of something; one statistics-misuse source for the cost claim (pick when drafting).
**Decided S363.** Logic is the foundation of method, not of certainty. The claim is dependency plus cost. Emergence enters both as constraint and as protection against mysticism. The book gives a short list of primitives per layer.
**Primitives per layer (DRAFT for MG):**
- *Logic:* valid inference (modus ponens and tollens); necessary vs sufficient; all vs some; consistency within a frame; the limits of self-reference.
- *Mathematics:* orders of magnitude and scale; rates of change; probability and distributions; proof vs example.
- *Physics:* conservation of energy and momentum; entropy and the second law; dimensional analysis and scaling; measurement and uncertainty.
- *Chemistry:* atoms and bonds; thermodynamics vs kinetics (whether a reaction can go vs how fast); equilibrium; dose and concentration.
- *Biology:* evolution by selection; the cell and the genome; homeostasis and feedback; energy flow through organisms and ecosystems.

## 3. No mechanism type is excluded by the substrate (SPLIT from 8) — WALKED THROUGH S363

**Canonical (draft v2).** The brain's substrate excludes no type of mechanism ("Bible Code" pattern). "I am not a maths person" or "not a creative person" is therefore false as a claim about your hardware. What is missing is training or a tool. The limits are resources, not kinds, and external aids (paper, code, instruments) extend the resources. You can also install mechanisms in yourself on purpose (habits, heuristics, stances), as martial-arts training installs its automation layers. Built-in mechanisms such as perceptual illusions and the biases in the *Emergenz* table are not deleted that way: you run a correcting model beside them and learn when to trust which. Some built-in responses may be retrainable too, but do not bet on it. Choose your models by usefulness, and usefulness means predictive and practical success: a comforting model that predicts badly is not useful.
**Not claimed.** Unlimited capacity (limits are stated in *Emergenz* pp.101–102). That every model is true. That built-in biases can be switched off. A Church–Turing triviality: the payoff is about people's self-limiting beliefs, not about computability.
**Bible Code, both edges.** (a) In a substrate this rich a correlating neuron can always be found, so single-neuron findings (grandmother, mirror neurons) are weak evidence. (b) For the same reason, any mechanism can be built.
**Verdict.** Literature: the implementability core is *not empirical*. Practice and expertise research is *consistent*. The persistence of illusions despite knowledge is *confirmed* (Müller-Lyer). Argument *valid*.
**Evidence to carry.** Box; the map–territory lineage; Müller-Lyer persistence; an expertise/deliberate-practice source for "missing training, not missing type" (pick when drafting; avoid the contested 10,000-hour reading).
**Decided S363.** The payoff is both "no fixed type" and deliberate self-installation. Correcting models are added beside built-in ones (some retraining is possible but not to be bet on). Usefulness means predictive success.
**Open.** Retire the old headline "The brain can model everything".

## 4. AI may cause brain atrophy — WALKED THROUGH S363

**Canonical (draft v2).** Habitual offloading of thinking to AI may cause atrophy: certainly functional, possibly structural. AI differs from writing and calculators in what it takes over. Earlier tools offloaded storage and routine calculation. AI offloads the reasoning and judgement that produce insight, which is the part you need to keep. The hinge is the *without loss of insight* test. Offload what can go without loss of insight (lookup, arithmetic, formatting), because doing that by hand wastes resources and costs creativity. Never offload what produces insight (deriving, judging, structuring). The risk is largest for learners, who offload a skill before it has formed; experts lose less, but still lose what they stop using.
**Not claimed.** That atrophy is established or demonstrated. That AI use is harmful as such.
**Verdict.** Structural: literature *silent* for AI; the analogues are *consistent*, since use shapes structure (Maguire et al. 2000, *PNAS*, doi:10.1073/pnas.070039597; habitual GPS use and steeper spatial-memory decline over 3 years, Dahmani & Bohbot 2020, *Sci Rep*, doi:10.1038/s41598-020-62877-0). Functional: early and small (e.g. Kosmyna et al. 2025, arXiv:2506.08872, n = 54, EEG, not peer-reviewed), to be named as such. Argument *valid*. The *Phaedrus* objection is answered by what is offloaded.
**Evidence to carry.** The above; the sweep's offloading table with effect sizes; Risko & Gilbert 2016 on cognitive offloading; the "without loss of insight" test table; the learning-mode/execution-mode split.
**Proposed test.** Longitudinal structural and functional measures in heavy vs light LLM users, split by learners vs experts.
**Decided S363.** AI is different because it offloads the insight step. The insight test is the hinge. Learners are most at risk.

## 5. Every statement can be true and false — WALKED THROUGH S363

**What it is (MG S363).** *"t5 is a thinking pattern, not a universal truth about truth."*
**Canonical (draft v2).** For every statement you meet, and above all for your own convictions, search for the sense in which its contrary holds: a different frame, scale, time, meaning or quantifier. Often you find one, and then you understand the statement better than before. Sometimes the search fails, and that is a result too. "Contrary" means the opposite claim, not the formal negation. MG's personal discipline since age 15, from martial arts and Taoism (seed file 01).
**Background, not claim.** *No Outside* (truth relative to a reference system, §6.2) explains why the pattern so often pays off. The book may mention it in one sentence, but it does not have to defend a theory of truth.
**Not claimed.** A universal truth about truth. P and not-P in one frame (*No Outside* §8.6; seed answer 1). Universal dialetheism. That the search always succeeds.
**Verdict.** As a thinking pattern: literature *confirmed* in spirit (debiasing by considering the opposite: Lord, Lepper & Preston 1984; steelmanning; Bohr's "the opposite of a profound truth"). Argument: *valid* for a heuristic.
**Evidence to carry.** Lord, Lepper & Preston 1984; Bohr's actual wording (the sweep corrected our paraphrase); Zhuangzi and *Tao Te Ching* ch. 2; the Wason exercise.
**Decided S363.**
- **Headline kept** as the hook. The first paragraph says at once that this is a pattern for thinking, not a doctrine.
- **Seed exchange up front.** The entry opens with the counterexamples MG was challenged with and his answers. Hard cases (2 + 2 = 4, "I exist") are shown as places where the search is hard or fails, not as refutations.
- **Guard against false balance.** Finding the sense in which the contrary holds is not giving it equal weight (vaccines, climate).
**Open.** The "eight truths" (2015) are a separate source, not the origin (finding 13).

## 6. Discourse asymmetry — WALKED THROUGH S363

**Canonical (draft v2).** With humans, the first move is to see the matter from their perspective as well as you possibly can: assume they are right and find out why they would be. This is not believing every idiot. It is understanding the position. What you then do with that understanding depends on the context: attack the position more efficiently, or learn something new about the world. With AI the first move is the opposite: assume it is wrong. A human states what they believe. A language model produces plausible text with no stake in it, and it tends to agree with you. That difference is structural, not a matter of today's error rates, although future AIs may be better. Verify in proportion to the stakes, never zero. Always watch for signs of sycophancy. A system prompt that works against it should be standard.
**Not claimed.** That every human is right. A rule about truth probabilities. That the final standard of evidence differs: only the first move differs.
**Verdict.** Literature *confirmed* for both halves: the principle of charity and Rapoport's rules (Dennett); hallucination, sycophancy and automation bias. Argument *valid*.
**Evidence to carry.** The sweep's AI-evidence table and its human/AI stage table; MG's misreading registry as a case study, with its categories named as in the registry (finding 9); one sycophancy source showing the effect and a mitigation (pick when drafting).
**Decided S363.** Charity means perspective-taking, not belief. The use of it is context-dependent. The asymmetry is structural, with a caveat that future AIs may be better. The AI half adds verification by stakes, constant attention to sycophancy, and anti-sycophancy system prompts as standard practice.

## 7. Always a little content and a little discontent — WALKED THROUGH S363

**What it is (MG S363).** *"its a thinking pattern, a reminder you should call upon sometimes to check if you are already delusional or in a beginning depression. like the light switch trick for lucid dreaming."* A reality check, not a doctrine about happiness.
**Canonical (draft v2).** Now and then, check both at once. Am I content? I should be: I am alive, and whatever else is good. Am I discontent? I should be: what could I be doing that would advance me, what is annoying me now, what has potential? If one answer is missing, the check has done its job. No discontent at all is the early sign of delusion or stagnation. No contentment at all is the early sign of a beginning depression. Pure happiness as a goal stops progress, and pure discontent as a way of life leads into depression. *Eight Pattern Wing Chun Kuen* (2012), ch. 6: "Without progress there is no real happiness. And without happiness, progress is worthless."
**Not claimed.** That ordinary positive affect harms achievement. A causal theory of depression: the direction of causation is left open.
**Model.** The lucid-dreaming reality check (flip a light switch; in a dream it usually fails). A cheap, repeatable test that tells you which state you are in.
**Verdict.** Literature: hedonic adaptation is *confirmed*, and it is why aiming at total satisfaction fails. Reality testing in lucid-dream training is *confirmed* as a practice. Anhedonia and loss of interest as early depression signs are *confirmed* clinically. The check itself is *silent*: it is novel. Argument *valid* for a heuristic.
**Evidence to carry.** Hedonic treadmill; Lyubomirsky 2005, cited as not touching "pure"; a lucid-dreaming reality-check source (e.g. LaBerge); the PHQ-2 items as the clinical analogue of the check (pick when drafting); Yerkes–Dodson as analogy only.
**Decided S363.** A thinking pattern and self-check, in MG's two-question framing. "Pure" means as a goal.
**Waiting on AIW-356.** The now-only rationale (Perelman's mushrooms) is not yet included.

## 8. Run the expansive stance on purpose (SPLIT from 3) — WALKED THROUGH S363

**Canonical (draft v2).** "I can do anything, achieve anything, know anything" is a thinking pattern you can run on purpose, and it works. It is about potential, never current skill: "I can learn this, I can achieve this", never "I already know this, I already can". It removes the a-priori "I can't" and leaves your current skill to be measured. Run it when you decide whether to try. When you check the results, run its partner: everything, including this stance, may be a misconception (thesis 5 applied to oneself). These are two patterns used at different moments, not one belief held twice. Calibration comes from outside, because the gap between the self you believe in and the self you are cannot be measured alone (*Simulation* ch. 14; the three-part self-model already in *Eight Pattern Wing Chun Kuen*, 2012, ch. 5). Take feedback from people who gain nothing by flattering or hurting you; ridicule from experts counts most. Results that cannot flatter count more still: tests, competition, sparring. Run the stance on the attempt, never on irreversible stakes without the check.
**Not claimed.** That the stance creates abilities. Omnipotence. That you already have the skill.
**Verdict.** Literature: task-specific self-efficacy is *confirmed* (Bandura). Global self-esteem does not cause performance (Baumeister et al. 2003), which is *consistent*, since the stance is about potential, not self-worth. Dunning–Kruger is answered by the potential/skill split; cite the statistical critiques (Nuhfer; Gignac & Zajenkowski). Growth-mindset results are contested and are not the evidence base. The stance-plus-calibration package is *silent*: it is novel. Argument *valid*.
**Evidence to carry.** Bandura; Baumeister et al. 2003; the Dunning–Kruger critiques; the three-discrepancies model (2012 → 2026).
**Decided S363.** Potential, not current skill. Stance and doubt are patterns for different moments. Three guards: feedback quality, reality as feedback, no irreversible stakes without the check.
**Open.** The thesis must not open with the implementability sentence, which belongs to thesis 3.

## 9. A kind of universal scientist is still possible — WALKED THROUGH S363

**Canonical (draft v2).** The belief that a universal expert is no longer possible is more false than true. A kind of universal scientist is possible: someone who understands the frontiers and the basics of many fields well enough to judge and connect them, and who contributes where those connections give an edge. The method: learn the basics of each layer (thesis 2), go to the frontiers, skip the technicalities in between, and hold one or two clear goals. A technicality is useless if it does not change the answer to your question, its predictions, its error bounds or its safety. Education systems are built largely to produce workers, not scientists, and a measurable share of today's research output is paper-mill product, workforce training or industry support.
**Not claimed.** Frontier-level contributions in everything. Exemption from learning. That science as a whole is a paper mill: the claim is a share, with numbers.
**Verdict.** The institutional half: literature *confirmed* as a share (paper-mill and retraction data; incentive studies such as Smaldino & McElreath 2016). The polymath half: *silent*, with historical and living precedent. Argument *valid, premise contested* (division of labour).
**Evidence to carry.** The sweep's paper-mill and retraction table, with numbers; the sweep's rule for useless technicalities (adopted); living, ordinary generalists rather than only Leibniz-class outliers (find when drafting).
**Decided S363.** The thesis is about understanding everything and contributing in places. The critique is stated as numbers and shares, about incentives, never people, and without tone. The examples are living, ordinary generalists.
**Perelman (MG S363).** *"perelman has nothing to do with universal genius he is the opposite probably, but he is an interesting story."* He is not an example for thesis 9. He is a candidate story elsewhere, most naturally under defining your own success (indifference to prizes and posts; "I am picking mushrooms", as reported). Accuracy guard: he was employed at Steklov. MG's own path is not used as an example.
