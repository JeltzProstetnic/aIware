## 1. The Class 4 defining criterion is stated two incompatible ways — **rule this first**

The paper says both of these:

| where | text |
|---|---|
| `:76` | "**Irreducibility is the defining criterion of the class**; its other characteristic features accompany it." |
| `:138` | "Universality serves here as *evidence* of irreducibility … rather than as the class criterion itself" |
| `:394` | "the defining criterion is **irreducibility with universality**" |

**Why it is not cosmetic.** §2.2 re-files Rule 30 as Class 4, and the whole taxonomy leans on that
re-filing. `:88` already concedes Rule 30's irreducibility is a conjecture. Under `:394`'s conjunctive
version, Rule 30 also needs **universality**, which rests on Wolfram's Principle of Computational
Equivalence — so its membership becomes conjectural twice over, on two independent conjectures, and
§2.2 no longer carries the weight §3 puts on it.

- **(a) — proposed.** Irreducibility is the criterion; universality is evidence for it. Repair `:394`
  to match `:76`/`:138`. Rule 30 needs one conjecture, not two, and the two-thirds of the paper that
  already say this stop contradicting the other third.
- **(b).** Irreducibility *with* universality. Then `:76` and `:138` must change instead, and §2.2
  must openly state that Rule 30's membership rests on two conjectures — which weakens §3's
  elimination argument at its base.

⚠ Either way, `:394`'s cross-reference to "Section 2.3" is wrong — the material is in §§2.2, 2.4, 2.5.
That gets fixed regardless.

## 2. `:707` — "structurally guaranteed"

- **Proposed:** "…is not merely possible but structurally favoured — any sufficiently complex Class 4

- **Cost of leaving it:** it is the strongest claim in the Discussion and the one a referee reaches
  for first, and the paper cannot defend it.

## 10. `:445` — the information-causality substitution

The paper puts "the locus capacity in place of a transmitted-bit count" and says "the quantum ceiling
follows." But information causality bounds the *receiver's gain relative to classical communication in
a task protocol*; a static capacity bound on jointly accessible information is a **different and
weaker** statement — LHV models are also capacity-bounded and sit at S ≤ 2. The concession at `:447`
covers the single-locus decoding postulate but **not** this substitution.

- **Proposed:** name it as a third assumption alongside jointness and minimality — "the substitution of
  a static capacity bound for the protocol's transmitted-bit count is an additional assumption, not a
  consequence" — and say the Tsirelson recovery is conditional on it. Cheap, and it converts a hole
  into a stated limitation.
