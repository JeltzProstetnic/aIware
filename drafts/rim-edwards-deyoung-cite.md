### Drafted sentence (full version)
> This is consistent with recent large-cohort evidence: Edwards and DeYoung (2026), analyzing two nationally representative U.S. longitudinal samples (NLSY79, *n* = 11,914; NLSY97, *n* = 7,008) with sibling-based within-family controls, found that specific cognitive abilities carried independent predictive weight for educational, occupational, and income outcomes over and above general intelligence *g* — on the order of 30–40% of *g*'s predictive importance for the main socioeconomic outcomes. Their sibling-controlled design rules out shared family confounds, and while their analysis remains within the classical g-plus-group-factors psychometric tradition rather than a recursive or relational model, its central result — that structure below *g* carries real, independent signal — converges with the present framework's claim that a single aggregate dimension is an incomplete summary of intelligence.
