# IIT: Integrated Information Theory — 40 claims

Format: ID [level] text. Arrows (X -> Y means Y holds in virtue of X) listed at the end.

- E1 [L4] All features of a consciously perceived stimulus should be maximally decodable from posterior areas, regardless of task (4, 12, 13)
- E16 [L4] Across various possible spatiotemporal grains to study the brain, the one constitutive of consciousness should be the one achieving maximal phi (3)
- E15 [L4] Reduction of transmission in callosal fibres should lead to a point at which one consciousness splits in two. (Split brain) (1)
- E14 [L4] Loss and recovery of consciousness should be associated with the breakdown and recovery of the capacity for information integration (5)
- E13 [L4] When c-e powers of neuronal populations are incongruent with c-e powers of the PSC, corresponding stimulus should remain unconscious (9)
- F3 [L5] Artificial systems, even if functionally equivalent to human brains, won't be conscious (no high PHI) (8)
- E12 [L4] Similarity judgments between experiences should map onto similarity between CESs, as reconstructed from brain states (2)
- E10 [L4] There should be content specific synchronization between high-level and low-level areas in the posterior cortex, regardless of the task (12, 13)
- E9 [L4] Network that corresponds to content of C should be actively maintained for the duration of that content in C (13)
- E8 [L4] Expert meditators experiencing 'pure presence' should exhibit decrease in gamma band, especially in posterior areas (11)
- E7 [L4] Experience of 'pure presence' should exhibit largely inactive posterior cortex (11)
- E6 [L4] Deactivating already inactive neurons of retinotopic cortex should remove part of spatial experience (10)
- F1 [L5] Conscious perception overflows report (1, 14)
- E5 [L4] Artificial activation of neurons near the then terminus in a directed grid should result in perceiving a stimulus as having occurred earlier (15)
- E4 [L4] Modulation of synaptic strength/excitability of neurons in directed grids should change properties of phenomenal flow regardless of activity levels (15)
- E3 [L4] Duration of the extended present should be proportional to the number of neurons constituting a directed grid (15)
- E2 [L4] Local strengthening or weakening of horizontal connections in topographic areas should lead to a local distortion of experienced visual space (10)
- D1 [L3] Posterior cortex, which has the right architecture for high integration and specialization, is the likely candidate for NCC (4)
- D7 [L3] Changes in the strength of connections within PSC should be associated with changes in experience, even if neural activity does not change (8)
- D6 [L3] Brain Complexity measures sensitive to specialization / integration should correlate with consciousness (5)
- D5 [L3] Inactive neurons can contribute to consciousness (1)
- D4 [L3] Horizontal connections within topographically organized visual areas would be needed to experience visual space (6, 7)
- D3 [L3] Spatial structure of experience should map into features specified by neurons organized in non-directed grid-like networks (6)
- D2 [L3] Temporal structure of experience should map into features specified by neurons organized in directed grids (15)
- C1 [L2] Lattices of specialized + integrated units are well-suited to achieve high phi (7)
- C9 [L2] PSC needs to be both specialized and integrated to achieve high phi (5)
- C8 [L2] Only distinctions with c-e powers compatible with the c-e powers of the whole PSC contribute to C (8)
- C7 [L2] Similarity between PHI structures should map into similarity between experiences (8)
- C6 [L2] Every distinction that is consciously experienced should be manifested in the CES for as long as it is experienced (8, 12, 13)
- C5 [L2] All units in the PSC must have the potential to be active (1)
- C4 [L2] C is extremely rich (i.e., highly structured) (14)
- C3 [L2] Entire PSC (not only task-relevant activity) supports CES (= experience) (1)
- C2 [L2] Type of experience (e.g., spatial vs temporal) should depend on the architecture of the substrate specifying the CES (1)
- B1 [L1] CONNECTIVITY: the connections between units of a PSC determine whether it can support high phi (8)
- B4 [L1] Spatiotemporal grain that matters for consciousness is the one that maximizes phi (8)
- B3 [L1] Functional (extrinsic) equivalence doesn't entail phenomenal (intrinsic) equivalence (8)
- B2 [L1] ACTIVITY: changes in the state of the PSC units determine changes in CES (and therefore in experience) (8)
- A1 [core] Based on introspection, every experience is essentially intrinsic, specific, unitary, definite, and structured = Operationally, the PSC must be a maximum of irreducible, specific, intrinsic c-e power, supporting a CES (1, 8)
- F2 [L5] Because mental action is not essential to consciousness, in a MIB task actively looking for a stimulus should not facilitate conscious access (10)
- E11 [L4] Similarity metrics  should be higher for content specific activation than task-specific activation in some posterior areas (12)
