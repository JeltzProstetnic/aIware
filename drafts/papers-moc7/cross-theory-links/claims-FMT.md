# FMT: Four-Model Theory — 64 claims

Format: ID [level] text. Arrows (X -> Y means Y holds in virtue of X) listed at the end.

- A1 [core] Free modelling: open-ended (Class 4-capable) computation, recruited by an autonomous system for its own modelling, is necessary for consciousness. Near-criticality is the signature it leaves in neural tissue, not the principle.
- A2 [core] Recurrent non-linear dynamics of sufficient complexity host a second computational level: persistent activity patterns that interact and compute over one another, individuated by their mutual effects rather than by the units that carry them.
- A3 [core] Phenomenology is real and physical, and it is that second level's modelling closed on itself: the self-model and the world-model each entering the other's update rule (self-referential closure). An identity, not a law: phenomenality is closure. Argued for, not derived.
- B1 [L1] Any conscious system must model world and self, each at a stored (implicit) and a generated (explicit) level: two kinds of model on two axes (IWM, ISM, EWM, ESM). Scope is a nesting (ESM within EWM); four is the minimum, not the count, and the four are not modules.
- B2 [L1] Real/virtual split: the implicit models are stored in substrate connectivity and are never directly conscious; the explicit models are generated from (constituted by) them plus current input. Constitution, not data transfer.
- B3 [L1] Qualia are level-constitutive properties of the running explicit models: real at the computational level, absent from the substrate-level description.
- B4 [L1] Self-referential closure is what distinguishes conscious from non-conscious computation: a simulation that is merely monitored or broadcast, without closure, has no inside.
- B5 [L1] Each necessary, neither sufficient: free modelling (capability, free instantiation, autonomous recruitment) and the four-model architecture. Sufficient: four models under closure + free compute + embodiment + at least one pain-pleasure axis.
- B6 [L1] A system has phenomenology or none. Above that, consciousness is graded on two axes, richness of content and depth of recursive self-modelling: minimal (the floor), basic, simply, doubly, triply extended. Points on a continuum, not discrete stages.
- B7 [L1] Some implicit-to-explicit permeability is forced (zero permeability = no explicit models) and its variability is the default: permeability is a family of modulable boundary properties with local and global modes.
- B8 [L1] Forking: because the explicit level is computationally open-ended, several self-model configurations (and offline runs such as recall or modelling another agent) can run on one substrate.
- B9 [L1] Redirection: the explicit self-model is an input-driven generator; when its normal self-referential input is disrupted it latches onto whatever input dominates.
- C1 [L2] The explicit models read and write the high-dimensional implicit models through a low-rank channel. The channel's current occupancy is the working model (Arbeitsmodell) = working-memory content = the global workspace; it is not what constitutes experience.
- C2 [L2] Arousal sets the channel's capacity, attention selects its occupancy, working memory is that occupancy. Attention is selection into the channel; closure is the pass back over what it holds, so the two dissociate.
- C3 [L2] For accessed content (in the self-model's closed loop), access and phenomenality are two level-descriptions of one process; accessible content, implicit or explicit, is preconscious until it enters that loop. Recursion depth, not a broadcast stage, turns presence into reportability; nothing overflows.
- C4 [L2] Minimal and basic consciousness: closure at the lowest recursion depth (minimal: the present moment, no discernible qualia; basic: discernible content). Phenomenal without global broadcast; reportable only via a rung above.
- C5 [L2] Temporal echo: recursion under closure smears content over time, and this smearing constitutes experienced temporal flow.
- C6 [L2] Substrate independence: any physical system meeting the sufficient conjunction (B5) is conscious, whatever its material; an engineered pain-pleasure axis is compatible.
- C7 [L2] Source distinguishability: composing the explicit models must not destroy the distinguishability between live and offline (forked) runs that the source pathways carry. No reality tag is posited; source attribution is the fragile term.
- C8 [L2] Meta-problem: the explicit self-model cannot model its own generating substrate, which produces the intuition that consciousness is mysterious (a triply-extended operation).
- C9 [L2] Binding is dissolved, not solved: the model kinds are poles of one shared substrate and its dynamics, so no separate binding mechanism is needed (one field, not necessarily consistent contents).
- C10 [L2] The relational structure of a qualia space is a relational invariant shareable across individuals; the absolute substrate state realizing a quale is idiosyncratic and is transferred only by instantiation, never by description.
- C11 [L2] Closure is an efficiency advantage inside a cost window, not a capability necessity: it pays where a system must model many model-bearing agents and itself in an open-ended environment, and ample compute can replace it on bounded problems.
- C12 [L2] In neural tissue free modelling usually shows as a near-critical signature (sigma ~ 1, DFA alpha 0.6-0.9, power-law avalanches). The signature is contingent: neither necessary nor sufficient for consciousness, so criticality data cannot disconfirm A1 in either direction.
- C13 [L2] Localization asymmetry: implicit models localize to structural connectivity; explicit models are distributed, transient dynamics that are not localizable to a region.
- C14 [L2] The explicit self-model and the explicit world model are architecturally separable model kinds and should be independently disruptable (functional selectivity, not focal modules).
- C15 [L2] What counts as a self model: a model of the system itself, however simple, representing its states, geometry and capabilities in proportion to what the system has. A position estimate alone is not one: a SLAM robot vacuum fails (its 'self' is a position, no implicit/explicit split, a fixed algorithm, no free compute recruited).
- D1 [L3] In cortex the thalamocortical relay is the leading realizer candidate for the low-rank channel; it is not the only one (olfaction reaches cortex without a thalamic relay and is conscious).
- D2 [L3] General anaesthesia abolishes experience by channel failure while the wired (implicit) layer stays intact.
- D3 [L3] In brains, permeability is set by neuromodulatory systems (serotonergic, GABAergic, dopaminergic and others), varying independently across sensory channels, regions and histology.
- D4 [L3] Psychedelics raise permeability globally (intermediate processing stages become explicit); anosognosia is a local permeability block (the ISM holds the deficit, the ESM does not receive it).
- D5 [L3] Candidate mechanism: cortico-basal ganglia-thalamo-cortical loops evaluate candidate model updates against dopaminergic prediction error before they enter the explicit simulation.
- D6 [L3] Self-model differences concentrate along a self-referential-to-sensorimotor gradient of cortical processing; default-mode co-activation is a correlate, not a localization of the ESM.
- D7 [L3] In REM sleep the substrate approaches but does not reach waking criticality; lucid onset is the ESM crossing from basic to simply extended consciousness.
- D8 [L3] A synthetic system meeting the sufficient conjunction of B5 (its pain-pleasure axis may be engineered) would be conscious. Not currently testable.
- D9 [L3] Current LLMs, including scaffolded agent loops with memory, are not conscious: they lack self-referential closure, self-produced update of their implicit models and autonomous recruitment of open-ended computation (a globally available, reportable workspace without closure is mimicry).
- D10 [L3] A Turing-universal but heteronomous system (externally clocked and programmed, e.g. a laptop) is not conscious: nothing recruits its universality for autonomous self-modelling.
- D11 [L3] Animal consciousness forms a gradient set by whether an organism sustains self-referential simulation through free modelling, not by phylogeny or behaviour (corvids qualify without a neocortex). Floor candidates: fish, amphibians, possibly insects; below closure, no experience.
- E1 [L4] Psychedelics at sub-ego-dissolution doses improve explicit awareness of deficits in anosognosia, dose-dependently and temporarily, correlated with complexity increases over the lesioned hemisphere; strongest in disconnection-type, absent in destruction-type cases.
- E2 [L4] In dissociative identity disorder, representational dissimilarity between alter states is greater in self-referential than in sensorimotor processing (a gradient), alter-specific, reproducible, and distinct from role-play controls.
- E3 [L4] Verified lucid-dream onset shows phase-transition dynamics (critical slowing, discontinuous onset) originating in self-model networks before spreading. A signature prediction: its failure falsifies D7/C12, not A1.
- E4 [L4] Processing guided by the implicit models (e.g. hippocampal semantic parsing, word prediction, representational plasticity) continues under consciousness-abolishing anaesthesia while the explicit models are suppressed. Disconfirmer: implicit learning abolished at BIS 40-60.
- E5 [L4] Disrupting the self-modelling loop itself (targeted disruption beyond metacognitive read-out, decision criteria held fixed, no-report designs; in silico closure-on/off ablation) degrades experience, not merely report or confidence. Falsifier: loop disruption leaves no-report markers of experience intact. Metacognitive disruption alone does not test closure (FMT predicts experience survives it; report may fail via long-term memory transfer).
- E6 [L4] A causal perturbation can degrade the explicit self-model while sparing the explicit world model, and the converse (selective ESM disruption ~ depersonalization; selective EWM disruption ~ fragmented world with intact self).
- E7 [L4] Permeability rises monotonically with 5-HT2A agonism (3+ dose levels; Lempel-Ziv complexity, 5D-ASC, content transfer), and global (psychedelic) and local (anosognosia) permeability changes have distinguishable neural signatures.
- E8 [L4] Thalamocortical effective connectivity breaks down at propofol-induced loss of consciousness, with structural connectivity preserved.
- E9 [L4] States intensifying phenomenal self-modelling (meditative absorption, jhana) shift cortical dynamics toward the near-critical signature. Signature-level: cannot disconfirm A1.
- E10 [L4] White-box trace: in inspectable systems (connectome, weights, running dynamics), an input traced to a self-model component that enters the world model's update rule and is entered by it marks consciousness, reportable or not (the non-report signature of minimal consciousness). Falsifier, where such systems also report: a traced signal reaching the closed self and world models without no-report markers of experience of it, or such markers for a signal never reaching the self model.
- E11 [L4] Human refuters of the bridge (phenomenality is closure): content traced into the closed self and world models while every no-report marker and the later report show no experience of it; or robust no-report markers of experience where closure is absent. The second is hard to establish in vivo: opening the loop in one place relocates it.
- E12 [L4] Shortening the temporal echo makes experience frame-like ('single frames, single thoughts, step by step') in every modality and in thought together, and the size of the frame-like report covaries with the shortening of the neural autocorrelation window (ACW, resting and task EEG/MEG). Test: placebo-controlled THC, two doses, crossover, eye tracking; TMS over V5 as a vision-only control with no global ACW change. Falsifier: frame-like reports without ACW shortening; ACW shortening without frame-like reports; or reports confined to vision or locked to saccades (the collicular route). Discrete-perception accounts tie frames to a slower perceptual cycle (alpha peak frequency down); FMT predicts alpha frequency need not move. Proposed S338, not yet in the FMT master.
- E13 [L4] In-silico echo cut: in a closed architecture whose self-model no longer receives its own state from the previous frame (within-frame closure kept), the self-model loses its representation of its own elapsed time and of the order of its own past states, while the world model's representation of external time survives. Three arms: closure + echo, closure + echo cut, recurrence without closure; linear decoding from self-model versus world-model units, plus a report of own elapsed time. Falsifier: the echo-cut arm decodes its own elapsed time from self-model units as well as the full arm does. Tests representation, not experience. Proposed S338, not yet in the FMT master.
- F1 [L5] During ego dissolution under salvinorin A, the reported identity content ('what I became') tracks the most salient content in the environment, even content with no contingency with the subject's heartbeat, breathing or movement; controlling what is salient predicts and directs it.
- F2 [L5] Within a modality, unsupervised label-free alignment of qualia-similarity structures generalizes across individuals, while a decoder of one brain's absolute realizing state fails on another's. The committed claim is the dissociation.
- F3 [L5] Working-memory capacity co-varies with the rank of the read/write channel and not with neuron count or brain volume; capacity scaling with brain volume across species would falsify the derivation.
- F4 [L5] Metacognitive efficiency (M-ratio, read-out by the self-model) is largely decoupled from first-order sensitivity (d', world-model discrimination). No priority claimed for the regularity.
- F5 [L5] Under perturbation of the substrate or of communication between levels, source attribution degrades before phenomenal experience does: dreaming is experience without source attribution, and dream recall frequency predicts dream-reality confusion.
- F6 [L5] Novel-input probe: a fixed lookup fitted to a system's past input-output behaviour fails on novel input wherever the system is conscious. Disconfirmer of A1: robust consciousness where a fixed lookup reproduces behaviour on novel input.
- F7 [L5] In model systems, the advantage conferred by explicit self-modelling is small on simple individual tasks and grows steeply with task sociality and compositional depth (efficiency at matched budget, not capability necessity).
- F8 [L5] If that selection mechanism is miscalibrated: too permissive gives positive symptoms, too conservative negative symptoms, noisy cognitive symptoms; 5-HT2A agonists should disrupt salience filtering, measurable as reward-valence violations in ratings of psychedelic imagery across doses.
- F9 [L5] Rising permeability exposes intermediate processing stages in hierarchical order: phosphenes and form constants, then complex imagery, then dream-like scenes (psychedelics, hypnagogia).
- F10 [L5] Functional-twin refuter of the bridge, tested in silico: no return-free system reproduces every function attributed to phenomenality (reports of presence, opacity about its own processing, reports of ineffability, flexible behaviour guided by valence) within the same resource bound as the closed system. Measured so far: return-freedom costs 71.5-74.0% of a model connectome, five to six times what relocating closure costs.
- F11 [L5] Simulated generation display: a display of a plausible build-up of 'your processing', contingent on and preceding the participant's own percept or decision, lowers reported mystery about that experience, whether or not the display is veridical. A fourth arm, a contingent and preceding but uninterpretable display (a leak analogue), leaves mystery unchanged or raises it. Arms: preceding display; the same display mistimed or uncorrelated; no display; uninterpretable display; a veridical sub-arm uses a real-time readiness-potential BCI. Falsifier: no drop relative to the mistimed display at matched belief in it; the veridical display beats the fake one; or the leak arm lowers mystery as much as the coherent display. Proposed S338, not yet in the FMT master.
- E14 [L4] Misbinding and field unity have separate causes: a focal manipulation of feature binding (right intraparietal rTMS, attention load) shifts guessing-corrected conjunction errors without changing the unity of each object and scene or global complexity markers; graded propofol sedation degrades the whole field without raising conjunction errors at matched feature accuracy, and never yields features experienced outside one field. Proposed S339, not yet in the FMT master.
- E15 [L4] Synchrony is a signature, not the pairing mechanism: in-phase versus anti-phase gamma stimulation (tACS) between colour- and form-coding cortex at matched arousal changes detection and clarity, not which features are paired; refuted if anti-phase raises conjunction errors at matched feature accuracy. Proposed S339, not yet in the FMT master.
