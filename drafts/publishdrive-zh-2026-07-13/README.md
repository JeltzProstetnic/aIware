## Why PublishDrive (not KDP, not direct-to-mainland)
- **Amazon has no mainland-China storefront** since 2023 — KDP's Traditional-only beta reaches Hong Kong / Taiwan / diaspora, not Simplified mainland readers.
- **Mainland platforms** (WeChat Read / iReader / Dangdang / JD / Duokan) are **closed to foreign self-publishers** — they ingest only through licensed mainland publishers, and a mainland sale needs a state-issued ISBN/书号 (NPPA) + content censorship review that an individual foreigner cannot obtain.
- **PublishDrive is the one legitimate aggregator with a mainland channel** (Dangdang/JD via its importer relationship), *and* it distributes to the global diaspora stores in a single upload:
  - **Diaspora reach (reliable):** Apple Books, Google Play Books, Kobo, + ~400 stores — sells Simplified Chinese to overseas readers (US/Canada/Singapore/Malaysia/Australia/Europe). Google Play & Apple Books are both dark inside mainland China, so this is diaspora, not mainland.
  - **Mainland attempt (best-effort lottery):** enable Chinese distribution → PublishDrive submits to the Dangdang/JD importer + censorship review. **No acceptance guarantee, no rejection feedback, up to ~6-month wait.** Treat it as a free lottery ticket, not a channel you can count on.

## Steps
1. Create / log in to a **PublishDrive** account (publishdrive.com).
2. New book → upload `book-manuscript-zh.epub` + `cover-zh.jpg`.
3. Paste fields from `metadata.txt`. Language = **Chinese (Simplified)**. ISBN = free PublishDrive ISBN (none reserved). Price ≈ **US$4.50**.
4. **Distribution:** select Apple Books + Google Play + Kobo (diaspora) AND tick the **China (Dangdang/JD)** channel to fire the best-effort mainland attempt.
5. Submit. Diaspora stores go live in days; the China channel may take months or silently fail.

## Alternative / supplement (no aggregator cut)
- **Google Play Books** direct + **Apple Books** direct — free, fast, strong diaspora reach (both need their own partner accounts). Use instead of, or alongside, PublishDrive if you'd rather not give an aggregator its cut.
- **KDP Chinese (Traditional) Beta** — optional extra HK/TW slice, but needs a *Traditional-script* .docx; our edition is Simplified, so skip unless you want to convert.
