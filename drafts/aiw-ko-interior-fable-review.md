# Interior Fable Review — Korean (ko)

This document consolidates the nine interior-review sections covering the full Korean (ko) manuscript, in order (source lines 1–2475: front matter and chapters through appendices A–H). Across all nine sections the review recorded **89 findings: 5 High, 41 Medium, and 43 Low severity.** The 5 High-severity items are all broken defined-term / callback chains where a load-bearing coined term appears under two different names: the graduated-consciousness level "단순 확장 의식" rendered "단일 확장 의식" (Section 4), the titular "복제 문제" split into 복제/복사 (Section 5), the researcher "바크(Bak)" collapsed to the Korean surname "박" (Section 6), the three-way "automaton" rendering 자동자/오토마톤/오토마타 (Section 7), and "operational knowledge" defined as "조작적 지식" but used everywhere as "운용 지식" (Section 8). The Medium tier is dominated by terminology drift on defined terms (automaton class names, PCI 섭동/교란, event horizon 사건 지평선, psychedelic 사이키델릭/환각제, split-brain 분할뇌/분리뇌), transliteration inconsistency of researcher names ('t Hooft, Bekenstein, Per Bak, Brunswik), half-width vs full-width parentheses around model abbreviations, and mixed quotation-mark typography. The Low tier collects register leaks, spacing, numeral-style, and minor nuance-loss items. High and Medium findings should be resolved before sign-off; several Medium terminology and parenthesis-width items require a single book-wide decision applied consistently across chapters.
