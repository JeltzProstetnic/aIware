CORRECTED 2026-08-05: pages 8, 10 and 12 printed upside down in the first version.
The two faces of one half-leaf need OPPOSITE rotations, because flipping a leaf about
a top spine turns it through 180 degrees. Front side is now upright throughout, back
side rotated - the standard pattern for a top-bound duplex booklet.

PRINTER SETTINGS  (get these right or the back sides land upside down)
  Paper .............. A4
  Sides .............. Print on both sides  /  Duplex
  Flip on ............ LONG EDGE   (also called "Portrait" or "Book" binding)
  Scale .............. 100 % / Actual size   — NOT "fit to page"
  Booklet mode ....... OFF.  N-up / multiple-pages-per-sheet OFF.
                       The imposition is already done in the PDF.

AFTER PRINTING
  3 sheets come out. Stack them in order, sheet 1 on top.
  Fold the whole stack once across the middle, short way, so the fold
  is at the TOP. Staple twice in the fold.

  Result: a 12-page A5-landscape booklet, spine at the top, pages flip up.
  Open it and you get a vertical spread — one page above the fold, one
  below, both the right way up.

IF THE TEST PRINT IS WRONG
  Back sides upside down relative to fronts
      -> your printer duplexes on the SHORT edge. Switch it to LONG edge.
  Fronts and backs on the wrong halves of the sheet
      -> same cause, same fix.
  Everything shifted / white border too big
      -> scaling was on. Set 100 % / actual size.
  Order scrambled
      -> booklet mode was left on in the printer driver. Turn it off.

  The small grey "p.N · sheet i front/back lower/upper" marks on the test
  version tell you exactly which panel landed where.

KNOWN LIMITS
  - Source images are 1664x928. At 190 mm wide that is 222 DPI effective,
    below the 300 DPI a print shop would want. Fine on a laser printer for
    a test; not yet right for the MoC7 run.
  - The QR on the back cover points at the Zenodo CONCEPT DOI
    (doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.18669891), which always resolves to the NEWEST
    version. That is v13 today, which still describes five principles.
    If v14 (three principles) has not landed before MoC7, either publish it
    first or repoint the QR at the v13 version DOI.
