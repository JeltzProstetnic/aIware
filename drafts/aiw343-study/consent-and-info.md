# Participant information and consent (draft v0.1)

**Perception and beliefs about the mind — a short survey**
Conducted by the Alpine Institute for Advanced Research. Principal investigator: Matthias Gruber, research@matthiasgruber.com.

**What is this about?** We study how ordinary perceptual experiences relate to people's views about the mind. To keep the results unbiased, we will explain the exact question once data collection has closed; the explanation will then be posted at https://alpine-institute.eu/study/.

**What will I do?** Answer questions online for about 25 minutes: about you (age band and gender only, no names), everyday perceptual experiences, your views about the mind, and a few standard personality and experience questionnaires. Some questions concern unusual experiences (for example, hearing things while falling asleep) or thoughts about death. You can skip any question.

**Is it voluntary?** Yes. Nobody — including your employer and the researchers — will know whether you took part. Taking part or not has no consequences of any kind. You can stop at any time. Pages you have already completed are saved anonymously as you go, but incomplete responses are never analysed and are deleted when data collection closes.

**Is it anonymous?** We do not collect your name, email, IP address or any device identifier in this survey. Answers are reported only in summaries. We ask only for a coarse age band and gender, so that no combination of answers points to a person.

**Optional second part.** At the end we ask whether we may contact you for a second session. If you agree, a separate form asks for your email address. It is stored separately, cannot be linked to your answers, is used only to send that invitation, and is deleted when recruitment ends (at the latest after 12 months).

**Risks and benefits.** No risks beyond everyday life are expected. If any question troubles you, you can contact Matthias Gruber, research@matthiasgruber.com; in acute distress: Telefonseelsorge 142 (Austria), Die Dargebotene Hand 143 (Switzerland, Liechtenstein). There is no payment; you contribute to basic research on consciousness, and you will receive the summary of results on the Institute website.

**Data protection.** Controller: Matthias Gruber, research@matthiasgruber.com. The survey is hosted by the Alpine Institute for Advanced Research as processor. Full privacy notice: https://alpine-institute.eu/study/privacy/. We treat your survey answers as anonymous: the survey stores no name, email, IP address or date, and published data are coarsened so that no answer can be traced to you. Some questions touch on religious and philosophical beliefs and on health; where the GDPR applies, the legal basis is your explicit consent (Art. 6(1)(a) and Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR), which you give by ticking the box below. The email address you may give in the separate part-two form is personal data; the legal basis there is also your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). The survey site sets only two technical cookies, a session cookie and a security token against forged form submissions; there is no tracking, no analytics and no third-party content. Data are stored on servers in the EU (self-hosted at survey.alpine-institute.eu); survey answers are kept for 10 years under good scientific practice, and published only in de-identified form (free-text answers are never published). Because nobody can tell which answers are yours, they cannot be withdrawn after you submit them. You may complain to the Austrian Data Protection Authority (dsb.gv.at).

**Ethics.** The study is anonymous and involves no intervention; under Austrian law it does not require review by an ethics committee.

**Questions?** research@matthiasgruber.com
