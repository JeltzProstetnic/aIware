Dear Editors,

I submit the Viewpoint "Does near-criticality set the level of consciousness? An architectural prediction, and how to falsify it" for consideration in *Cortex*.

The Viewpoint argues that the level of consciousness is set by the organisation of a system's models of the world and of itself rather than by proximity to criticality, and derives a prediction that separates the two: under a perturbation of the self-model that spares the substrate, first-order sensitivity (d′) and metacognitive sensitivity (meta-d′) should move orthogonally, where a scalar account predicts covariation. It specifies the within-subject design that would decide the question and the result that would refute the account. It reports no new data.

A preprint of this manuscript is posted on Zenodo (https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23594247, CC BY 4.0). The manuscript is not under consideration elsewhere.
