## Recommendation

**First choice — Bob W Copenhagen Østerbro.** The only real lodging within 10 minutes of the venue.
At €854 it is **€554 cheaper** than the sauna option, and the missing sauna costs €8–20 a visit at
Øbro-Hallen, 15 minutes away. ⚠ Caveats: **no reception at all** (contactless self check-in), and the
cheap tiers are non-refundable.

**Backup — Charlottehaven.** Take it if the in-house sauna is non-negotiable. Østerbro aparthotel,
free sauna + tylarium + pool + gym from 05:30, **free cancellation on every rate**, 28 minutes on
foot or 10 by bike. Minimum stay 2 nights.

**If price dominates:** Hotel Nora at €775 for 6 nights, 20 minutes' walk — but one room left.

## Three things that decide the timing

1. **Efterårsferie: YES.** Københavns Kommune's official ferieplan gives autumn holiday 2026/27 as
   **12.10.2026 – 16.10.2026** — your conference week exactly. ⚠ **But the effect is mild**: a
   domestic family-leisure bump on a shoulder-season base, not a squeeze. October is one of
   Copenhagen's cheaper months. The real crunch was the *previous* week (IBA, ~5,000 delegates,
   4–9 Oct). The only mid-size in-week event is Intergraf, 14–16 Oct at Bella Center, which pulls on
   Ørestad stock, not Østerbro.
2. **The organisers do list hotels — but there is no deal.** MoC7 Practical Information names ~22
   hotels in three price bands with the advisory *"most of them quite busy, so we advise you to make
   reservations well ahead."* **No block booking, no discount code, no negotiated rate.** The list is
   chosen for *metro* access, not walking — its closest entry is 27 minutes on foot.
3. **The market is soft but the good rooms are not.** 264 properties available for 11–16 Oct, yet:
   Hotel Nora 1 left, A&O 2 left, Bob W's refundable tier 2 left, Rye115 already gone on the 11th.

**⇒ Book a free-cancellation rate now rather than waiting for 8 September.** Take the **6-night**
option refundable and drop to 5 later at no cost. Note the **Fri→Sat night is disproportionately
priced everywhere** (Nora: DKK 1,330 against its DKK 890 blended rate), which is the real argument
for deciding the Friday-evening-vs-Saturday departure deliberately rather than by default.
