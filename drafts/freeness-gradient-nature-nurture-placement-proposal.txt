PLACEMENT PROPOSAL — the freeness gradient, and the nature/nurture rationale
S299, 2026-08-10. Decision 6 from docs/pending-s298-followups.md.

THE HEADLINE: these are two different KINDS of content and should not go to the
same place. Treating them as one decision is what made it look hard.


1. FREENESS GRADIENT -> THE FMT MASTER PAPER (v15, as an AIW-193 member).
   Not the book first. Not the blog first.

Your criterion: chain-of-thought is free WITHIN a learned landscape; "really
free" escapes the basin and re-authors the model space. The apex case is
projective self-commitment — binding future-me to X while the local incentive
gradient points elsewhere — and it comes with a falsifier already attached:
detune the self-reference and you should get a myopic agent.

Four reasons it belongs in the paper.

(a) It is a DISCRIMINATOR, and discriminators are what a theory paper is for.
    The most common objection to any computational consciousness theory in 2026
    is "an LLM doing CoT is already doing your free modelling." The master paper
    currently has no answer to that. It needs one.

(b) MoC7 is a room that will ask exactly this and little else. v15 is cut just
    before Copenhagen. That is the audience the paragraph is built for.

(c) The falsifier makes it paper-grade rather than essay-grade. A claim with a
    stated way to fail is publishable; the same idea without one is a vibe, and
    the book is where vibes are allowed to live.

(d) The book does not need it. Lay readers do not raise the CoT objection.

   THE RISK, and I would rather name it than have a reviewer name it:
   "escapes the basin" and "re-authors the model space" are currently metaphors,
   not operationalizations. If we cannot define basin-escape non-circularly
   before the v15 cut, it goes in as a STATED OPEN CRITERION plus the falsifier,
   not as a claim — or it slips to the book. That is my recommended fallback,
   not a reason to delay the decision.


2. NATURE/NURTURE RATIONALE -> THE COMPANION PAPER (AIW-140), rationale section.
   Not the master. Not the book.

This one is not theory, it is experimental justification: the paragraph that
licenses front-loading structural priors ("the minimal rig funds the maximal
one"), plus the mandatory scope sentence — the minimal rig proves closure-play
CAN consolidate an imitation, NOT that an open-ended self bootstraps the
coordinate map.

Three reasons.

(a) Rationale for a design choice is methods-section material by nature.

(b) Putting it in the master would import an experimental constraint into a
    theory paper. That is precisely the drift AIW-191 exists to stop.

(c) The home already exists — AIW-141(b)'s ruled-in rationale section. No new
    structure needed.

   The honest-boundary sentence is NOT optional and must travel with it. It is
   the sentence that stops the companion overclaiming, and it is cheap.


3. BOOK AND BLOG GET THE FREENESS GRADIENT SECOND.

Once it is in the paper it is very good blog material (AIW-122) and Ed-3 book
material — projective self-commitment is vivid and lay readers will like it.
Paper first, though: if the blog runs it first, the paper claim reads as
derivative of our own marketing.


WHAT I NEED FROM YOU: yes/no on (1) and (2), and on the fallback in (1) if
basin-escape will not operationalize in time.
