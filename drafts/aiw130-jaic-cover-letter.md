# Cover letter — Journal of Artificial Intelligence and Consciousness

**To:** The Editor-in-Chief and Editors, *Journal of Artificial Intelligence and Consciousness* (World Scientific)
**Section:** "Assessing AI Consciousness"
**Re:** Research Article submission — *"An Operational Detector for Machine Consciousness: Self-Referential Closure and Open-Ended Computation as an Intrinsic, Substrate-Neutral Test"*
**Author:** Matthias Gruber (independent researcher; ORCID 0009-0005-9697-1665)

Dear Editors,

I am submitting the enclosed Research Article to the **"Assessing AI Consciousness"** section, whose remit — "philosophical, mathematical, or theoretical studies … criteria, tests, indicator properties, or formal frameworks on machine consciousness" — the paper answers directly: it delivers an operational, substrate-neutral **test**.

Behaviour cannot settle whether a machine is conscious. The paper supplies an intrinsic, structural procedure that reads the organization of the system itself: three intervention-defined checks — a self-attractor, self-referential closure in which the self-model enters the world-model's own update, and open-ended computation actually deployed for the self-modelling — yielding a verdict with graded confidence and an explicit unit-individuation edge case. The procedure is grounded twice over: by a buildable minimal open-ended spiking substrate (an existence proof) and by in-silico dissociations, with the decisive scaled test stated as open.

The paper answers the three questions that Intrinsic Computational Functionalism (Kanai & Ma, 2026b) leaves open: it names a consciousness-relevant structure for that framework's §9.2, gives an extraction procedure of the kind its §9.4 names as the next step, and offers a candidate grain criterion for its §9.3. The three checks and their grounding are motivated on their own dynamical-systems terms.

**Originality and exclusivity.** The manuscript is original, has not been published previously, and is not under consideration at any other journal.

**AI-use disclosure.** As stated in the Acknowledgements, large language models (Anthropic Claude, via Claude Code) assisted with literature triage, prose drafting, figure alt-text, and prototyping the in-silico check code reported in the companion; all theoretical claims, the detector design, and the final text are my own.

**Shared-framework disclosure.** This paper and a companion manuscript on consciousness in biological brains both cite my full Four-Model Theory preprint (version 20, doi:10.5281/zenodo.23477765; concept DOI 10.5281/zenodo.18669891) as the complete framework. The two make distinct central claims on distinct primary evidence — this one an operational substrate-neutral detector grounded in a spiking-substrate design and in-silico dissociations; the companion an account of near-critical cortical dynamics on neural evidence — and agree on what is necessary (free modelling, stated substrate-neutrally here and through its neural-tissue signature there). I disclose both for transparency.
