## ⚠ READ THIS FIRST — the novelty check came back occupied, and it changes an obligation

**"Motivation as resource allocation" is occupied three times over, and one occupant is inside the ability
literature itself.**

- **Kanfer & Ackerman (1989)**, *Journal of Applied Psychology* 74, 657–690 — motivation as the allocation of
  limited **attentional resources** during skill acquisition.
- **Shenhav, Botvinick & Cohen (2013)**, *Neuron* — expected value of control; control allocated by
  expected-value optimization.
- **Kurzban, Duckworth, Kable & Myers (2013)**, *BBS* — felt effort as the **opportunity-cost readout** of
  allocation.

All three confirmed against publisher/index records by the proposing agent.

**What remains separable, and the agent judges it enough:** all three allocate **momentary attention or
control intensity within a task**. None allocates **offline simulation time in an explicit self-model across
developmental time**; none re-types the intelligence construct; and none derives psychometric measurement
consequences (Brunswik attenuation, consistency-over-level) from the policy nature of the thing.

**⭐ And the Kanfer & Ackerman adjacency is a gift rather than a threat: Ackerman is already the paper's chief
interlocutor**, so the reframe *completes a line the field's own measurement tradition started*. The proposal
makes a §3.4 subsection naming all three **mandatory** — the paper must cite them before a referee does.

## 1. The thesis change, in one sentence

The current paper claims the field **omitted a component**. The Platinum edition claims the field
**mis-typed the components** — Performance is the only one that is a capacity in the psychometric sense,
Knowledge splits along a physical structure/process boundary, and Motivation is not a component of the loop
at all but the **allocation policy over it** — and that single re-typing **derives four standing
embarrassments the field currently only reports**: motivation's resistance to trait measurement,
Brunswik-symmetry attenuation, the education-moves-Gf/WM-training-doesn't contrast, and consistency-over-level.

## 2. The strategic change

**What the paper is for.** It stops being "add motivation to CHC" and becomes **the bridgehead of the FMT
grounding programme into psychology** — the first paper where FMT's physical criterion does work inside a
psychometric argument.

**It also fixes a structural weakness the review already found.** The current paper concedes at `:157` that
PPIK has interests in its formal structure while denying it at `:35`. *"You forgot it"* therefore fails.
*"You typed it as a trait and it is a policy"* does not — **because PPIK types it as a trait too**.

**Audience.** *Journal of Intelligence* stays the target, with two camps now addressed by name: the
Ackerman/Wittmann measurement tradition, and the network/mutualism camp. **The paper does for motivation what
mutualism did for `g`** — explains why a construct that does not refer still predicts.

**Newly willing to say:** the constructs are mis-typed, argued entirely through the field's own witnesses
(Meehl 1978; Borsboom et al. 2004 on validity-as-existence-and-causation; Eronen & Bringmann 2021; van der
Maas et al. 2006); age-normed IQ defines away the raw-decline question (Salthouse 2009 **with** the
Schaie-group rebuttal stated, no side taken — *the disagreement is the exhibit*); `g` may be a network
property rather than a thing.

**Must stop saying:** "the field forgot/excluded a component" as the thesis (it survives only as the §2
*symptom* the re-typing explains); every residue of "uniquely human"; "until now"; the Conclusion's
flattening of §3.4's concession; and **"psychology is pseudoscience" in any paraphrase** — the identical
content routes through the named witnesses or it does not appear.

**Title — committed, not offered:**
> **"A Schedule, Not a Substance: Motivation as Allocation Policy and the Mis-Typed Components of Intelligence."**

## 6. The predictions — all nine survive, none cut

- **Become derivations:** **P7** (Brunswik aggregation) — a state- and situation-bound policy *must* attenuate
  under narrow single-occasion measurement, so attenuation stops being an excuse and becomes a theorem.
  **P8** (consistency over level) — a trait predicts level, a policy predicts consistency; the Wittmann
  variability datum the paper reports becomes the datum the reframe **derives**. These two now jointly carry
  the unity claim.
- **Strengthen:** **P5** — an engineered exploration drive is a *fixed* policy over a fixed distribution; the
  M the loop needs reallocates as the self-model grows. **P6** — grading as policy intervention.
- **Unchanged:** P2, P3, P4, P9.
- **Weaken (wording only):** **P1** must be restated to avoid the `:317`/`:333` early-IQ blocker; under the
  policy reading its operative predictor is childhood *engagement consistency*, folding it toward P8.

## 7. Risks, ranked — what a hostile referee attacks first

1. **"This is Kanfer & Ackerman / EVC / Kurzban relabelled."** The worst one. Cite all three in §3.4 before
   any referee does and state the separation exactly. **Unearned novelty here sinks the paper.**
2. **Internal inconsistency after a partial sweep** — any surviving "three components" hands the referee the
   paper contradicting its own thesis.
3. **"Anti-psychometrics."** Mitigated only by the named witnesses and the mutualism template: the paper must
   explain **why the mis-typed constructs still predict**, not merely that they are mis-typed.
4. **"A policy is unfalsifiable — everything is allocation."** Answered by P7's *r* ≥ .50 / *r* ≈ .30
   criterion and P8's consistency-vs-level contrast, both flagged **in the text** as the reframe's exposure.
5. **Wittmann & Süß (1999).** ✅ **Resolved S312** — the claim belongs to the Singapore 2002 ICAP paper and
   has been reattributed. Note the proposal's own observation: **the reframe reduces dependence on it**, since
   a policy account needs the *variability* findings rather than a motivational path coefficient.
6. **The death test as crude neuroscience** — concede the edges in the same paragraph.
7. **Salthouse taken as settled** — the Schaie rebuttal appears beside it.
